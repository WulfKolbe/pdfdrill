WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE COUNT
------------------------------------
This project's argument is that a benchmark which cannot see its own errors
is worth little, and that measurements should be checkable by someone other
than the person making them. I have been reporting "519 tests passing" as
the headline of every unit while half of them were unrunnable on his
machine. The number was not wrong; it was not checkable, which is the same
failure I have been documenting in OmniDocBench, in MathPix's confidence
column, and in my own crop metric.
