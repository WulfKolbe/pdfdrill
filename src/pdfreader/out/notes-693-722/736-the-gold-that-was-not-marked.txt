THE FIX
-------
A centred maths block IS a display equation -- centred, on its own line,
with its number beside it. `gold_equations` now takes a `center` whose
content is mathematics, and ONLY that: the block must carry `$...$`, and
what remains outside the maths must be an equation number or nothing, so a
centred figure, caption or heading is not swept in.

    gold 421 -> 422

    pdf2mmd  \mathcal{M} = \frac{SO(4, n)}{SO(4) \times SO(n)} \times SO(1, 1)

AND THE PART THAT CANNOT BE FIXED — WORTH KNOWING
-------------------------------------------------
Equations (1) and (2) are still not gold, and should not be. The
transcription lost the letters:

    PDF       (e^a_µ , ψ_µ^A , A^α_µ , B_µν , χ^A , σ)
    gold      ({\it e}µ{\it a}, ?µ{\it A, A}µa, {\it B}µ?, ?{\it A}, s)
    pdf2mmd   (e_{\mu}^{a}, \psi_{\mu}^{A}, A_{\mu}^{\alpha}, B_{\mu\nu},
               \chi^{A}, \sigma)

`?` stands where ψ and χ are, `s` where σ is, and the α is gone. HERE THE
READER IS RIGHT AND THE GOLD IS WRONG. Nothing can be measured against it,
and making it a gold equation would score a correct reading as a failure.

I checked whether that degradation reaches the gold that IS used: of the 422
display equations, ZERO contain a `?`. The damaged material in this document
sits in the two equations the gold never marked as equations, so it has
never entered the comparison. The 422 are clean.
