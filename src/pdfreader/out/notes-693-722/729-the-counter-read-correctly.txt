MEASURED DIRECTLY
-----------------
For each of the 421 gold display equations, is there a single emitted block
whose normalised content equals it? Normalisation strips whitespace, braces,
`\left`/`\right`, the `\big` family and the manual spacing commands -- i.e.
generous.

    gold display equations                                421
    delivered as ONE block with matching content           21   (5.0%)

Five per cent. Not 9%, and 9% was never the right number anyway: it counts
documents whose TALLY matches, which a document can do while every equation
in it is wrong.

So his reading is right in substance. 95% of the authored equations are not
delivered whole. "817 vs 421" is not "a bit over-split"; it is a different
object from a list of equations.
