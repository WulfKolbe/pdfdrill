746 — THE STREAM INDEX WAS NOT THE STREAM

You asked what happens around the `\frac{1}{8}` bar and the longer line after
it. The answer is bigger than that fraction: THE READER'S NOTION OF STREAM
ORDER WAS WRONG, and wrong in exactly the place you were pointing at.


WHAT THE PDF ACTUALLY DOES THERE
--------------------------------
    [(+)]TJ 12.956 8.088 Td [(1)]TJ          <- numerator, RELATIVE Td
    ET
    q  1 0 0 1 193.406 525.446 cm
       []0 d 0 J 0.478 w 0 0 m 5.853 0 l S   <- the bar: a STROKED LINE
    Q
    BT
    /F15 11.9552 Tf 193.406 514.256 Td [(8)]TJ    <- denominator, ABSOLUTE
    /F20 11.9552 Tf 7.049 8.201 Td [(me)]TJ ...

The bar is not a rectangle and not a glyph: `0 0 m 5.853 0 l S`, a path
stroked at width 0.478 under a CTM that carries it to (193.406, 525.446).
Paths cannot be drawn inside a text object, so TeX ENDS the text object,
saves the graphics state, draws, restores, and BEGINS A NEW ONE. The text
matrix resets at `BT`, which is why the `8` is placed absolutely at
(193.406, 514.256) while everything around it moves by small relative `Td`
steps -- and why the next `Td` after it, `7.049 8.201`, is measured from the
DENOMINATOR rather than from the `+`.

So yes: a save/restore, and a text object torn in half around it. That is
what you suspected.

(There is no `<00>` in this corpus -- checked all 102, none uses hex strings
at all. `<00>` is the CID form, `[<00><03>...]TJ` with Identity-H encoding;
these files are Type 1 with literal strings and kerns, `[(cosh)-222(\036)]TJ`.
The negative numbers inside the brackets are kerns in 1/1000 em, not
positions.)


AND HERE IS WHAT IT COST
------------------------
`_stream_index` recorded glyph order by walking `extract_pages(laparams=None)`,
on the reasoning that switching layout analysis off leaves the producer's
order alone. Measured against `render_char`, the interpreter's own callback:

    render_char      +  1  8  m  e  -  6  sigma  [  -  3  2      <- the stream
    extract_pages    +  [  m  e  -  6  sigma  1  8  -  3  2      <- what we had

The numerator and denominator are moved SEVEN PLACES LATER and the big
bracket seven places earlier. THE REORDERING HAPPENS AT THE BT/ET BOUNDARY,
which is precisely what wraps a fraction rule or a big delimiter -- so the
index was wrong exactly where the layout is hard, and right everywhere it
did not matter.

Every pass that trusts the stream inherited it: the row sort of 741, the
script-band merge, the sandwich of 744.

Now recorded at `render_char`. The index is the stream.
