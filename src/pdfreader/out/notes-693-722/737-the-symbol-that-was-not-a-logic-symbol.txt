737 — wzlxjtu-026: ⊮ WAS THE CLUE, NOT THE SYMBOL

The report was "I saw some of these ⊮ (U+22AE, does not force) and they
vanished in pdf2mmd". Both halves are right, and neither is quite what it
looks like. There is no ⊮ in this document. What is there is 𝟙, the
double-struck identity matrix, eight times.


WHAT EACH READER DOES WITH IT
-----------------------------
The page loads `dsrom12` — the font of the `dsfont` package, every glyph of
which is double-struck — and uses its `one` eight times.

    gold       \mathds{1}      ×8      correct
    MathPix    \mathbb{1}      ×8      TYPESETS ⊮
    pdf2mmd    1               ×7      the symbol vanished
               (dropped)       ×1      the eighth, silently

`\mathbb{1}` is not a spelling variant. amssymb's blackboard alphabet is
msbm's, which carries A–Z AND NO DIGITS, and msbm's `1` slot holds
`\nVdash`. Compiled and read back out of the PDF:

    \documentclass{article}\usepackage{amssymb}\usepackage{dsfont}
    amssymb: $\mathbb{1}$ and $\mathbb{H}$   ->   ⊮ and H
    dsfont:  $\mathds{1}$                    ->   1

SO THE ⊮ IS MATHPIX'S AND IT IS A WRONG SYMBOL, not a wrong font. An
identity matrix is being printed as a logic relation. Nine `\mathbb{<digit>}`
over this corpus, every one of them that.

And they "vanished in pdf2mmd" because pdf2mmd wrote a bare `1`: the reading
was not wrong, it had simply stopped being a symbol at all. Which is the
worse of the two failures, because nothing marks it.


WHY
---
`dsrom12` was in no family rule, so it fell through to `text`, and a text
font's `one` is the character `1`. Two consequences, and the second is the
one that actually loses material:

  * projected as an ordinary digit — seven times;
  * NOT IN `MATH_FAMILIES`, so where the glyph sat in an inline formula
    rather than a display it landed in a TEXT span, which carries no LaTeX,
    and was dropped with no crop and no deferral — the eighth.

This is the same shape as the txfonts and XCharter misclassifications in
733: a maths font nobody had named, quietly read as prose.


FIXED
-----
`dsrom\d*` is a family, `doublestroke`, in MATH_FAMILIES beside `fraktur`
and `script`; its glyphs project as `\mathds{<char>}`; and `dsfont` is
derived into the preamble by the usual rule — THE FONT SAYS WHICH PACKAGE.
That last part is why pdf2mmd can be right here and MathPix cannot: the
glyph came from a named font, and the font names its package.

    wzlxjtu-026   \mathds 0 -> 8    gold 8    exact
    wzlxjtu-005   \mathds 0 -> 1    gold 1    exact
                  and its `= -$ 1.` becomes `= -\mathds{1}$`
    corpus        \mathds 0 -> 9    MathPix \mathbb{<digit>} 9


MEASURED
--------
    correct        138 of 422 (32.7%)   UNCHANGED
    corpus .tex    102 of 102 compile clean
    outside docs    18 of 18 compile clean
    tests          488 passing (was 484)

The correct count does not move, and that is a fact about the METRIC, not
about the fix: `canon.py` strips font commands on purpose, so `\mathds{1}`,
`\mathbb{1}` and a bare `1` all canonicalise to `1`. A LOST FONT IS INVISIBLE
TO IT. Every one of these nine symbols is now right in the output and was
wrong before; no aggregate here can show that, so it is written down instead.
