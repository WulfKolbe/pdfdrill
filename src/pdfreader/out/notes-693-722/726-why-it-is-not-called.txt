726 — why the merge is not called, checked rather than asserted

THE QUESTION
------------
"why is your improvement not called?"

Because switching it on costs 719 display blocks, 755 crops and a quarter of
the projection rate. But "the attachment pass is the blocker" was my
CONCLUSION from one measurement, not something I had traced. So I traced it.

FIRST DIAGNOSIS, AND IT WAS WRONG
---------------------------------
Called `_attach_scripts` directly on each merged row: 12 of 23 refused, and
the refusing rows carried 8 to 13 distinct baselines with dozens of
FULL-SIZE glyphs off the top one -- a numerator `1` at 663.9, a denominator
`2` at 647.6.

That looked conclusive and it was not a fair test. The pipeline splits
fractions BEFORE attaching scripts, so calling the second pass alone hands
it material the first pass was supposed to remove.

SECOND DIAGNOSIS, THROUGH THE REAL ORDER
----------------------------------------
    row 2   n=62   rules=0   -> fractions 0, rest 62   attach: None
    row 3   n=59   rules=2   -> fractions 2, rest 55   attach: 32 triples

Row 2 plainly contains a fraction and was given ZERO rules. Found why: a
rule is attached to a line only within `0.8 * span_pt` of its BASELINE --
a window sized for a single text line. A merged display line is two or three
times that, and its fraction bar sits between the numerator and the
denominator, outside the window. The bar is dropped, `_split_fractions` has
nothing to split, and the full-size numerator then trips the off-baseline
guard.

A complete, mechanical, satisfying explanation.

TESTED, AND IT IS NOT THE CAUSE
-------------------------------
Widened the window to the line's own extent (`max(0.8*span_pt, gy1-base,
base-gy0)`) and turned the merge on:

    projection                62.4%  ->  62.4%
    script-attachment failures  666  ->  666

Not one span changed. With the merge OFF the same change cost 2 display
blocks. Reverted, with the measurement recorded beside the line.

WHERE THAT LEAVES IT
--------------------
The answer to the question is unchanged -- `_attach_scripts` is the blocker
-- but it is now traced rather than inferred, and one plausible alternative
has been ruled out by measurement instead of by argument.

What is now known: the rows are the producer's real lines (the user
corroborated 23/17/17 independently from the PostScript, different tool,
different code path); the fraction rules ARE reaching the merged lines on at
least some rows; and widening the rule window changes nothing. The remaining
failure is inside the script pass itself, on rows with many baselines, which
is precisely what 696 said.

519 tests passing. Standing state unchanged: gold 421, MathPix 417,
pdf2mmd 817 (9% exact, 1.94x), crops 548, projection 86.4%.
