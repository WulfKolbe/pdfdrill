747 — wzlxjtu-074: BOLD MATHS, AND AN ACCENT CUT OFF FROM ITS LETTER

I chose the document by evidence rather than by counting forward: over the
corpus, wzlxjtu-074 has the WORST GAP TO MATHPIX. Gold 6 equations, MathPix
misses 1, we emitted 2 blocks and missed 5.

Two defects, one on top of the other. Both are fixed.


1. BOLD MATHS DID NOT EXIST
---------------------------
The paper writes its vectors and matrices `\bf{t}`, `\bf{F}`, `\bf{\Sigma}`.
`\bf` selects CMBX -- Computer Modern Bold Extended -- which this reader
classifies as `text-cm`, and a plain letter from a TEXT font has no maths
projection. So every bold letter came back UNKNOWN and took its whole span
with it.

The reported reason was `unmapped-glyph`, never the accent:

    x=199.05 CMBX12 name=t        kind=unknown  tex=None
    x=287.28 CMBX12 name=G        kind=unknown  tex=None
    x=308.32 CMBX12 name=Delta    kind=unknown  tex=None
    x=346.15 CMBX12 name=F        kind=unknown  tex=None

MEASURED over the corpus: 582 unmapped CMBX glyphs INSIDE MATHS SPANS, in 12
documents -- `F` 116 times, `n` 79, `t` 54, `Sigma` 43.

A bold text letter used as a maths symbol now projects as `\mathbf{F}`, and
an uppercase Greek as `\mathbf{\Sigma}`. `\mathbf` and not `\boldsymbol`: it
is what the author wrote, it needs no package, and it sets uppercase Greek
correctly -- checked by compiling `$\mathbf{\Sigma}$`, clean.

Safe without knowing the span: prose is emitted from the glyph's TEXT, never
from this token, which only a maths span reads. Lowercase Greek is refused,
because `\mathbf{\alpha}` asks the operators font for a letter it has not
got.


2. THE ACCENT WAS IN THE WRONG SPAN
-----------------------------------
With the bold letters mapped, equation 1 came out right and the other four
still did not. NOW the reason was the accent, and this is what it was:

    stream 595  circumflex  x=347.02  base=504.23   span p1l11s6
    stream 596  F           x=346.15  base=501.34   span p1l11s7

CONSECUTIVE IN THE STREAM -- TeX emits `\hat{F}` as the accent and then the
letter -- and stacked on the page, the accent centred 0.9pt over the letter.
A span boundary fell between them, so the accent pass could never see its
base: `accent-not-composed:circumflex`, five crops on that page.

And a display broken by a crop is not a display. It is emitted as inline
fragments, which is why FOUR EQUATIONS WERE MISSING rather than merely
wrong:

    $d_{n} = \sup\limits...\{d_{GY}$ [crop] $H_{n}) - d_{GY}$ [crop] $F_{n}, G)\}^{+}$

A run boundary does not get to overrule the producer. An accent at the end
of a run whose NEXT STREAM POSITION is the first glyph of the next run,
centred over it and set above its baseline, moves across.

    wzlxjtu-074   2 blocks -> 6, matching gold's 6, e.g.

      gold     d_{GY}(\bf{t}, \hat{F}_n, G_n) = 1 - G_n(\Delta(...))
      pdf2mmd  d_{GY}(\mathbf{t}, \hat{\mathbf{F}}_{\mathbf{n}},
                      \mathbf{G}_{\mathbf{n}}) = 1 - \mathbf{G}_{\mathbf{n}}(...)
