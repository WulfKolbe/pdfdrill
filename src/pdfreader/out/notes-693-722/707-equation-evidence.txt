707 — equation entries for the evidence apparatus

THE CONCEPT, from evidence-equation.pdf
---------------------------------------
One row per equation:

    Identifier | Page | Conf. | LaTeX source | Rendered | Image

`Rendered` is the LaTeX compiled; `Image` is the crop from the original PDF.
inkdrill compares the two as INK and the deviations become a residual file.
The comparison is only as good as the row it is given.

SHIPPED: equations.py
---------------------
`pdf2mmd.sh` now writes `<stem>.equations.json` beside the lines.json and the
docmodel. On the Prolog paper:

    total 329   display 15   inline 314
    complete 319   partial 1   empty 7   unbalanced 2

Each row carries the identifier, page, kind, confidence, LaTeX, the region in
the same 250-dpi pixel space as the crop URLs, and the deferral reasons.

THREE DECISIONS WORTH RECORDING
-------------------------------
1. THE IDENTIFIER IS CONTENT-ADDRESSED, with the human-readable `EQnnnn`
   alongside. Sequential ids alone would renumber on every re-read and turn
   a diff between two runs into noise.

2. CONSECUTIVE DISPLAY LINES ARE ONE EQUATION. A `cases` construct reaches
   the page as a fence line, a left-hand side and one line per branch.
   Scored separately, each fragment came out at 1.000 while the construct as
   a whole was never assembled -- four perfect scores for a broken reading.
   The reference table shows that same equation as ONE row at 0.672.

3. CONFIDENCE IS THE PROJECTION FRACTION AND NOTHING ELSE. A structural
   defect is reported SEPARATELY as `structural_ok`.

   The temptation was to fold the two together and land near the reference
   table's 0.672. Two reasons not to. The weighting behind that number is
   not known here, so matching it would be fitting to one observation. And
   collapsing two different facts into one score is precisely what makes a
   benchmark unable to see its own errors -- the point of the OmniDocBench
   finding. A consumer wanting one number can combine them; one wanting to
   know WHY can read both.

   On the Prolog paper the two `cases` constructs are exactly the rows where
   confidence is 1.000 and `structural_ok` is false. That pair of values
   says "every glyph was read, and the structure was not rebuilt", which is
   the true state and is what 695 measured.

WHAT THE APPARATUS STILL NEEDS FROM THIS SIDE
---------------------------------------------
- The `Rendered` column needs the LaTeX to COMPILE. `is_emittable` checks
  balanced braces and argument presence, but an unbalanced `\Biggl\{` passes
  it and will not compile. The `structural_ok` flag now marks those rows, so
  a renderer can skip or repair them rather than failing.
- Inline equations outnumber display ones 314 to 15, and their crops carry
  neighbouring prose. An ink comparison on those will need a tighter region
  than the span rectangle, or it will score the prose too.
