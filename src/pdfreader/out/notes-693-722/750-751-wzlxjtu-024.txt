750 — HOW FAR A BIG OPERATOR IS RAISED DEPENDS ON WHICH ONE IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------
Equation 3 is `\int dx\,\rho(x) = 1` and came out `dx \rho(x) = 1`. The
integral was not unmapped -- `integraldisplay` projects to `\int` perfectly
well. It was in the PROSE LINE ABOVE, the one reading "...should approach an
L1 function normalized as".

A big operator is drawn raised, so the row grouper lowers it before deciding
which row it is on. That correction was ONE CONSTANT, 0.7 em, for every
operator. Measured over 60 documents, as the raise above the baseline of the
nearest full-size glyph beside it:

    summationdisplay   n=18   0.95 em    (17 of 18 exactly)
    integraldisplay    n=14   1.361 em   (10 of 14 exactly)
    c P l m s d t e k i       0.000 em   (exact, 100+ samples)

An integral is raised HALF AN EM MORE than a sum. Corrected by 0.7 em this
one keyed to 437.8, and the prose line's baseline was nearer than the
equation's.

The last row is every operator built from LETTERS -- `\cos`, `\max`, `\sup`;
the operator merge marks the head letter `bigop`, and it sits on the
ordinary baseline like the text it is. The blanket 0.7 em was moving all of
those off their own row too.

I tried to avoid a table. TeX centres these on the maths axis, so the box
CENTRE should identify the row without any constant: measured, that fits the
letter operators (0.06 em, tight) and not the CMEX ones (sum 1.05, int
1.23), because pdfminer does not report the real depth of a CMEX glyph. So:
a measured table, and 0 for anything not in it.

    equation 3   `dx \rho(x) = 1`  ->  `\int dx \rho(x) = 1`


751 — A VINCULUM BELONGED TO WHICHEVER RADICAL STARTED AT ITS x
----------------------------------------------------------------
Equations 5 and 6 are

    F \approx -\frac{9\sqrt{2}\pi N^{5/2}}{5\sqrt{8-N_f}}
    G_6 = \frac{5\pi\sqrt{8-N_f}}{27\sqrt{2}} N^{-5/2}

and both were crops, `fraction+overline+unmapped-glyph`. Their lines had
been handed each other's rules:

    p1l21  y 244..285   rules at 275.19, 259.96, 257.57, and 158.13   <<<
    p1l26  y 146..188   rules at 275.19, 257.57, 176.31, 160.53, 158.13

A rule ninety points below a line, on that line. The vinculum rule attaches
a bar to a line when the bar starts at a radical sign's right edge, AT ANY
HEIGHT -- "nothing else in a row does that". True while one radical on the
page starts there. Both equations set `\sqrt{2}`, at x=302.79 and x=301.64,
and each bar abutted both signs.

TeX draws the vinculum at the TOP of the sign, inside its box. That is the
test `_rule_role` already applies to the same decision; it applies here now
too. Each line keeps exactly its own three rules.
