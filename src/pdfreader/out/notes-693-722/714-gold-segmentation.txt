714 — the gold comparison: 7% vs 94%, and what it is actually made of

THE USER'S MEASUREMENT
----------------------
    agrees with gold on the equation count
        MathPix    96 of 102  (94%)
        pdf2mmd     7 of 102  ( 7%)
    totals: gold 421, MathPix 417, pdf2mmd 1646

That is the result. MathPix reproduces the author's environment count almost
exactly and can be paired row-for-row with gold; pdf2mmd cannot.

FIRST FINDING: pdf2mmd DISAGREES WITH ITSELF
--------------------------------------------
The markdown and `equations.json` give DIFFERENT equation counts for the
same document, in both directions:

    doc 1   md-blocks  9    equations.json display 21
    doc 4   md-blocks 16    equations.json display  9
    doc 5   md-blocks 18    equations.json display  8

`equations.json` has fused consecutive display runs since 707; `to_markdown`
opened a new `$$` per line group and had no fusion at all. One tool, one
document, two answers to "how many equations". That alone disqualifies
either number as a metric until they agree.

WHAT WAS FIXED
--------------
`to_markdown` now fuses a display block with the one immediately above when
the two are vertically adjacent (within 2.0 em, chosen by sweep: 1.2 gave
2.4x, 2.0 gave 2.12x, 3.0 gave 2.09x on 35 files -- flat beyond 2.0).

    102 documents   gold 421   pdf2mmd 1646 -> 1414   3.91x -> 3.36x
    exact agreement 6 (6%), within one 16 (16%)

Still 3.4x. The fusion is not the main term.

SECOND FINDING: IT IS NOT MOSTLY TABLES, AND IT IS NOT MOSTLY SPLITTING
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Document 95 -- gold 4, ours 51 -- is a TABLE. Rows like

    q t AB C D theta_p q A B
    -1 -2 -1 +-1 +-1 -1 2 -2

are indented, carry no prose, and satisfy `is_display`. But measured across
35 files, only 8% of display-flagged lines have three or more wide column
gaps. Tables are a real defect and doc 95 is dominated by it, but they are
not the general cause.

So 679 display-flagged lines across 35 files against a gold count near 145
is mostly genuine multi-line splitting that adjacency fusion does not catch
-- the lines are not adjacent enough, or are separated by material that
breaks the run.

A MISTAKE WORTH RECORDING
-------------------------
The threshold sweep was done with `sed -i "s/<= [0-9.]* \\* size$/.../"`,
which also rewrote an unrelated line 528 lines away: the raised-marker test
`g.size <= 0.85 * size` became `<= 2.0 * size`. One test caught it
immediately (`test_a_same_size_glyph_is_not_a_marker`). A blanket regex edit
across a 1000-line file to tune one constant is not a sweep, it is a
liability; the test suite was the only thing standing between that and a
silently changed heuristic.

STATUS
------
482 tests passing. Ratio 3.36x against gold, agreement 6%. The number is
better than 3.91x and nowhere near MathPix's 0.99x. The remaining term is
not identified, and guessing at it is what produced the last two partial
fixes.
