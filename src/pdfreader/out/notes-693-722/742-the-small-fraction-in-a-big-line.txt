742 — FILES 91 AND 92: HOW A VERY LARGE LINE IS HANDLED

You asked how the reader handles the very long rows in 91/92, the ones that
open `\frac12 \frac{large numerator}{large denominator}`. The answer was:
badly, and for one measurable reason.


WHAT THESE ROWS ARE
-------------------
wzlxjtu-091's first equation is ONE `gather*` of eight rows. Its bands come
out 30 to 53 points tall and carry UP TO EIGHT FRACTION BARS EACH, because
every `(pq)^{\frac12}` in the row draws one. On that page:

    p1l3    h=45.4   8 rules
    p1l8    h=35.2   6 rules
    p1l20   h=30.4   8 rules
    p1l26   h=53.1   8 rules

and almost every one of those spans refused.


THE REASON, AND IT IS ONE LINE OF EVIDENCE
------------------------------------------
Every one of those bars is 3.7pt wide and sits at the same y. Instrumented,
p1l3's eight bars report:

    x=159.8  above_b=[616.0]  below_b=[609.6]   above=True    fraction
    x=225.0  above_b=[616.0]  below_b=[609.6]   above=False   overline
    x=297.8  above_b=[616.0]  below_b=[609.6]   above=False   overline
    x=309.9  above_b=[616.0]  below_b=[609.6]   above=False   overline
    x=362.7  above_b=[616.0]  below_b=[609.6]   above=False   overline
    x=435.4  above_b=[616.0]  below_b=[609.6]   above=False   overline
    x=447.5  above_b=[616.0]  below_b=[609.6]   above=False   overline
    x=510.1  above_b=[616.0]  below_b=[609.6]   above=False   overline

IDENTICAL EVIDENCE, DIFFERENT VERDICTS. Seven `\frac12` became
`\overline{}`, and a span holding an unaccounted overline refuses: that page
reported `fraction` 8 and `fraction+overline` 6.

It is not inconsistency, it is NON-LOCALITY. `_is_own_row` decides whether
what sits above a bar is a numerator or a text line running across it by
counting the glyphs on that baseline that fall OUTSIDE the bar, within six
ems. That test is right for a bar alone on a line. In a row packed with
other superscripts there is always something out there, so the verdict turns
on which neighbours happen to be in range -- and the leftmost bar wins
because its window is emptier.


THE FIX: JUDGE A SMALL FRACTION LOCALLY
---------------------------------------
What a text line running over a bar CANNOT do is be set at SCRIPT SIZE. TeX
sets a fraction inside an exponent smaller than the type around it, and it
sets the rule to the width of its parts. So material above AND below, both
centred within the bar, both smaller than the surrounding type, is a
fraction -- and no neighbour can change that answer.

Added beside `_is_own_row`, not instead of it: the old test still decides
every full-size bar, so an `\overline{x}` in running text -- where the thing
below is full size -- never reaches the new one.

    wzlxjtu-091 rule roles   fraction 8 / overline 44   ->   46 / 6

and the first equation now opens the way gold does:

    gold      \frac12 \frac{\Gamma_e(t^{-2})\Gamma_e\big(pq^3t^2A^{-2}B^2\big)
                            \Gamma_e\big(q^{-1}t^{-2}B^{-2}\big)...}
    pdf2mmd   \frac{1}{2} \frac{\Gamma_{e}(t^{-2})\Gamma_{e}\bigl(pq^{3}t^{2}
                            A^{-2}B^{2}\bigr)\Gamma_{e}\bigl(q^{-1}t^{-2}
                            B^{-2}\bigr)...}

WHAT IS STILL WRONG WITH THESE TWO DOCUMENTS
--------------------------------------------
I am not going to call 91 and 92 fixed. Two things remain, both named:

1. THE EIGHT ROWS ARE STILL FOUR BLOCKS. Gold's first equation is one
   `gather*` of eight rows; the reader emits four `$$`. The rows are
   30-53pt tall and interleave vertically -- row N's descenders reach into
   row N+1's ascenders -- so band-building cannot separate them by baseline
   alone. This is the 727 problem at its worst: several baselines per row,
   and the rows themselves overlapping.

2. THE 75 EMPTY DELIMITER PAIRS are unchanged. wzlxjtu-092's denominator
   still reads `\bigl( \bigr)\bigl( \bigr)...`: its numerator and the
   DENOMINATOR'S big delimiters share one band, because both are tall, while
   the denominator's ordinary-size contents are a band below. The furniture
   rejoin cannot move the delimiters, because that band is not pure
   furniture -- it holds the numerator too.

Both are real and both are measured. Neither is a threshold.
