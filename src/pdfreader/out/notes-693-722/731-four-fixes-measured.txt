WHAT WAS WRONG, IN ORDER OF WHAT IT COST
----------------------------------------
1. FRACTION BARS WERE BEING CALLED OVERLINES.
   `_rule_role` requires a fraction's numerator to be its OWN row: no glyph
   on that baseline outside the bar's x-range. But `\frac{a}{b}+\frac{c}{d}`
   is an ordinary display and its two numerators SHARE a baseline, so for
   bar 1 the glyph `c` looks exactly like a text line running across it.
   Both bars were demoted and neither fraction was ever built.

       author's bars in the gold set   1157
       classified "fraction"            489  ->  902
       classified "overline"            685  ->  307

   The fix is the other bar: a glyph covered by a DIFFERENT rule at the same
   offset is that fraction's part, not this line. Exact geometry, no threshold.

2. STACKED LIMITS NEVER REACHED `_attach_scripts`.
   structure.py states "no stacked limits above/below big operators" as a
   limit it does not model. But `_attach_scripts` already treats a bigop as a
   main glyph and hangs scripts on it -- the limits simply never arrive.
   Baseline grouping puts the row above and below a `\prod` in rows of their
   own, exactly as it does a fraction's numerator, and a fraction is rescued
   only because its bar is evidence that the rows are one line. An operator
   has no bar. Its evidence is its own geometry: a row wholly inside the
   operator's column, in smaller type, within a window scaled to the operator.

3. A DISPLAY'S MAIN ROW WAS ARRIVING IN PIECES.
   wzlxjtu-006, `L=\prod_{i=0}^3 e^{\phi^i K^i}`:

       g4  base=627.61  x=[286.8,328.0]   the raised operator
       g5  base=620.26  x=[263.1,283.4]   'L='
       g6  base=620.26  x=[304.0,540.0]   'e(1)'

   g5 and g6 share a baseline EXACTLY. They are one line, split because the
   `\prod` stands between them on its raised baseline, leaving a 20.6pt hole
   that reads like a column gutter. Nothing downstream can recover from that.
   Rejoined only when something STANDS IN THE HOLE at a nearby baseline --
   two columns of prose also flank a gap; what they lack is a raised glyph in
   it. Then the operator rejoins the row whose x-range it stands inside.

       069:  four blocks  `L =` / `\prod_{\phi^i K^i}` / `i=0` / `e`
             with the upper limit `3` outside the display altogether
       730:  $$ L = \prod_{i=0}^{3} e^{\phi^{i} K^{i}} $$      = gold, exactly

4. THE RUN RULE WAS A VERTICAL GAP.
   `run_of` fused two display groups when `-1.5*size <= gap <= 2.0*size`,
   scanning display groups alone and ignoring everything between them. Wrong
   in both directions: a fraction or a tall delimiter pushes two rows of ONE
   equation past 2.0*size, and nothing stopped two separate displays with
   prose between them from fusing.

   Replaced with the look-ahead -- your own instrument, applied in the
   projector's coordinates where the x of every line is already known. A run
   ends at a return to the BODY MARGIN. An indented fragment (`$\bigl($`,
   the 91.pdf case) does not end it; a crop does not; prose does. One guard
   remains beside it, for two displays with nothing at all between them.
