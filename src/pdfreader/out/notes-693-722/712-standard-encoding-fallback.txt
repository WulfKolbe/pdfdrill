712 — the StandardEncoding fallback: my fork's bug, confirmed and fixed

WHOSE BUG
---------
Mine. Upstream pdfminer exposes no glyph name at all, so a font with no
/Encoding produced nothing and the span deferred -- the correct outcome. The
fork adds `LTChar.glyphname` from `cid2name`, and when /Encoding is absent
pdfminer has already fallen back to StandardEncoding, so the fork reports
that fallback AS IF IT WERE THE FONT'S OWN NAME.

An abstention became a confident wrong answer, which is the one failure this
project exists to avoid.

VERIFIED BY RENDERING
---------------------
1.pdf, TeX-matha10, each glyph's EXACT box rasterised at 500dpi and the five
placed side by side so no neighbour could be misread:

    cid  reported          really
     16  quotedblleft      =
     18  quotedblbase      ~
     24  perthousand       !=
     80  P                 in
     82  R                 notin
    112  p                 (
    113  q                 )

`\pi^{*}(\omega_{0})` was being read as `\pi^{*}p\omega_{0}q`.

THE FIX, IN THREE PARTS
-----------------------
1. ABSTAIN where the name is certainly the fallback. A Latin letter or a
   text-punctuation name in a font that HAS NO LATIN ALPHABET -- TeX-matha,
   TeX-mathx, CMEX, LMMathExtension, MSAM, MSBM -- cannot be the font's own
   name. CMMI and CMSY are excluded: CMMI carries the maths italic alphabet
   at its ASCII slots, so `p` there really is `p`, and distrusting it would
   break every variable.

2. A VERIFIED SLOT TABLE keyed on BaseFont prefix and cid. Seven entries,
   every one read off a rendered glyph. Nothing goes in on the strength of a
   table or a guess -- a guess is what caused this.

3. The TeX-math* fonts were classified as TEXT, so they emitted their
   fallback characters literally. Now math-symbol, math-extension and
   math-italic by name.

THE NUMBERS GOT WORSE, AND THAT IS THE POINT
---------------------------------------------
    dataset crops        442 -> 542
    zero-crop files       25 -> 16

A hundred glyphs that were read with a wrong name now refuse. The seven
verified slots recover some; the rest -- TeX-mathx cid 112 among them, 27
occurrences -- stay refused until someone renders them.

Any metric counting crop links will call this a regression. It is the
opposite: 442 included an unknown number of confidently wrong readings that
no crop-count could see, which is exactly the OmniDocBench failure mode from
the inside.

NEXT, AND IT IS CHEAP
---------------------
The remaining abstentions are concentrated: TeX-mathx cid 112 (27), and
`ring`/`acute`/`dieresis` from TeX-matha7 and matha5 at cid 6, 1 and 4 (55
together). Each is one render away from a verified slot. The method is
established; only the looking is left.
