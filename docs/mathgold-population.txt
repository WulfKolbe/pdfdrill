  row 1  floor on the single-expression subset (n=1908 of 2994 parsed, 64%)

  row 2  floor over all gold equations, environments held out as Unresolved
         -- the honest denominator; 36% unresolved IS the state on this corpus

Environments are NOT split into rows to enlarge the denominator: an align
block's rows are related by its alignment points, and splitting discards real
structure to improve a number.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Commit 79331bf records 21,240 and commit 13368a6 records 59. Re-measured
independently across the library; both hold, and the difference is STRUCTURE
versus PLACEMENT.

  A  provenance == "tex"              87 equations, 59 with region+page, 2 docs
  B  source-built latex_original/env  21,230 equations, 0 with region+page, 389 docs

A is gold structure AND placement: an `injectlatex` overlay attaches the
author's LaTeX to an equation that already has a page and a rectangle.
B is gold structure ONLY: a source-built model is built from .tex and carries
no page geometry until `geometry`/`merge` attaches it.

Which denominator applies depends on what is being scored:

  T4 (1) latex -> SLT          use B (21,230). Parsing needs no geometry;
  T4 (2) SLT <-> .lg round-trip     this is the corpus the 99.8% parse rate
                                    and the lossless round-trip were measured on.
  T4 (3) the floor             use A-with-geometry (59, over kolbe2018hubbard
                                    and 2510.11170v2). The floor scores pdfminer
                                    CHARACTERS AND POSITIONS, so an equation
                                    with no rectangle cannot be scored at all.

So the floor is anecdotal at n=59 and must be reported as such. Enlarging it
is not a mathgold task: it means attaching geometry to source-built models,
which moves B into A.
