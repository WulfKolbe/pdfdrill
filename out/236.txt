236 — the handover list keys on folder path
==============================================================================

THE CHANGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
`pdfdrill handover <dir> [--ready-only] [--json]`. One row per DOCUMENT, keyed
on **folder path**, which is unique by construction. `title` is carried as a
display field and is never a key.

At runtime, before the list is emitted:

    paths = {r["path"] for r in rows}
    if len(rows) != len(paths):
        raise HandoverCollision("%d rows over %d distinct paths — the list
                                 collapsed")

and the command turns that into "REFUSING to hand over: …" rather than printing
a shorter list. The assertion is in the code, not only in a test: a list that
collapsed is not handed over at all.

The eleven, listed: **11 rows, 11 distinct paths, 11 distinct titles.**

WHAT THE KEY CHANGE IS WORTH — MEASURED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   1,062 corpus documents carry a model with a title.
   **159 of them share a normalised title with at least one other**,
   collapsing onto **72 keys**.
   A title-keyed list therefore loses **87 rows**, and says nothing about
   having lost them.

The largest collisions are the same paper under different filenames — five
copies of "Lightlike infinity in C G A models of spacetime" (0403019, and three
numbered duplicates, and a fourth); four of "The magnitude of categories of
texts enriched by language models"; three of "Clifford Geometrodynamics"
(0212041, Clifford_Geometrodynamics, gr-qc_0212041). Those are distinct folders
with distinct reports and distinct residuals. Merging them under one heading
does not deduplicate anything; it hides ten measurements behind one.

Worst single key: **"Theorem"** — four documents (2302.06594, 2302.06594 (1),
2306.0024v1, math_0202059) whose extracted title is the word Theorem. A
title-keyed list files four papers under a heading that is not a title at all.

A NUMBER I HAD TO WITHDRAW BEFORE REPORTING IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The first pass found 25 prefix pairs and put one of the eleven in the list:

   "Geometric Algebra" is a prefix of
   "Geometric, Algebraic and Topological Methods for Quantum Field Theory"

It is not. Normalising punctuation to spaces turns the second into "geometric
algebraic and topological …", and "geometric algebra" is a prefix of that only
because it cuts ALGEBRAIC in half. Requiring the prefix to end at a word
boundary drops five such pairs — the Geometric Algebra/Algebraic one, and four
of "Theorem" against "TheoremLlama" — and takes the count from 25 to 20. It
also removes the only hit among the eleven, so the reported answer changed on
the correction.

THE LIST FOUND TWELVE THINGS THAT ARE NOT DOCUMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The first run over the eleven produced **23 rows and 12 distinct titles** —
which looks exactly like the collapse this task is about, and was not. The
extra twelve were:

   refine.report.pdf                      (a derived report)
   scatter_xy, scatter_xz, scatter_zy, scatter_xzy, minimal-uft,
   common-effect, orientation-collider, orientation-non-collider,
   orientation-shielded, orientation-unshielded, triangle-cancelling

— e-print FIGURES from a `latex/` subdirectory. `iter_documents` walks the root
and its subdirectories, so pointing it at one document's folder enumerates that
document's figures as documents. All twelve had no title, which is why the
title count read 12: they collapsed onto the empty string.

Two fixes, both narrow:

**refine.report.pdf is now a derived PDF.** out/217 named it that way ON
PURPOSE, so a `report.*` sweep could not catch it beside the quarantined
report.ink.json.MISPAIRED. The same rename walked it straight past
`_is_derived_pdf`, which knew only the literal "report.pdf". A name chosen to
escape one glob escaped the check that was supposed to see it. This affects
every caller of iter_documents, not just this list.

**A PDF with no model is not a document to hand over.** That makes the list's
population the same one `publishready` judges, and it is what the figure PDFs
fail.

After both: 11 rows, 11 paths, 11 titles.

WHY THIS FAILURE READS AS A FINDING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A collapsed row is not empty and does not error. It carries one document's
measured rows under another document's heading — "6 rows measured" beneath a
name that promises 1,149 equations. A reader sees a document that measured
badly, not a list that lost a row. That is the same shape as out/213's six
reports exiting 0 with glyphs discarded, and out/234's 55-of-55 class C: the
artefact is present, well-formed, and describes something other than what its
label says.

Keying on path removes the possibility rather than detecting it. The assertion
is there for the case where something else reintroduces it.
