WHAT THE TWO CLASSES ARE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   TOP LEVEL (2,113) is one defect, and the EQ/FO split is its signature:
   2 in 5 display equations carry it, 1 in 650 inline formulas do. That is
   exactly what a swept-up sentence mark looks like - an inline formula sits
   mid-clause, a display equation sits at the end of one. Full stops lead
   commas 1429 to 690.

   INSIDE A BRACE GROUP (50) is mostly the P7 TEXT-TAIL case wearing a
   different hat: '\\text { überwunden werden muß. }' - German prose carried
   inside the math region, its full stop at brace depth 1. A few are real
   notation instead ('\\mathrm{R}_{6}^{*,}' - the comma belongs to the
   superscript). Both are false positives for 'trailing punctuation', so the
   number to act on is 2,113, not 2,163.

COUNTED / NOT COUNTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   counted     last non-space character is , ; . or : , allowing closing
               braces after it ('...\\right),'  '...\\end{array}.')
   NOT counted '\\right.' / '\\left.' - a trailing dot there is LaTeX syntax
               for an invisible delimiter. Without that exclusion every
               one-sided delimiter in the corpus would be a false hit.
