092 - the ink compare after 090: how many of the 725 resolve
==============================================================================

ANSWER: NONE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   0 of the 725 flagged rows resolve. One improves and stays flagged.

   The premise behind the question was that dropped glyphs had been inflating
   the ink distances. Measured, they were not - because almost none of the
   flagged rows contained a character that could be dropped.

WHY THE FIX BARELY TOUCHES THIS SET
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Of the 725 rows, 11 contain any non-ASCII character at all:

     10 x U+00E9 'e-acute'  - Latin Modern sets it correctly, always did
      1 x U+21D2 '=>'       - the only genuinely affected row

   So the maximum this fix could ever have resolved is 1 row, and it did not
   resolve that one either.

   The 725 were flagged for the reasons out/067 and out/069 established:
   delimiter auto-sizing, \frac against \dfrac, line and column spacing, and a
   resolution difference that opens letter counters in the render. None of
   those is a glyph problem, and no font change addresses them.

THE ONE AFFECTED ROW, MEASURED BOTH WAYS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   0911.3722_EQ0019, standalone render vs its scan crop:

     before   12 characters dropped   ink 24   comp -17  hole -7
     after     0 characters dropped   ink 21   comp -14  hole -7

   Three arrows returned, three components recovered, and the row is still 21
   against a noise floor of 6. It does not resolve because its disagreement
   was never mainly about the arrows: the LaTeX is missing the leading
   "Pack" and the trailing "-complete." as well, and those are real content -
   see out/093, which finds where they were lost.

   comp -14 means the scan still carries fourteen components the render has
   nothing for. That is the truncation, not the typography.

WHAT WAS STILL WORTH FIXING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The standalone renderer now carries the same glyph rescue as the report.
   Before this, a standalone render silently omitted every character the maths
   fonts lacked, and the ink metric compared an INCOMPLETE render against a
   complete scan - charging the OCR for our renderer's loss. out/070 did
   exactly that, recording three dropped arrows as a MathPix defect when the
   arrows were in the LaTeX all along.

   That error is now impossible for the 89 mapped code points, and 091 makes
   any new one announce itself. The corpus-wide effect is one row of 725,
   which is the honest measure of how much it mattered here - and no measure
   at all of how much it mattered to out/070's conclusion, which it reversed.
