METHOD
------
A document "has the author's LaTeX" when the library holds it on disk: a
`texsrc/` directory (in the folder, its .pdf.drill sidecar or blob dir) or a
`<stem>.tgz` beside the PDF whose contents are really source, not a PDF saved
under that name (689). `%` comments stripped before counting. Version and copy
twins (v1/v2, "_OCR", " (1)", "arXiv_") merged into one paper; the richest twin's
counts stand for it.
