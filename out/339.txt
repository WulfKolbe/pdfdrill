====================================================================

HOW, and it is not order

  The author's .tex gives no page. But the base images are EMBEDDED in the
  author's own PDF, so `pdfimages -list` states the page and pixel size of
  each -- no compile, no ordinal, no inference. All 84 annotated bases
  have their exact pixel size embedded; 41 at exactly one page.

  The crop side states page and rectangle in its filename (331). Both
  numberings are the PDF's: embedded images span pages 5..106, crops
  5..128, overlapping on 45 pages. 18 pages carry a crop and no embedded
  image -- the author's tikzcd diagrams, drawn in TeX and cropped by
  MathPix as pictures. 4 carry an embedded image MathPix did not crop.

  one candidate crop, but figures/ch2/bestproof1.jpg and bestproof2.jpg
  BOTH pointed at 053_1359_819_889_380 -- two subfigures of one float,
  which MathPix cropped once. Two claims on one answer is not a pair, and
  it is the same case 301 dropped as contested. Keeping either would let
  a later measurement be scored right about a pairing nobody knows.

  Ambiguity is REPORTED, not resolved. Every ambiguous row below keeps
  all of its candidates; taking the nearest or the first is 282's defect
  and the reason 301 scored the ordinal join at 0 of 8.
