265 — Section quality re-measured. The consumer is right, and 259's report
      was wrong.
==============================================================================

THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTION AS ASKED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
259's font_size ranking changed nothing measurable. Before it and after it:

    documents with a multi-level section tree     163
    documents still flat                          336

Zero documents gained a level. The reason is not that ranking does not work —
it is that the branch almost never runs, and finding out why exposed something
much larger.

259's REPORT WAS WRONG. THE CORRECTION.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
out/259 says "34,128 headers (79%) take their level from font_size, in 975
documents". The real number is 2.

I measured the font_size branch by replaying it over all 43,160
`section_header` lines and never checked that `HeaderProcessor` reaches them.
It does not:

    if not kids:
        continue

A header with no `children_ids` was dropped before any level was assigned, and
that is 34,126 of the 43,160 lines. So the population I reported as "taking
their level from font_size" was in fact the population being discarded
entirely. The two numbers coincided (34,128 vs 34,126) which made the wrong one
look confirmed.

That is the second time in this run the same mistake has produced a plausible
number: measuring what a function WOULD do to the corpus instead of what the
pipeline actually does to it. 262 caught the other one by building a real
model, and so did this.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY BROKEN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    section_header lines                          43,160
      no children -> dropped outright             34,126   (79%)
      with a child -> became a Section             9,034

    documents with any section_header               1,128
      producing at least one Section                  499
      producing NONE at all                           629
      losing at least one heading                     974

The dropped lines are not marginal. They carry their caption in the line's own
fields:

    text_display '\section*{REFERENCES}'                     text 'REFERENCES'
    text_display '\section*{1. Introduction}'                text '1. Introduction'
    text_display '\section*{4. Spin-foam models and loop quantum gravity}'

That is what a consumer means by calling Section "noisy and ignored". The set
it was handed is missing four fifths of the document's headings, in a pattern
that has nothing to do with the document — so what survives looks arbitrary.
The consumer was not working around a fixed defect. It was working around a
live one, and a bigger one than font_size.

THE FIX
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
`HeaderProcessor` now falls back to the header LINE's own text when it has no
child, and skips only a header with no text anywhere.

    Sections created                 9,034  ->  43,159
    documents with >=1 Section         499  ->   1,128
    documents flat                      336  ->     431
    documents with 2 levels             163  ->     699

Every recovered heading arrives with its LaTeX command intact — MathPix writes
`\section*{…}` into the header line's own `text_display` and puts the plain
caption in `text`, which is exactly why they never needed the child:

    section       24,809
    subsection    18,349
    (none)             2

Caption quality across all 43,159: 42,634 usable, 525 thin (under 3 characters
or digits only). 98.8%.

WHY font_size NOW FIRES ONCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because 43,158 of 43,159 headers carry an explicit `\section`-family command,
and the command wins — correctly, as 259 argued: the author's own statement of
depth beats an inference from pixel height. The ranking is not wasted; it is
the fallback for the case that turns out to be rare in this corpus. 259's
mechanism was sound and its measurement was not.

NO DOCUMENT REACHES 3 LEVELS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
699 documents have exactly 2 and none have more, because
`\subsubsection`/`\paragraph` do not occur at all — 0 of 43,159. Whether MathPix
never emits them or these documents never use them is not something this
measurement can distinguish, and I am not going to guess. It is stated here as
an open question rather than reported as a depth limit.

Suite 2,582. 4 tests: a childless header becoming a Section, a childless
subsection keeping its depth, the child still winning where there is one, and a
header with no text anywhere still being skipped.
