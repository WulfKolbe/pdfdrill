WHY THE HEIGHT IS BOUNDED, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH KEEPING
-----------------------------------------------------------
The width cap bounds the height for free, but only because of the shape of
the evidence. A crop is drawn at min(region width x px2mm, 74 mm), so its
drawn height is 74 mm x (h/w). The tallest ratio in this document is
513/695 = 0.74, giving 54.6 mm.

That holds because these crops are LINES AND DISPLAYS — wide and short. A
crop with the opposite ratio would not be bounded: a 200 x 2000 px region
drawn 74 mm wide is 740 mm tall, three times the text block, and longtable
would drop the row in silence. Nothing in the code prevents that today; the
corpus simply does not contain one. That is a property of the input, not a
guarantee of the builder, and it is the thing to watch if B is ever pointed
at a document with tall narrow figures.

The `1 demoted` is the compile fixpoint demoting one malformed row to
source-only — the mechanism working, not a height loss.
