675 — MathPix's math delimiters, translated into each projection's own form
      (measurement + the normalizer; not yet wired)

WHY
---
674 refused the transclusion of math on a table cell, a heading, a title, a
TOC entry and a figure label. Refusing the transclusion leaves MathPix's own
delimiters standing in the text — and they are MathPix's, not ours.

WHAT MATHPIX ACTUALLY WRITES
-----------------------------
Measured over 306 documents, both `text` and `text_display`:

    \(...\)   1,012,698        \[...\]   28,857
    $...$            74        $$...$$        2

So MathPix is consistent: `\(...\)` inline, `\[...\]` display, essentially
never dollars. The delimiter is not document-dependent after all — the
earlier "$...$" reading was my own regex matching TiddlyWiki's `{{!!field}}`
and `<$link>` syntax, not maths. Corrected before it reached the fix.

WHAT OUR PROJECTIONS EXPECT
----------------------------
Each projection already HAS a standard, and they are not the same one:

    TiddlyWiki   <$latex text="…" displayMode="…"/>    (TEMPLATES, FO/EQ)
    OKF/Markdown $…$ / $$ … $$        (okf._self_widgets_to_markdown)
    LaTeX        delimiters stripped, wrapped in an environment
                 (latex_pipeline._strip_math_delimiters)
    HTML         katex.render on a data-tex attribute

They agree on the ROUTE: the TiddlyWiki widget is the pivot, and every other
projection converts from it. Math that becomes a DocObject takes that route.
Math that stays in a body text does not — it bypasses the pivot and reaches
every projection as MathPix's raw delimiters.

THE MODULE — src/docops/mathdelims.py
--------------------------------------
One conversion at the pivot, three named policies:

    to_tiddlywiki   \(x\) -> <$latex text="x" displayMode="false"/>
    to_markdown     \(x\) -> $x$        \[x\] -> $$ x $$
    to_plain        \(x\) -> x
    render(text, policy)  — an unknown policy raises, never defaults

`test_markdown_matches_what_okf_makes_of_the_widget` pins the agreement: a
body converted directly and the same body converted via the widget land on
the same string, so a formula does not read differently depending on whether
it happened to become an object.

A body carrying `\begin{...}` is LaTeX SOURCE and is returned untouched — a
Table's `raw_text` tabular, a listing. Rewriting math inside a tabular breaks
the tabular, and those bodies go to the SVG route (RECLASSIFY.md) where the
raw LaTeX is what gets compiled. 159 such bodies in the published set.

THE QUOTING DECISION, and why it is a refusal
----------------------------------------------
A TiddlyWiki attribute has no escape character, so a body containing `"`
cannot be double-quoted. TiddlyWiki accepts single and triple-double
quoting — but the reader downstream does not: `okf._attr_value` parses only
the double-quoted form, and `okf._ATTR_RE` tokenises on whitespace, so a
single-quoted value with spaces does not survive tokenisation at all. A
quote-switching version was written first and its own test caught this.

So the body is left in MathPix's delimiters and `unconvertible()` reports it.
Measured: 74 of 1,428,984 math bodies contain a `"` — 0.005%, all of them a
`\text{"…"}` inside the maths. `to_markdown` has no such limit and converts
them, which is correct: dollars have no quoting problem.
