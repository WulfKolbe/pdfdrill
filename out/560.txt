`has_tiddlers` globs on purpose. Its siblings are not the pre-rename
predecessors 558 deleted — they are VARIANT arrays, `x.spoken.tiddlers.json`
and the translated projection. All of them are projections of one model and
must be current TOGETHER, so `artifact_current` takes min(mtime) and a
freshly rebuilt main array must not mask a stale spoken one.

"Which file is this document's array" and "are all its projections current"
are different questions, and only the first was globbing for want of an
answer. The glob stays, with that reason written beside it.
