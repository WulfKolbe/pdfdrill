160 — children_ids as a route to equation confidence
==============================================================================
QUESTION
Follow children_ids from equation containers to the child math line and read
the confidence there. How many of the 1,148 previously-unscored equations gain
a value?

ANSWER
Zero. Not "few" — zero, and for a structural reason: no equation ever has
children_ids. The route does not exist.

THE ROUTE DOES NOT EXIST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scanned every lines.json in the corpus (1,336 documents).

   equation/math lines examined            129,506
   ... of those, carrying children_ids            0
   unscored equation/math lines             29,048
   ... of those, carrying children_ids            0

children_ids is real, and common — 60,000+ occurrences in a 400-document
sample — but it belongs exclusively to STRUCTURAL containers:

   complex_cell 24,863   table 7,212   list_item 11,148   figure_label 1,850
   column 3,677   table_spanning_cell 4,409   footnote 731   section_header 680
   authors 429   abstract 344   title 232   diagram 357   toc_row 1,078  ...

Not one of those categories is `math` or `equation`. MathPix does not model an
equation as a container of child lines; an equation is a leaf.

THE REVERSE DIRECTION, ALSO TESTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If an equation is not a parent, it might be a CHILD of something scored. Built
the inverted index (child id -> parent line) and asked it directly:

   unscored equations that are a child of ANY container       34  (of 29,048)
   ... whose parent carries a confidence                      22
   parent types: complex_cell 22, list_item 6, column 6

22 of 29,048 is 0.07%, and the value would be the parent's confidence, not the
equation's. That is not a route, it is a rounding error.

Tested the same question a third way at the object level — model objects have
their own `children` list — and got 0 of 23,698.

WHAT DOES RECOVER VALUES: 4,573 EQUATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
While testing route B (object -> stream anchor -> line payload) something else
turned up. For each unscored Equation object I resolved its `mathpix_lines`
anchor and read the payload sitting at it:

   unscored Equation objects                            23,698   (162 documents)
   ... with a mathpix_lines anchor                      15,833
   ... whose OWN payload already carries a confidence    4,573   <-- recoverable
   ... with no mathpix anchor at all                     7,865   (LaTeX-source
                                                                  equations; no
                                                                  MathPix score
                                                                  exists for them)

4,573 equations have the number sitting in the stream we already store, one
dereference from the object that reports it missing. Nothing needs fetching.

VERIFIED END TO END, AND A TRAP FOUND ON THE WAY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1206.0238, two equations, both unscored, both with payload confidence = 1.

   pdfdrill model <pdf>            -> Equation 2, scored 0   (NO CHANGE)
   pdfdrill model --force <pdf>    -> Equation 2, scored 2   (recovered)

A plain re-run does not recover them. It prints "Built unified model: 238
objects" and changes nothing — the rebuild guard declines, and the summary line
it prints is indistinguishable from the summary of a real build. I read that
line as evidence of a rebuild and concluded the value was unrecoverable; it was
only when a delete-and-rebuild produced different object ids that the no-op
became visible. `--force` is required, and the output gives no sign of which
one you got.

CORRECTING out/120
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
out/120 said of this residue:

   "those documents' lines.json carries no per-line confidence. Same shape as
    the pdfminer finding - the field is absent at source, not dropped in transit."

That is wrong for 4,573 of today's unscored equations: the field is present at
source, present in the payload we stored, and absent only on the object. It was
dropped in transit after all.

Three of the five documents out/120 named as worst cases are now fully scored:

   1605.03431                  146 equations, 146 scored
   DKT05                        60 equations,  60 scored
   Normalization_square_roots   64 equations,  64 scored

THE 1,148 FIGURE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I cannot report a gain against that exact population and will not pretend to.
out/120 measured 119 of 724 documents; the corpus is now 1,336 models, roughly
tripled by the overnight MathPix batch, and out/120 lists only its five largest
documents, not the 119. The population no longer exists to be re-measured.

What is measurable today, stated with its own population:

   documents with equations                              1,036
   equations                                           119,328
   unscored equations                                   23,698  (19.9%)
   documents with at least one unscored equation           162
   documents where EVERY equation is unscored              129  (23,636 eqs)

   recoverable by `model --force`                        4,573  (19.3% of unscored)
   recoverable via children_ids                              0

Largest fully-unscored documents: Srednicki QFT (2,894), Principles of Model
Checking (2,149), Ibragimov Tensors (1,868), Debarre Algèbre 1 (1,451),
qftbook (1,447).

CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The children_ids hypothesis is dead and cheap to bury: the attribute never
appears on an equation, in either direction, at either the line or the object
level. The 4,573 that a forced rebuild would recover is the finding worth
keeping, and it is free — no API call, no new data.
