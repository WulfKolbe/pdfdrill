WHY TWO ARE EMPTY, AND WHY IT IS NOT A MISALIGNMENT
-----------------------------------------------------
inkdrill measured 55 lattice rows for 57 table rows. collect_results pairs the
two sequences with `zip(idents, recs)`, which stops at the shorter — so the last
two identifiers get no record.

That could have been much worse than two blank fields. zip pairs by POSITION: if
the shortfall happened mid-document, every row after it would be paired with the
NEXT equation's measurement and the whole tail would be silently mislabelled.
The script warns only in the opposite case (`len(idents) < len(recs)`), so this
direction passes unremarked.

It did not happen here, and that is checked rather than assumed:

   measured id sequence == tex row order[:55]   ->  True
   the two unfilled are positions 56 and 57 of 57

So the shortfall is at the END and the 55 written values are correctly paired.

   (My first attempt to verify this used pdftotext to count identifiers per
   page and was useless: it recovers only 45 of the 57, because the identifier
   is set \tiny\ttfamily with \allowbreak breaks. The tex-order comparison
   above is the instrument that can actually answer the question.)

WHAT WOULD FIX IT PROPERLY
--------------------------
Pair on the row's own geometry — the lattice row index inkdrill already reports
— rather than on position in two independently-derived lists. Until then the
`zip` is safe only while the measurement is complete or short at the tail, and
nothing in the script checks which.
