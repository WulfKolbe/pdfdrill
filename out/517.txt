3. WHAT EQ0294 ACTUALLY IS
---------------------------
The crop (report-crops/mielke-geometrodynamics_EQ0294.jpg) prints

  Σ_α = e_α ⌋ L − (e_α ⌋ DΨ) ∧ ∂L/∂DΨ − (e_α ⌋ Ψ) ∧ ∂L/∂Ψ .

with ⌋ used three times as a BINARY OPERATOR — the interior product.
MathPix read the same printed glyph three different ways:

  1st ⌋  ->  \downharpoonleft      wrong glyph, right arity
  2nd ⌋  ->  \downharpoonleft      wrong glyph, right arity
  3rd ⌋  ->  \right\lrcorner       WRONG ARITY — an operator became a fence

To balance the fence it invented `\left.` at the front and moved the
closing paren to `\right)` at the very end. So the third term's bracket
no longer groups `(e_α ⌋ Ψ)`; it groups from the start of the equation to
just after Ψ. The mathematics changed.

The rendered image is nearly identical to the printed one — the ⌟ shrinks
to fence size and hugs e_α, and a large `)` still appears after Ψ. That
is why the ink measured `N|+0`: same component count, distance inside the
noise floor. A grouping error is a small-ink error.

So EQ0294 is invisible to all four gates at once: it compiles, it
renders, the ink agrees, and the confidence is mid-range. That is a
blind spot, not a refusal.

4. DOES renderable() HAVE A DELIMITER LIST? NO.
------------------------------------------------
There is no delimiter list to check for corner symbols or arrows —
neither in renderable() nor anywhere in src/. The only \left/\right rule
in the gate is a COUNT:

  len(findall(r"\\left(?![a-zA-Z])"))  ==  len(findall(r"\\right(?![a-zA-Z])"))

Any token may follow \right; only the tally is checked. So `\right\lrcorner`
passes for the same reason `\right)` does, and a delimiter list would not
have caught EQ0294 either — the value is balanced. What is wrong is the
arity of the symbol, which no counting rule can see.

6. AN INSTRUMENT ERROR, RECORDED
---------------------------------
My first scan returned 24,497 rows, with `a` (28,029), `l`, `h`, `s`, `r`
as the top "non-standard delimiters". Those are \rightarrow,
\rightleftharpoons, \rightharpoonup, \rightsquigarrow, \rightrightarrows:
the pattern `\\right\s*(...)` matched inside every arrow command. Fixed
with the same `(?![a-zA-Z])` lookahead renderable() already uses; the
count fell from 24,497 to 35 and the refused count from 28 to 0.

Both numbers were plausible on their face. The tell was that `a` cannot
be a delimiter.

7. WHAT THIS SUGGESTS, NOT BUILT
---------------------------------
`\right` + a corner symbol is 35 rows in 7 documents and, on the four
