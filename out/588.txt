588 - the manifest describes what was SELECTED, not what was SHIPPED

CONFIRMED. johnston-linear-matrix-algebra, findings shape, both bounds:

  --pages   pdf pages   manifest rows   manifest distinct   shipped distinct
  10        10         68              52                  24
  0         20         68              52                  52

  --pages 0 : 52 against 52 - THEY AGREE, and nothing ships that the
              manifest does not name.
  --pages 10: 52 against 24 - 28 identifiers named and never shipped,
              0 shipped that are not named. One-directional, which is
              what truncation predicts and what a producer bug would not.

24 IS THE NUMBER IN THE ORIGINAL ERROR:
    MeasureRefused: run holds 24 data rows, the manifest expects 68
The manifest's 68 is its ROW count (24 corrected lines - 8 pairs x 3 -
plus 7 unresolved plus 37 flagged); 52 is the distinct identifiers behind
those rows; 24 is what a 10-page shipout leaves. inkdrill counted 24 and
was told to expect 68.

THE MECHANISM, in the code

  report_tex.py ~3249-3518   tables_manifest.append(...) runs once per row
                             as the rows are emitted - every row, no bound
  report_tex.py 3534         pagesel_line(pages) goes into the PREAMBLE
  report_tex.py 3539         the manifest is written from the full list

And the reason the two cannot agree, already written down at
report_tex.py:2043 in the constant's own comment:

    `pagesel` discards pages at SHIPOUT, so the document is still TYPESET
    in full

So the row list is complete by construction and the PDF is not. This is
the pages_bound class in a third place: 578 found --pages dropped by the
CLI, then rebound by two different functions to two different wrong
values; here it reaches the build correctly and the manifest simply does
not know about it.

WHAT FOLLOWS

--pages and measurement are incompatible by construction. A bounded
report can be read and cannot be measured: the artefact inkdrill
rasterises is the shipout, and the record it segments by is the row list.
Truncating the manifest to the shipped rows would make the two agree, but
the honest form is the one already implied - phase 1 refuses a bound
rather than producing a manifest it cannot honour.

585 already made phase 1 unbounded (MEASURE_PAGES_BOUND = None), so the
pipeline no longer creates this state. What it still does is ACCEPT a
bound silently on any other reporttex build and write a manifest that
disagrees with the PDF beside it. Not fixed here - this task was a
hypothesis to check.

johnston is left built at --pages 0: 20 pages, manifest and shipout in
agreement at 52. That is the self-consistent state of the two.
