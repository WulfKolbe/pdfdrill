THREE THINGS I CLAIMED TONIGHT AND HAD TO RETRACT, recorded because the
method that produced them is the lesson:

  1. "s42256 EQ0018 is a substituted sub-expression at confidence 1.0."
     FALSE. I had compared two DIFFERENT ROWS. My band extraction took the
     last three vertical rules in a band, and the bands spanned 2-3 table
     rows -- ratios 1.9x to 3.4x the crop height on every row I checked.
     Every image verdict before that point came from the wrong pixels.
     The fix: compare the shipped crop JPEG (keyed by identifier) against
     the reading typeset on its own. No geometry, nothing to mis-pair.

  2. "1910.05241 EQ0025 drops the tenth matrix column." FALSE. The array
     spec is 9 and row 1 holds 9 cells; they agree, and across all 16
     array-bearing rows in that document none is wider than its spec. I
     miscounted a wide crop against a differently scaled rendering.

  3. "The straddle rate went UP after the escaper (4.71% vs 3.93%)." That
     compared two different populations. Like-for-like on wzlxjtu, the only
     corpus with both states: 1.61% -> 1.35%.

THE ESCAPER'S REAL SCORECARD (709b). Worst unbreakable run in the shipped
