343 - the placement split, on measured extents
==================================================================

HOW MANY DOCUMENTS NEEDED COMPILING: NONE.

  The author's own compiled PDF is already in the library for all eleven
  documents -- 4 to 135 pages each, the largest 33 MB. The rectangle can
  be READ from it with pdfplumber instead of derived from `scale=` and the
  file's pixel size. 337 is what made that necessary: for width=\linewidth
  I had hardcoded 'a Tufte text block, ~12 cm', and against the real
  rectangles mpsapprox2 was 340 pt computed against 143 measured, psi_N2
  261 against 142, pt2 408 against 222.

  The error has a DIRECTION. An extent that is too large pulls siblings
  inside it, so computing the rectangle manufactures overlap. That is why
  the correction moves one way.

THE SPLIT

                        342 computed     343 measured
  around                       79              106
  overlapping                 114              62
  ------------------------------------------------
  share overlapping           59%             37%

  THE MAJORITY INVERTS. 342 reported 59% overlapping and concluded a
  prompt assuming 'around the figure' is wrong for most rows. Measured, it
  is right for most rows: 106 of 168, 63%, sit outside the image.

WHAT MOVED

  around       -> around       63
  overlapping  -> overlapping  60
  overlapping  -> around       28
  around       -> overlapping  2

ROWS THE MEASUREMENT CANNOT DECIDE, and they are not counted above

  several sizes       33
  none placed         10
  no tikzpicture       6
  unreadable base      3
  not embedded         1

  `several sizes` is the honest one: the same image file is placed at
  DIFFERENT rectangles in different figures, so one measured extent cannot
  stand for all of its inclusions. Resolving those needs each inclusion
  matched to its own placement, which is a further join and is not done
  here rather than guessed at.

  `not embedded` is one row in 1805.00791, whose figure is vector rather
  than raster, so pdfplumber's image list does not carry it.

DOES IT SCALE PAST SEVEN

  Yes. The recoverable population is 106 rows, not 79 -- and 337 measured
  that population at a median ink distance of 10 against 180 for the
  overlapping one, with the two not touching. So the set worth calling a
  model on is a third larger than 342 implied, and it is the majority of
  what could be decided rather than a minority.

  The 33 ambiguous rows are worth resolving before that, since they are
  the largest undecided block and would likely land mostly in `around` too
  -- but that is a prediction, and it is written here as one.

out/343.json carries every row with its measured extent and counts.
