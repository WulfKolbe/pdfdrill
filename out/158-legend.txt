158 — the legend on every page (\endfoot + \endlastfoot)
========================================================

WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
The legend was emitted AFTER \end{longtable}, so it appeared exactly once — on
whichever page the table happened to finish on. For 0902.0431 that was page 26
of 26. A reader on page 7 had a coloured square beside a coloured bullet and no
key anywhere on the sheet.

WHAT IT IS NOW
--------------
The legend is a \multicolumn footer row installed as BOTH hooks:

   <legend row> \\ \hline
   \endfoot
   <legend row> \\ \hline
   \endlastfoot

Both are required and they are not interchangeable: \endfoot is used on every
page EXCEPT the last, \endlastfoot only on the last. Supplying one alone leaves
exactly the complementary set of pages bare.

   endhead      2   (equations table, formulas table)
   endfoot      2
   endlastfoot  2

One detail that had to change with the move: the two legend lines were joined
by `\\`, which inside a \multicolumn cell ends the table ROW and would have
split the legend across two rows. It is now `\newline`, which breaks a line
inside a p-column.

RESULT: 25 OF 27 PAGES
----------------------
   pages carrying the legend : 25
   pages without             : 13 and 19
   last page (27)            : YES — \endlastfoot fires correctly

WHY TWO PAGES STILL HAVE NONE
-----------------------------
Not a missing hook. Both hooks are in the file and both work; pages 13 and 19
are pages longtable had no room to place a foot on.

Each holds exactly ONE row:

   page 13   0902.0431_EQ0937   scan crop 1196x1111px -> 122 x 113 mm
   page 19   0902.0431_EQ0941   scan crop 1220x1144px -> 122 x 114 mm

Those are the two tallest crops in the report; the next largest is 69 mm. And
the crop is not even the tall part — EQ0937's Rendered cell is a multi-line
`aligned` block containing two 3x3 arrays and a column vector, set at natural
size. Header + that row + footer exceeds the 261 mm text height of A3
landscape, so longtable places the row alone and drops the foot.

The compile log confirms it is a fit problem and not an error: 0 Overfull
\vbox, no longtable warnings. LaTeX did what it was asked and there was nowhere
to put the footer.

WHY I HAVE NOT "FIXED" THOSE TWO
---------------------------------
The obvious remedy is to cap the crop height so no single row can consume a
page. That would break something load-bearing: the crops are placed at
PIXEL-EXACT original size via px2mm derived from pdfinfo and the lines.json,
which is what makes the Scan column measurable against the Rendered column at
all. Scaling a crop to fit a footer would silently invalidate the ink
comparison for that row.

Shrinking the legend would not reliably help either — the shortfall on these
pages is the height of an entire multi-line equation, not the two lines of the
key.

So the honest state is: the legend repeats on every page where a footer can be
placed, which is 25 of 27, and the two exceptions are pages occupied by a
single row taller than the space a footer needs. If those two matter, the fix
is to the ROW (split the rendered cell, or drop the crop to a linked thumbnail
for oversized rows), not to the legend.
