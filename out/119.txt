THE TWO THAT ARE NOT PURELY TYPOGRAPHIC
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 16  1510.06699_EQ0188   ink 16, conf 0.987   BORDERLINE
     Scan: \gamma^{a\,0} and a\phi^{2\,0}\mathcal{R}. Render: \gamma^{a0} and
     a\phi^{20}\mathcal{R}. MathPix closed the gap between two tensor indices.

     Read one way it is spacing and the meaning survives: a and 0 are still
     two indices. Read the other way "a0" and "20" are single tokens and a
     parser downstream cannot recover the split. I count it separately rather
     than force it, because the judgement depends on who reads the LaTeX next.

     Both sides also carry ".(204)" INSIDE the math - MathPix put the equation
     number in the latex. That is a genuine defect and it is invisible to the
     ink, because the scan carries it too.

 17  1510.06699_EQ0239   ink 6, conf 0.997   TYPOGRAPHY + REGION OVERRUN
     Content identical, \dfrac against \tfrac. The scan crop additionally
     carries a trailing ", (" - the opening of an equation number the region
     caught. Its comp -2 is that fragment, not a transcription error.

WHY THIS MATTERS FOR out/115
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   115 measured variants C and D on these exact 20 rows and got a median of
   0 with no improvement. This file is why: there was nothing for a better
   transcription to fix. A vision model cannot make \left( render at a fixed
   size, cannot restore a trailing comma the pipeline deliberately moved to
   another field, and cannot widen a \bar into an \overline.

   The null in 115 is not evidence that the model is weak on hard rows. It is
   evidence that these rows are not transcription problems at all.

AND IT SETS A CEILING ON THE INK METRIC HERE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   These 20 are a stratified sample of the 724 flagged rows - median ink 10
   against a population median of 10. If the sample is representative, then
   the flagged population is overwhelmingly typographic, and out/100's
   classification agrees from the other side: 43% 'wrong' by mark shape, 22%
   merged glyphs, 14% pure displacement.

   So "ink >= 6 and confidence >= 0.9" is not a defect detector. It is a
   RENDERING-DIFFERENCE detector, and on this corpus the rendering differs
   from the page far more often than the reading does.

   That is a limit of what the standalone-render comparison can find, not a
   limit of the corpus. Closing it means rendering with the document's own
   preamble - its delimiter conventions, its \dfrac habits - rather than a
   generic standalone, which is a different instrument than the one that
   produced these numbers.

METHOD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Each row viewed as a stacked pair, MathPix's render above its scan crop,
   at full width. Judged on whether every symbol, index, sign and coefficient
   matches - not on the five-tuple, which is what out/100 does mechanically
   and which cannot make this call.
