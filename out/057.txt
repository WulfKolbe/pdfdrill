WHY THE EFFECT IS SMALL, AND WHY IT WAS STILL WORTH DOING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   12 equations of 1,328 is 0.9%, and out/041 predicted exactly that: 12 of
   its 935 differing pairs collapsed to zero when spacing-only tokens were
   neutralised. The measurement agreeing with a prediction made from other
   data is the useful part, not the size.

   The reason to make the change is not the 12 rows. It is that out/055
   found the two comparators disagreeing about the SAME character while
   being used in sequence: injectlatex PAIRS equations with
   normalize_latex (>= 0.55, ~ deleted) and 040 then SCORES those pairs
   with the SLT (~ counted). A pair could be admitted on the understanding
   that ~ is irrelevant and then charged for it — a bias that fell entirely
   on MathPix, since \mathrm{~d} is its habit and not the authors'.
