on every subscripted occurrence — which is almost all of them.

THE LESSON, WHICH IS 525's LESSON AGAIN
-----------------------------------------
A word boundary after a LaTeX command name is wrong whenever the command
takes a subscript, and every conditional-independence symbol in this
corpus takes one. The check that would have caught it is the one this
project keeps applying to everything else: ask whether the test COULD have
returned a different answer. 36 of 139 looked plausible, and the count of
DISTINCT SPELLINGS — one, where the vendor correspondence said six —
should have been the tell. I read it as evidence the corpus had changed
instead of evidence my pattern had.
