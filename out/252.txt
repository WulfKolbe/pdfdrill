252 — conversion_output: unread, and the premise does not hold
==============================================================================

DOES PDFDRILL READ IT? NO — IT ONLY CARRIES IT.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One occurrence in the whole codebase:

   src/pdfdrill/docpack.py:100   PROF_KEYS = ("font_size", "confidence",
       "confidence_rate", "is_printed", "is_handwritten", "conversion_output")

That is the packer collapsing per-line profile fields into a shared table. It
preserves the value through the .docpack round trip and nothing branches on it.
No module in src/docmodel/modules/ mentions it. No projector mentions it.

THE COUNTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   3,999,815  line objects
   2,007,865  conversion_output = true
   1,457,476  conversion_output = false      36% of all lines
     534,474  field ABSENT (505,464 of them `text`, 29,010 `math`)

"IF IT DOESN'T, WE ARE INCLUDING CONTENT MATHPIX MARKED AS EXCLUDED"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
That is the premise, and it does not survive the split that matters:

   false, and a CHILD of another line   1,216,604   83%
   false, and TOP-LEVEL                   240,872   17%

`false` does not mark worthless content. It marks a line that must not be
emitted as its OWN block in the converted output — overwhelmingly because
something else already carries it:

   708,915  simple_cell     rendered inside its table
   201,948  text            children of list_item / cells
   154,937  table_row       inside its table
    46,459  table_column        "
    34,369  complex_cell        "
    18,497  list_item       nested inside another list item
    17,563  table_of_contents_row

Every one of those is content pdfdrill SHOULD include, and does — through the
container. TableProcessor builds cells from `children_ids`; ListProcessor now
does the same (out/248). Emitting them standalone as well is exactly what
`false` is telling us not to do, and pdfdrill does not.

AND THE DECISIVE CHECK: ZERO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   text lines with conversion_output = false AND no parent_id:
       0 lines, in 0 documents, across the whole corpus.

There is no standalone prose anywhere that MathPix marked excluded and
pdfdrill includes. The failure the task anticipated does not occur, and it
cannot occur silently in future either — the class is empty, not merely small.

WHAT THE 240,872 TOP-LEVEL FALSE LINES ARE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   112,196  page_info          running heads, folios, "Typeset using REVTEX",
                               "arXiv:quant-ph/0008113v1 25 Aug 2000"
    42,086  equation_number    "(1)" — rendered INSIDE its equation
    35,977  list_item          the container; its children carry the words
    17,392  figure_label
    15,675  column
     6,042  diagram
     4,235  section_header · 2,725 footnote · 1,341 authors · 1,052 title
       662  abstract · 632 table_of_contents_container

pdfdrill's existing treatment already agrees with the marking, by other means:
page_info and figure_label are paragraph BREAK types that produce no object;
equation_number is attached to its Equation rather than emitted separately;
list_item containers are read for structure and their children for words.

The agreement is coincidental rather than derived — nothing consults the field
— but it is agreement, and the audit was worth running to establish that
rather than assume it in either direction.

WHERE READING IT WOULD ACTUALLY CHANGE SOMETHING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three types appear in the top-level false set AND are consumed into objects by
pdfdrill: section_header (4,235), footnote (2,725), title (1,052), authors
(1,341), abstract (662). Those are lines MathPix says should not be emitted as
their own block, which pdfdrill turns into Section, Footnote, Page-title and
Abstract objects.

That is not necessarily wrong — a Section object is structure, not emitted
prose, and the marking is about conversion output rather than about modelling.
But it is the only place the two disagree, it is ~10,000 lines, and I have not
established which is right. Recorded, not acted on.

Nothing changed in this task. The field stays unread, because the audit found
no case where reading it would remove content that is being wrongly included.
