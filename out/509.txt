THE RULE AS MEASURED
--------------------
  corrected            the row carries an accepted refinement. A PAIR: the
                       failed entry above, the recovered one below, both
                       against the same scan. Two rows of space.
  unresolved           the row does not render and nothing has repaired it.
  doubted but correct  confidence below the threshold AND the ink says the
                       render matches the scan.

"The ink says it's right" is K (clean), N (below the measured noise floor) or
S (stable across 300 and 600 dpi). W (weak) and C (component) flag a real
difference and are not agreement.

Everything else — read correctly, ink agrees, nothing changed — says nothing
and is already in formula-report.html and tables.html.

  2010.14265 loses 3.

  Note what this does NOT mean. 1510.06699 has 279 equations and an ink
  distribution of C 33, W 35, N 157, K 52, S 2 — 68 rows the ink calls a real
  difference. It has nothing to say under this rule because none of those
  rows was CHANGED and none FAILS TO RENDER. The rule reports what was done
  about a defect, not that a defect was measured; the residual column in
  formula-report.html still carries the measurement.

  Whether that is what you want is worth deciding explicitly. "The ink
  disagrees and nobody has acted on it" is arguably a fourth state, and under
  the present rule it is invisible.

"DOUBTED BUT CORRECT" IS ZERO AT 0.01, AND THAT IS NOT A BROKEN JOIN
---------------------------------------------------------------------
I checked, because a zero that size usually is one. The join is exact: on
johnston, 1,670 ink rows against 1,670 EQ titles, 1,670 in common, ids like
`johnston-linear-matrix-algebra_EQ0001` on both sides.

The zero is real. Across the 21 documents, 7,548 rows carry both a confidence
and an ink class, and the confidence bands look like this:

  confidence        rows   ink agrees   share
  -------------------------------------------
  0.00 – 0.01         10            0      0%
  0.01 – 0.05         27            5     19%
  0.05 – 0.10         25            5     20%
  0.10 – 0.30        138           33     24%
  0.30 – 0.50        236           76     32%
  0.50 – 0.90      1,124          662     59%
  0.90 – 1.01      5,988        4,822     81%
  -------------------------------------------
  all              7,548        5,603

Only TEN rows in the whole published corpus have a confidence below 0.01, and
the ink disagrees with every one of them. johnston's single such row is
EQ0528 at 0.000514, ink class C|+54 — MathPix doubted it and the ink says the
doubt was justified.

So at your threshold the section would be empty in all 21 documents. Move it
to 0.05 and it holds five rows, in three documents:

  fong-spivak-seven-sketches   3
  1510.06699                   1
  gilmore-lie-groups           1

That is a real population and a small one. It is also, on this evidence, the
opposite of a calibration failure: the share of rows the ink agrees with
rises monotonically with confidence, 0% -> 19% -> 20% -> 24% -> 32% -> 59% ->
81%. MathPix's confidence tracks the ink across the whole range. The
correspondence's existing finding — that their confidence does more work than
either side expected — is confirmed here from a second direction, and the
"doubted but correct" section documents the exceptions to a rule that holds.

Worth saying plainly before it is built: five rows across three documents is
a section that will be absent from eighteen reports.

ON THE NAME
-----------
Splitting it reads better than one heading, and the counts support the split:
31 corrected rows against 38 unresolved is two populations of comparable size
doing different work.

  Corrected            31 pairs, in 6 documents
  Unresolved           38 rows, in 11 documents
  Doubted but correct   0 at 0.01, 5 at 0.05, in 3 documents

`residual` stays the right word inside the table, on the column that carries
the class code, because that is what the ink computes. It stops being the
section's name.

NOT BUILT
---------
Nothing was changed. Three things want a decision before it is:

  1  the doubted threshold — 0.01 gives an empty section everywhere; 0.05
     gives five rows in three documents
  2  whether "the ink disagrees and nobody acted" is a fourth state or stays
     out. 68 such rows in 1510.06699 alone, which is the difference between
     that report being one page and being twelve
  3  what a corrected pair shows when the failed entry does not render at all

     "before" half of the pair is a blank cell and the scan is the only
