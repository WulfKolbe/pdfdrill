125 — do inline formulas have their own region, or only a parent line region?
=============================================================================

ANSWER: none of them have their own. Not one, in any document.

POOLED (every model on disk)
----------------------------
   documents with maths objects            1,138
   Formula  objects                      266,957     with own region:      0   (0.00%)
   Equation objects                       49,004     with own region: 37,496   (76.52%)

   documents where ANY Formula has its own region:  0

   The Equation figure is the control. A per-object region is something this
   model plainly CAN carry, and does carry on three quarters of display
   equations — so 0.00% on inline formulas is a property of the extraction, not
   a missing feature.

WHAT THEY HAVE INSTEAD
----------------------
   Formula props : bibkey, display, flow_index, latex, next_in_flow, page,
                   parent_section, prev_in_flow
   Equation props: the same PLUS region, cdn_url, confidence, confidence_rate,
                   image_id, latex_raw, refnum

   Every Formula carries a `page`, and a `surface` realization anchored into
   the `mathpix_lines` stream. That anchor is the parent line:

      formula   A_{n}, B_{n}, C_{n}, D_{n}
      parent    type=text  region={x:462, y:900, w:1287, h:45}
                text "Lie algebras, called \(A_{n}, B_{n}, C_{n}, D_{n}\)
                      (classical type) and \(G_{2}..."

   Corpus-wide:
      anchor resolves to a mathpix_lines unit   232,195   (86.98%)
      ... whose parent line carries a region    232,195   (86.98%)
      in models with no mathpix_lines stream     34,762   (13.02%)

   The two resolution figures are identical: whenever the anchor resolves, the
   parent line HAS a region. There is no third case where a formula resolves to
   a line that is itself unplaced.

   The 34,762 are LaTeX-source models, which have no OCR stream to anchor into
   at all — those formulas have a page and nothing else.

WHAT A PARENT-LINE REGION IS WORTH
----------------------------------
   parent-line width, px:  min 15   median 1,292   p90 1,612   max 3,792

   The median parent line is ~1,292 px wide and 45 px tall — a full text line.
   An inline formula is typically a few characters inside it. So "has a region"
   via the parent is true and nearly useless for locating the formula: it
   narrows the position to a line, not to the mathematics.

   That is the practical consequence, and it is why the count matters. Any
   operation needing a crop of an inline formula — an ink comparison against
   the scan, a CDN crop, a per-formula confidence — cannot be done from this
   model. All three exist for Equations and none can exist for Formulas, and
   the reason is one field.

   It also explains a result from earlier today: 0902.0431's report has crops
   and a confidence column for its 1,219 equations and neither for its 3,170
   inline formulas. That is not a projector omission. There is nothing to
   project.

WHY IT IS STRUCTURAL
--------------------
   MathPix emits a `math` line for DISPLAY mathematics — its own element, with
   its own region, its own confidence, its own crop. Inline mathematics is not
   an element: it arrives as `\( ... \)` inside the text of a `text` line, and
   the FormulaProcessor extracts it from that string. There is no coordinate to
   inherit because MathPix never measured one.

   So this is not a defect in the model builder. Recovering per-formula regions
   would mean measuring them ourselves — locating the inline span within the
   parent line's raster — which is a different capability, not a fix.
