==============================================================================

PART 1 — THE ROW DOES NOT FAIL FOR THE REASON THE QUESTION ASSUMES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The row is EQ0857, Geometrodynamics of Gauge Fields (Mielke), page 278,
equation (12.4.4), MathPix confidence **0.925** — a green cell:

   \left.\mathbf{匕}_{n} \mathscr{G}^{A} \cong n\right\rfloor
                                    \left(D \underline{\tau}^{A}\right),

It is refused by report_tex.renderable(), first gate:

       if cjk_defect(lx):
           return ""          # 128: hallucinated script never reaches xelatex

**匕 is U+5315**, a CJK ideograph, standing where Mielke writes the Lie
derivative script letter. MathPix hallucinated a Chinese character; the 128
rule refuses the whole snippet on sight. The \rfloor has nothing to do with it.

That is not an inference. Three measurements:

   1. the snippet compiled standalone under the report preamble — 0 errors
   2. the snippet compiled inside a longtable with the report's exact column
      spec (|p{20mm}|p{7mm}|p{13mm}|p{92mm}|p{104mm}|p{120mm}|) — 0 errors
   3. the row restored into the full report.tex and compiled whole —
      **494 pages, 0 errors**

The \left. … \right\rfloor construction renders. It renders WRONG, which is
part 2, but it renders.

WHILE THERE — THE OTHER TWELVE NON-RENDERING MIELKE ROWS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rebuilding report.tex from the tiddlers before any demotion separates the two
mechanisms cleanly. 13 rows do not render, from two different places:

   FOUR refused at BUILD time by renderable(), never offered to xelatex:
      EQ0472  p155  CJK 十 (U+5341) in \stackrel{十}{\Omega}
      EQ0857  p278  CJK 匕 (U+5315)                      <- the row asked about
      EQ0190  p080  trailing "}" after \] — brace imbalance
      EQ0453  p151  trailing "}" after \] — brace imbalance

   NINE demoted at COMPILE time, and all nine are the same error:
      ! Undefined control sequence  ...  \overparen
      (lines 164, 301, 345, 388, 681, 1699, 1700, 1845, 2834)

**\overparen is simply not defined in the report preamble.** It is not an OCR
defect at all — MathPix read the wide arc accents correctly and the preamble
cannot typeset them. One \usepackage (yhmath / mathabx) or one \newcommand
recovers 9 of this document's 13 unrendered rows. Two of the remaining four
are a stray closing brace the \end{X} stripper does not cover, and only two
are genuine OCR hallucination.

PART 2 — \left / \right PAIRED WITH CONTENT, CORPUS-WIDE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POPULATION: 1,227 documents that have a .md, 1,164,283 math blobs
(display $$…$$ and inline $…$), scanned in full.

CRITERION. \left…\right nest, so every \right has exactly one \left partner;
the PAIR carries the meaning, and counting tokens instead would be wrong.
Flagged: a pair with the null delimiter `.` on one side and a FLOOR or CEILING
on the other. Mathematics has no half-floor idiom, so a null-paired floor is
not a fence — it is an operator that MathPix read as the closing half of
\lfloor…\rfloor and wrapped in \left…\right to make it stretch.

Every one of the 18 is exterior calculus or geometric (Clifford) algebra, and
in every one the glyph is the same object: the **contraction / interior
product**. e_β ⌋ ϑ^α = δ^α_β in Mielke; A_k ⌋ B_s and A_k ⌊ B_s, the right and
left contractions, in Hitzer, inner_2002 and the 1306.1660 pair. It is one
mathematical idea, not a scatter of unrelated glitches.

WHAT MAKES IT CERTAIN: THE SAME DOCUMENTS ENCODE IT BOTH WAYS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In those same 18 documents the same operator is written **bare** — correctly,
as an ordinary symbol between operands — and **wrapped** in \left/\right:

                bare   wrapped
                 567       584      49.3% bare / 50.7% wrapped

A coin flip. Same author, same page, same glyph, and MathPix produces two
incompatible encodings at near-equal rates. The wrapping is therefore not a
reading of the source; it is instability in the recogniser. (1205.5935v1: 236
bare vs 222 wrapped. Mielke: 32 vs 95. inner_2002: 58 vs 39.)

THIS IS NOT A COMPILE FAILURE. IT IS A SILENT MIS-RENDER.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   of the 315 flagged blobs, **314 pass renderable() and render** (100%)
   the 1 refusal is EQ0857 — and it is refused for CJK, not for the floor

renderable() compares the COUNT of \left against the COUNT of \right. The
construction is balanced, so it passes; xelatex accepts \rfloor as a delimiter,
so it compiles. No gate anywhere looks at whether the two halves of a pair are
partners. 424 operators are on rendered pages right now, sized as fences.

Measured, at \displaystyle, comparing wrapped against bare:

   e_β ⌋ ϑ^α                          width  28.97pt  vs  26.11pt   +11%
   (∂L/∂(dA)) ⌋ T^β                   height 24.00pt  vs  23.07pt    +4%
   Σ_{j=1}^{n} (a_j/b_j) ⌋ e_j        height 36.00pt  vs  30.67pt   +17%
                                      width  46.41pt  vs  41.32pt   +12%

The pattern is the point, not the percentages: **the operator is sized by the
expression to its left**, and grows without bound as that expression grows. An
operator whose size depends on its operands is wrong at any magnitude. It also
becomes a single \mathinner atom, so it loses the spacing an operator gets and
acquires a grouping the author never wrote.

WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED, AND WHY THE NUMBER WOULD BE 5× LARGER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not flagged: | \| \vert \Vert \uparrow \downarrow \updownarrow \backslash / —
because two null-paired idioms are correct LaTeX and common:

   \left. X \right|_{a}^{b}      restriction / evaluation bar
   \left| \psi \right\rangle     ket — the `|` is the legitimate opener

A first pass that asked only "is the partner glyph absent from the blob?"
returned 4,033 hits, of which **1,816 were \right\rangle** — bra-kets, every
one correct. That detector would have reported a defect five times its real
size and pointed the reader at quantum-mechanics notation.

For completeness, the same null-pairing test widened to parens, brackets,
braces and angles gives **5,922 hits · 4,065 blobs · 413 documents**. That
number is NOT the answer to this question: a paren is never content, and OCR
splitting a formula at a line break produces genuine one-sided parens. It is
recorded only so the strict figure is not mistaken for the whole surface.

TWO NUMBERS THAT DID NOT SURVIVE CHECKING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The first detector counted delimiters with the regex \\right — which also
matches **\rightarrow**. It reported 4 Mielke blobs as unbalanced. All four
were perfectly balanced; the arrows were being counted as fences. Corrected to
\\right(?![a-zA-Z]), the unbalanced count across all 4,091 Mielke blobs is 0.

The first renderable() check ran against the truncated 230-character examples
stored in the scan's JSON and reported 19 of 40 refused. Truncated LaTeX is
unbalanced by construction. Re-run against the full blobs: 1 of 315.

Both were wrong in the direction of making the defect look bigger.

AVAILABLE FIXES, NOT APPLIED HERE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. \overparen in the report preamble — recovers 9 of Mielke's 13 unrendered
   rows, and it is a preamble gap, not an OCR defect.
2. A pairing check in renderable(), or better a repair: a null-paired floor or
   ceiling is the contraction operator; unwrap it to the bare glyph. The 567
   bare occurrences in the same documents are the target form, so the repair
   has its own ground truth. 424 rows across 18 documents.
3. The trailing-"}" case (EQ0190, EQ0453): the \end{X} stripper handles a
   trailing environment closer but not a trailing brace.
