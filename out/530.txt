WHY THE FORMAT CHANGED, IN ONE LINE
------------------------------------
KaTeX renders in a browser with no preamble. 482/484 measured what that
costs: `\bm` 327 occurrences across 7 documents, `\Perp` defined by no
package at all and spanning 10, and 11,088 of 11,624 undefined occurrences
being the source document's own macros. So the HTML rendering could not be
evidence, and 523's plan folded exactly that into B as its equations
section. B is LaTeX, rendered through the document's own preamble.

WHAT THE PROSE SECTION GAINED (529, inside B)

The one miss is `\square`, a QED symbol MathPix emits as its own line with

The section says in the report what it is: "The page, the confidence and
the picture of an inline formula are its HOST LINE's — a formula has none
of its own. A line's confidence is not a formula's." That is 147's rule:
two instruments in adjacent cells must each be named.

**369 inline formulas now have a picture.** They never had one before, in
any artefact.

A JOIN BUG FOUND BY LOOKING AT THE PAGE
----------------------------------------
The first build matched formulas to host lines by first containment. The
formula `X` matched the arXiv margin stamp — `arXiv:2010.14265v2 [stat.ML]
4 Aug 2021`, set vertically — because that line contains an X and comes
first. B drew a 500pt-tall sidebar as the picture of a one-letter formula
and reported the stamp's confidence as the formula's. One row, one page of
whitespace.

`host_of` now prefers a DELIMITED match (`$x$`, `\(x\)`) — the formula as
the page actually set it — and among candidates takes the smallest region.
22 pages became 21 and the rows are uniform. Tested.

I found it by rendering page 4 and looking at it, which is the thing this
whole sequence has been about.
