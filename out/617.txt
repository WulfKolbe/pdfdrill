618 — THE DEPTH WALK REPLACES THE COUNT, AND REFUSES NOTHING NEW

  \left/\right was `count(\left) == count(\right)`. It is now a walk: +1, -1,
  refuse the moment depth goes negative, refuse if it does not end at zero.
  `\right) x \left(` balances by count and is now refused.

  Environments were compared as SORTED MULTISETS, which is order-blind in two
  ways, and both are now refused by a stack walk:
      \end{a}\begin{a}                    a close before its open
      \begin{a}\begin{b}\end{a}\end{b}    interleaved, not nested

  Braces already walked depth; that was correct and is unchanged.

  ROWS THE OLD CHECK PASSED AND THE NEW ONE REFUSES:

      \left/\right     0   of 660,504
      environments     0   of 660,504

  So the answer to "how many rows balance and are still wrong" is ZERO. The
  old check was unsound and the corpus never exercised the unsoundness. That
  is worth saying plainly rather than dressing up: this is a correctness fix
  with no observable effect on any current row, and its value is that the next
  \right)…\left( is refused by the gate instead of reaching xelatex.
