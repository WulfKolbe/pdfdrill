   The mean sits far below the median (0.746 vs 0.927) because the
   distribution is bimodal, not merely skewed: half the lines are at or
   above 0.93 and a quarter sit below 0.53. MathPix is either confident or
   quite unsure; it is rarely in between.

   A separate field `confidence_rate` also exists and is NOT the same
   quantity - median 0.9995, min 0.9402. It never drops low, so a reader
   who picks the wrong field will conclude the OCR is uniformly excellent.
