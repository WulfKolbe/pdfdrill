   READ THE LINE COUNTS BESIDE THOSE MEDIANS. Six of the eight worst pages
   carry a SINGLE math line, so their "median" is that one line and says
   nothing about the page. 117 of the 199 pages have exactly one math line.
   I first wrote that low confidence "clusters by page", using this list as
   the evidence; the list cannot support that claim and the sentence was
   wrong.

   Tested properly instead, on the 31 pages carrying three or more math
   lines: mean within-page variance is 0.036 against 0.074 for the same
   values randomly reshuffled across pages (sd 0.009) - more than four
   standard deviations, so lines on a page ARE more alike in confidence
   than chance. But only 1 of those 31 pages has every line below 0.5. So
   the effect is real and modest: neighbouring equations tend to be read
   about equally well, which is not the same as whole regions failing.
