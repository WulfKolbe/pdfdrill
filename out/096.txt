   not a row that stayed the same.

THE COMPONENT COLUMN IS THE RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Every one of the five goes to a component delta of 0 or +1. Before the fix
   the scan carried 6 to 23 components the render had nothing for; afterwards
   it carries none.

   That is the signature out/069 identified for a content error - components
   large and negative, meaning the scan has marks the render cannot account
   for - and it disappears completely once the truncated words are restored.
   The rows were flagged because our normaliser had deleted the words, and
   the ink metric was correctly reporting that the render was missing them.

   The two that stay above the floor go to 7, one unit over, with their
   components already reconciled. What remains there is layout, not content.

WHAT THIS SETTLES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   out/070 recorded 0911.3722_EQ0019 as a MathPix content error - "drops all
   three arrows, the leading Pack and the trailing -complete." Two corrections
   have now landed on that one row:

     out/093  the arrows were never dropped; our renderer could not set U+21D2
     out/096  the words were never dropped by MathPix either; text_tail
              removed them, and restoring them takes the row from ink 21 to 1

   The row is not an OCR defect at all. MathPix read it correctly at
   confidence 0.916, and every symptom I attributed to the OCR was produced
   downstream of it - once by the renderer, once by the normaliser.

   That is worth more than the three class changes. A finding that survived
   two reports and a cross-session hand-off was an artefact of our own
   pipeline, and the ink metric had been pointing at it correctly the whole
   time.
