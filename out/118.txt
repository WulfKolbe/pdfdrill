READING IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   E and C are indistinguishable on the median (-8 both).

   The honest reading is that this is 19 rows and the two medians are
   close. What it can support: adding the rule sentence did not COST
   anything, which is not obvious - out/113 showed the notation hint (B)
   made things substantially worse than the bare prompt, so a hint is not
   free by default.

   What it cannot support: that the hint fixed spurious fractions. Nobody
   has counted how many of these 19 crops contain a bar that MathPix
   turned into a \frac. Without that denominator, an improvement could
   come from anywhere in the prompt and a null could mean the population
   had nothing to fix - the same trap out/113a hit with B, where the hint
   was measured on 18 crops that did not contain the notation.

WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS CONCLUSIVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Count the crops whose MathPix latex contains a \frac whose numerator or
   denominator is empty or single-character - the signature of out/116's
   spurious rule - and split E's rows on it. That is the B-split lesson
   applied before the run instead of after, and it is one measurement.

   out/117 is the other half: it looks for MISSING components near a rule,
   which is the direction a hint about absent structure could act on. E as
   written cannot use it, because 116 found the opposite error.
