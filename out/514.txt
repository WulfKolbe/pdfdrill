THE TWO CHANGES
---------------
1  THE CROP. It was `<img style="max-width:100%">` in a cell sharing the
   table with seven other columns, so it rendered at roughly an eighth of the
   width — unreadable, and it is the EVIDENCE the two readings are being
   judged against. The scan column now takes 34% of the table, the image
   renders at full column width on a white ground with a hairline border, and
   "open at full size" beside it opens the crop at native size.

   The data URI is emitted ONCE. Putting it in both an `href` and a `src` is
   the obvious way to do the link and it doubled the page from 2.1 MB to
   4.2 MB for no new information; a four-line click handler opens the image's
   own `src` instead. Back to 2.1 MB.

2  THE METRIC'S DIRECTION. The column read "398 → 397" and said nothing about
   which way was better. It now says, in the header and again in the lede:

     ink distance — lower is closer, 0 is exact

   and every row carries its delta, coloured, with the word: `-1 · closer`.
   The direction is not a guess — `inkconvert.flag_of` returns "clean" at
   distance 0 and "noise" at ≤ 7, so lower is closer and 7 is the measured
   noise floor.

   All 32 corrections that carry numbers FELL. None rose. But two fell by one
   — 398→397 and 233→232 — and that is an acceptance a reader should be able
   to see is weak. Before this change they could not.

   33 rows, 32 with a crop. The one without is 0707.4470_FO0175, whose crop
   was never fetched because an FO tiddler carries no CDN uri.

A DEFECT CAUGHT BY BUILDING IT
-------------------------------
The first johnston build came out with NO Corrected section and 551 flagged
rows instead of 543.

`build_report` passed `dest.parent` as the document directory, and `dest` was
a scratch build directory. The model lookup found nothing there, returned no
corrected pairs, and all 8 of johnston's corrections fell through into
"Flagged, not acted on" — 543 + 8 = 551.

Both numbers still looked plausible. A section that loses its rows INTO
another section is the worst shape of wrong, because nothing is missing from
the total. It is fixed to use the tiddlers file's own directory, which is the
document by definition.

This is the second defect in two tasks that only appeared when the artefact
was actually built and looked at — the first being every one of 21 reports
aborting on a missing `\end{document}` while still reporting a page count.
