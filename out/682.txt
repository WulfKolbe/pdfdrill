682 — counting, not membership: 24 rows of one misread symbol in one book

WHAT THE USER FOUND, IN THREE REPORTS
--------------------------------------
  EQ0472  a variable with a slash from lower left to upper right, dropped
  EQ0974  "high conf MathPix but more than 2 character wrong"
  EQ0293  "\rfloor instead of \downharpoonleft ... nearly no ink difference,
           but the angle clearly wrong"

Every one of them RENDERS. Not one fails `display_safe`, the env check, the
CJK check, the compile, or `contradicts()`. They were found by a person
reading the report, which is the only instrument that had been catching them.

WHY contradicts() MISSED ALL THREE
-----------------------------------
It asks MEMBERSHIP — "is this glyph in the reading". A reading with one π
where the page has two passes. A reading that ignores an overprinted slash
passes, and passes doubly because `contradicts` deliberately exempts unmapped
glyphs (`test_an_unmapped_glyph_is_never_required_to_appear`, written by me
that morning).

`missing_glyphs()` counts instead. Measured:

    EQ0974   page has π x2, MathPix read x1; pix2tex read x2 and is RIGHT
    EQ0472   page has /arrownortheast x1 overprinted on an R
             (R's box 197.74-203.83 sits ENTIRELY inside the glyph's
             195.42-205.23, same baseline) — reading has it x0
    EQ0293   page has /floorright x4, reading has \rfloor x0 and
             \downharpoonleft x4

THE SCALE, IN ONE BOOK
----------------------
mielke rows where the page's `/floorright` count != the reading's `\rfloor`
count: 24 rows, 43 missing. 30 misread as `\downharpoonleft`, 13 dropped.
The interior product ⌋ is also read in this book as `\jmath`, `」`,
`\downarrow` and (correctly) `\rfloor` — five readings of one glyph.

A NAIVE VERSION OF THIS CHECK REPORTED 832 ROWS AND WAS WORTHLESS. Its top
hits were `/bracketleftbigg`, `/parenrightbigg`, `/summationdisplay` — all
CORRECT readings, since `\left[`, `\bigg)` and `\sum` are what a reading
legitimately writes for those. `missing_glyphs` therefore counts only names
`NAME_TOKENS` can turn into LaTeX, which keeps the signal and is why that
table must grow by demand rather than by inventory.

REPAIRED: 19 of 24, AND THE 5 REFUSALS ARE THE POINT
-----------------------------------------------------
`\downharpoonleft -> \rfloor` is a substitution and the census justifies it.
In 5 rows MathPix DROPPED the glyph instead of misreading it, so there is no
wrong token to replace: putting `\rfloor` back requires knowing WHERE, which
is placement, not substitution. The route declines and names them
(EQ0306, EQ0311, EQ0544, EQ0579, EQ0686). EQ0292 and EQ0294 are short by one
after repair — both failure modes in one equation.

TWO FIXES THE REPAIRS FORCED
-----------------------------
* `justifies()` counted token MULTIPLICITY, so EQ0293 — the same glyph
  misread four times — was refused as a "rewrite" for introducing `\rfloor`
  four times, while a genuine rewrite introducing three DIFFERENT tokens
  passed. It now counts DISTINCT tokens and adds a balance test: a
  substitution puts back what it takes away, n of X for n of Y.
* `contradicts()` stays membership-only and is now the weaker of the two
  checks; `missing_glyphs()` is the one that finds dropped symbols.

FOR INKDRILL, NEXT WEEK: THE ANGLE PROPERTY
--------------------------------------------
The user's own diagnosis, and the numbers support it exactly. ⌋ and ⇃ are
both "a vertical stroke plus a short second stroke". Their INK is nearly
identical, which is why component counts, hole counts and the ink distance
cannot separate them — and why 30 instances passed every measurement this
repository has. What differs is the ANGLE AT THE JUNCTION: ⌋ meets at 90°,
⇃ at an acute barb.

This matters beyond one symbol, because the census route only works on
born-digital pages. On a scan there is no text layer and no `/Encoding`, and
the angle is the only evidence available. The same property separates:

    ⌋ \rfloor        vs  ⇃ \downharpoonleft   90° vs acute barb
    / negationslash  vs  ↗ arrownortheast     stroke vs stroke + head
    + plus           vs  × times              0/90° vs 45°
    ⊥ perp           vs  ⊢ vdash              junction position

A SECOND GEOMETRIC PROPERTY, ALSO FROM THIS SESSION: BOUNDING-BOX
CONTAINMENT. A glyph whose x-extent encloses another glyph's on the same
baseline is an OVERPRINT, not a sequence — proved on EQ0472, and the same
book composes `⇐`+`⇒` into `⟺` the same way (MathPix reads that one
correctly). 13 rows corpus-wide carry an unaccounted slash-overlay glyph:
mielke 9 (arrownortheast), gilmore 2 and fong-spivak-invitation 2
(negationslash). That test needs no ink at all — the PDF gives exact boxes —
so it belongs in the census, and only the SCAN version needs inkdrill.

NOT DONE
--------
* The 5 dropped-glyph rows need placement, not substitution.
* EQ0472's slashed R is still unrepaired: `\cancel{R}` needs the cancel
  package in the preamble, which is a build change, not a reading change.
* The 13-row overlay class is measured, not fixed.
* mielke needs another rebuild + re-measure + republish to show these 19.

===========================================================================
682 FIX — a table entry seeded from memory, caught by the corpus
===========================================================================

`NAME_TOKENS["similarequal"]` shipped as `\cong`. It is `\simeq`: ≃ (U+2243)
and ≅ are different symbols, and I wrote the entry from recall rather than
from a row — in a table whose entire justification is that a lookup cannot
hallucinate the way a model can.

Measured, not argued: across the 25 mielke rows whose page carries
`/similarequal`, MathPix writes `\simeq` 29 times and `\cong` never. So all
29 "missing glyph" reports it produced were FALSE POSITIVES against readings
that were already correct.

The ground-truth manifest went 60 cases -> 36 when the entry was fixed. Had it
shipped, 29 of 60 "defects" in a corpus meant to score future work would have
been noise, and every one of them would have looked like a MathPix error.

The rule this earns: EVERY ENTRY IN NAME_TOKENS COMES FROM A ROW. Seven
entries, four of which a repair actually needed; the other three
(`floorleft`, `ceilingright`, `ceilingleft`) are mirrors of a verified one and
should be treated as unverified until a row uses them.
