255 — the contract, one level down: 24 fields, and two of them are ours
==============================================================================

WHY FIELDS ARE THE QUIETER LOSS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An unnamed TYPE drops a whole line, and a missing line is at least visible in a
projection. An unread FIELD drops a dimension of every line that carries it and
leaves the text intact, so nothing downstream looks wrong. `conversion_output`
sits on 3,465,341 lines, survives docpack round-trips perfectly, and is read by
nothing (out/252). `subtype` sat on 1,239,021 and was unread until 256, when it
turned out to hold the answer to a problem the code was solving with a
heuristic its own docstring apologised for.

THE FIVE YOU NAMED, AND WHAT EACH TURNED OUT TO BE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not one of them is the same kind of thing, which is the point of making the
contract name reasons rather than just listing exemptions.

  subtype (1,239,021)      — no longer a violation. 256 reads it.

  parent_id (2,031,812)    — redundant, and now VERIFIED rather than assumed.
                             Over 500 documents, 1,122,695 of 1,122,695 lines
                             with a parent_id are listed back in that parent's
                             children_ids. Exact inverses. Walking children_ids
                             downward loses nothing. I wrote the check because
                             "obviously redundant" is the shape of the three
                             wrong dead-code verdicts in out/237b.

  selected_labels (61,603) — opaque. Each value is a list of 32-hex MathPix
                             label ids, ['f883f3e2b61b46259fe0510affe81c0d'].
                             They resolve against nothing we hold. There is no
                             content behind them to lose.

  out_of_column (52,668)   — NOT A MATHPIX FIELD.
  margin_role   (52,668)   — NOT A MATHPIX FIELD.

The last two are the finding. `semantic.geometry_columns.tag_out_of_column`
stamps both onto page lines in place, and `commands.py` reads them back in the
same pass — pdfdrill writes them and pdfdrill reads them. They appear in
lines.json for 90 documents only because those files were re-saved after
tagging. The proof they are ours is that both sit on *exactly* the same 52,668
lines, and `margin_role`'s values come from pdfdrill's own
`classify_margin_item` enum — MathPix has no such vocabulary.

So a field contract that assumed "every field in lines.json is a MathPix field"
would have reported two of pdfdrill's own annotations as MathPix output we were
failing to read. The inventory's provenance block says this in as many words,
because the next person to regenerate it will not rediscover it.

THE TWO REAL GAPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  cnt (3,465,341)       A four-point polygon — the line's actual quadrilateral,
                        [[996,2714],[1078,2714],[1078,2750],[996,2750]] — where
                        `region` is only its axis-aligned bounding box. For
                        rotated or skewed lines the two differ, and every crop
                        we build uses the box. docpack packs `cnt` faithfully
                        and nothing reads it.

  font_size (3,463,072) A pixel height per line (31, 29, 28, 22…). Header
                        detection runs on text patterns alone. The signal that
                        would settle a heading from a bold run is on every line,
                        unread.

Neither is fixed here — 255 was scoped to build the contract, and both are now
declared gaps that a test will keep visible rather than notes in a report.

THE RULE THIS TASK RUNS ON
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every reason above was written after reading the field's values, not its name.
That is 253's lesson applied before the fact rather than after: `cnt` had to be
printed to know it was a polygon and not a count, `selected_labels` to know the
labels were opaque hashes and not text, `is_printed` to know it is constant to
within 8 lines in 1.37 million. A contract whose reasons are guesses is a
contract that certifies guesses.
