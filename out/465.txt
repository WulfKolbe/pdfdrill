THE DEFECT: the escaped CLOSING brace loses its backslash in 3 of 3 lines. The
escaped opening brace loses it in 1 of 3. A space after a backslash is closed
up twice. On a page whose entire subject is escaping braces in TeX, the escapes
are what goes missing.

It is not a misreading of a symbol — the model classified a typewriter code
listing as mathematics and then normalised it as if it were. That is a
mechanism, which is why it is worth sending: the two low confidences (0.468,
0.405) say the model knew something was wrong on those lines, and 0.891 on the
first says it did not.

Line 1 reaches our markdown as ONE $...$ span (.md line 5281). Our inline-math
extractor then split it at the $ characters INSIDE the listing — which are
literal typewriter characters, not delimiters — into FO0317 / FO0318 ('and') /
FO0319. Line 2 (.md 5294) split the same way into FO0320 / FO0321 / FO0322.
FO0320 and FO0318 and FO0321 happen to be balanced, so they render and never
showed up. Only FO0323 is a whole MathPix line.

So: three of the four unresolved rows are a pdfdrill defect in the inline-math
tokenizer, one is MathPix's. The pdfdrill half goes on our list, not theirs.
