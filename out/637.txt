THE SHAPE, READ OFF THE DATA
  MathPix hangs a page's footnote text off a `footnote` PARENT line as CHILD
  `text` lines. `FootnoteProcessor` reads the parent. `ParagraphProcessor`
  builds a Paragraph over the children, and
  `heading_cleanup.extract_footnote_paragraphs` reads the `\footnotetext{…}`
  MathPix left in THAT. So the two objects for one footnote NEVER SHARE A
  LINE — which is why 636 could report `Footnote+Footnote = 0` while the page
  foot printed everything twice, and why plain extent overlap identifies only
  20 of the 45 pairs. Expanding a `footnote` line to its own children
  identifies ALL 45 and joins nothing else: the 3 footnotes with one object
  stay single.

THE RULE — EXACT, ALL THREE CONDITIONS (src/docmodel/footnote_extent.py)
  A Footnote already in the document IS this footnote when
      (1) `page` is equal, AND
      (2) `refnum` is equal and non-empty, AND
      (3) its extent overlaps this body's extent once a `footnote` line has
          been grown to its own children.
  `refnum` alone is not a key (16 penev_A refnums name more than one
  Footnote — 638); `(page, refnum)` alone is not either (644's within-page
  collision). Where the body's lines were NOT located the extent is empty and
  NOTHING IS ADOPTED — an adoption on (page, refnum) alone would be a guess,
  and 638 refuses the same guess for a marker. The expansion is applied ONLY
  to a line MathPix typed `footnote`: growing a prose line to its children
  would let a paragraph swallow whatever MathPix hung off it.

WHAT ADOPTION DOES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
  The cleanup fills the existing Footnote and creates no second object:
    * `content` AND the `cleaned` realization both take the cleaned body — a
      fresh prop beside a stale realization is the "both reach the output"
      defect in another spelling;
    * the extent this pass located is ADDED, so the lines the cleanup claimed
      stay claimed (dropping them would report lines as covered by nobody
      while their text sits in a Footnote — the 193-false-unclaimed trade 636
      already refused);
    * `filled_by: "footnote_cleanup"` records who filled it.
  The host keeps its ID (every marker and transclusion pointing at it
  survives) and its own flow position — it is anchored on MathPix's footnote
  line, which is where the footnote is.

  WHICH BODY WINS IS THE RULING'S, NOT A PREFERENCE: the CLEANED one. It is
  measurably better on the two things that matter to the projection — its
  citations resolve (`\cite{Deco1996}` rather than the literal "(Deco and
  Obradovic 1996)") and it names more of its own maths (29 of the folded
  Formulas against 23 for the surface body) — and measurably worse on
  whitespace (15 doubled spaces, one split word). Both halves are above.

THE THIRD COPY — THE MATHS (src/docops/projectors/footnotes.body_math_ids)
  A Formula whose lines lie ENTIRELY inside a Footnote's extent AND whose
  latex the body actually carries is folded into the body: emitted once,
  inside the `\footnotetext`, never again standalone.
  'INSIDE A BODY **AND** NAMED BY IT', never 'inside a body'. penev_A's
  page-6 block has a `\footnotetext{` whose braces never balance, so its
  Footnote's content is "" (636-a) while 15 Formulas sit on its lines:
  skipping those would be pure loss with nothing to show it. They are left,
  and the difference is the whole reason the rule has two clauses.
  ONLY `Formula` — not `Equation`. An Equation carries a `\label` that `\ref`
  resolves against, and folding one away would break every cross-reference to
  it. Stated in `BODY_MATH_TYPES`, not assumed.

CONCERNS
  The ruling names the cleaned body and the cleaned body is not uniformly
  better. It resolves its citations and names more of its own maths; it also
  carries the paragraph pipeline's whitespace damage. Both halves are
  measured above rather than one of them being quietly preferred.
  `body_math_ids` compares a Formula's `latex` to the body text as a
  SUBSTRING. That is exact for the 29 it folds and it is why the 15 formulas
  of the empty-body block are left; it would miss a body whose maths the
  paragraph pipeline had reflowed, and such a formula is emitted standalone —
  the safe direction, and not counted separately.
  `footnote_adopted` accumulates in `doc.meta` across runs, like
  `footnote_orphan_tail`. `cmd_clean` prints it only when that run lifted
  something; `status` and `conserve` print the total, which is the model's
  total and not the run's.
  Both documents' models were rebuilt FOUR times between them, always from
  CACHED MathPix output. No extraction was re-purchased and `mathpix --force`
  was never run.
