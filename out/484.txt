STIX2 IS NOT THERE, AND CANNOT BE
---------------------------------
482 recommended stix2 for `\overparen` and `\oiint`. That was wrong, and the
compiler says so in one line:

  ! LaTeX Error: Too many symbol fonts declared.

amssymb + bbm + mathrsfs + stmaryrd already spend TeX's sixteen math
families. Adding stix2 produced 100 errors. So the two commands it would have
supplied go in as `\providecommand` instead — the technique 442 used for
`\longdiv`, and the reason it is the right one here is that a macro costs no
font family. `\Perp` is a `\providecommand` for a different reason: no
package defines it, and it spans 10 documents.

A test asserts stix2 is absent AND that adding it really does exhaust the
symbol fonts, so the reason is checked rather than remembered.

WHAT 482 SAID AND WHAT THIS FOUND
---------------------------------
482 sized the package-fixable population at 536 occurrences over 17 commands
and named four packages. Two corrections come out of actually doing it:

  stix2 is impossible, for a reason no census could see — it is a property of
  the preamble it would join, not of the commands it defines.

  `cancel` earns its place (10 occurrences) and `esint`, `wasysym`, `mathabx`,
  `tipa` and `esvect` do not: every command they would have supplied is either
  covered by the three `\providecommand`s or occurs once or twice. Four
  packages, not ten.

And 482's larger finding stands untouched: 11,088 of the 11,624 undefined
occurrences are the source document's own macros, 4,180 of them PSTricks from
a single book. Nothing here touches those, and nothing should — `injectlatex`
is the route for them, and out/056's 286 note already measured that it can
make things worse.
