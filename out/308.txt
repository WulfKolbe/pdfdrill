308 - \right. the invisible right delimiter
====================================================================

  \right. prints NOTHING. `\left( x \right.` sets an opening parenthesis
  with no closing one: legal, compiles, and to any rule that COUNTS
  delimiters it is balanced - \left and \right are matched. The defect is
  invisible to every check that counts rather than looks.

  The construct has legitimate uses - a one-sided brace, a fence carried
  across a line break - so its presence is not by itself a defect. Whether
  the page shows a delimiter is a question about the SCAN and is not
  decided here. This is the count and the openers.
