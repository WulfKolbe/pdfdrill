READING THESE
=============

\ref — 33,624 calls corpus-wide against 23,678 \label targets.

  A Reference object per call is what LATW's InlineElementScanner emits
  ({{key||REF}}); pdfdrill has zero `type="Reference"` on the source path and
  a \ref survives only as characters inside a Paragraph. So a cross-reference
  is currently unresolvable and uncountable: nothing can answer "what points
  at this theorem" without re-parsing the prose.

  33,624 is also a warning about the shape of the fix. That is more objects
  than the corpus has Equations, and most carry no information beyond a label
  string. The useful unit is arguably the EDGE (this paragraph -> that label),
  not an object per call, and 23,678 labels is the count of things that can
  actually be pointed AT. Emitting 33,624 objects because LATW does would be
  porting a decision rather than a capability.

\includegraphics — 4,529 orphans of 5,105 corpus-wide, in 380 of 459 documents.

  An "orphan" is an inclusion that is not inside a tikzpicture, tabular or
  other graphic block, so extract_graphics never returns it and NOTHING is
  emitted for it. 89% of all author inclusions are in that state, and 83% of
  documents contain at least one. That is the class the task named, and it is
  larger than the class that IS handled by a factor of eight.

  A figure that is only \includegraphics{figures/ch2/graph} produces no
  object, no SVG, no tiddler and no report row. It is not degraded — it is
  absent, and nothing says so.

THE THESIS IS THE EXCEPTION, WHICH IS WHY THIS WAS NOT OBVIOUS
==============================================================

  thesis:  85 of 100 inclusions sit inside a graphic block, 15 orphan
  corpus:  576 of 5,105 do,                                4,529 orphan

  The thesis wraps nearly every figure in a tikzpicture to annotate it — the
  node-placed style 335 counted at 239 across 11 documents, 85 of them here.
  So the document this work has been aimed at is the one document where the
  orphan class barely appears: 15%, against 89% everywhere else.

  Measuring coverage on it would have said the gap was small. That is the
  same shape as 348's reason for existing: a corpus reports the constructs it
  happens to contain, and one document reports its own habits.
