519 — compare.html documented as the sixth artefact
===================================================

Written into docs/layers/ARTEFACTS.md (§ `compare.html`, and two new rows
in "Which artefact answers which question"). The file's title changes from
"The four HTML/PDF artefacts" to "The six" — it had already grown to five
with corrections.html in 510 and the heading was never updated.

WHAT IT SHOWS
-------------
`pdfdrill compare` -> `docops.projectors.comparison_html`. One row per
display equation carrying a MathPix crop. Baseline three columns:

  LaTeX (mathpix) · KaTeX (mathpix) · MathPix image

and then, per COMPETING reading the object carries, another LaTeX + KaTeX
pair with that candidate's score. Competing readings are realizations
whose provenance is not MathPix's: `tex` (the author's e-print, put there
by `injectlatex`), `snip` (/v3/text), `llm`.

WHO IT IS FOR
-------------
Someone deciding which of two readings is right. That is the whole of it,
and it is why the artefact only earns its place when a second reading
exists.

WHY 6 OF 21 — AND THE REASON IS NOT A GOOD ONE
-----------------------------------------------
Measured, not assumed. The six are johnston, lyche, mielke, penev_A,
penev_B, voloshin. Their column headers:

  penev_A, penev_B   #·ref·p·score·LaTeX(mathpix)·KaTeX(mathpix)·
                     LaTeX(tex)·KaTeX(tex)·MathPix image      9 columns
  the other four     #·ref·p·LaTeX(mathpix)·KaTeX(mathpix)·
                     MathPix image                            6 columns

So on FOUR of the six there is no second reading and the file degenerates
to LaTeX source + rendered maths + crop — which is exactly what
formula-report.html already shows. On those four it is a duplicate.

The model confirms it:

  penev_A               111 realizations of provenance `tex`
  johnston              0   (only `change` 8, `bibliography` 3)
  voloshin-hypergraph   0   (only `change` 3, `bibliography` 8)

  documents                        compare.html shows          count
  penev_A, penev_B                 a real comparison               2
  johnston, lyche, mielke, voloshin the baseline only               4
  the other fifteen                nothing — never built           15

Corpus-wide: 27 of 2,818 folders carry compare.html.

BUILD FOR ALL 21, OR NOT
-------------------------
NOT — and the reason is stateable, which is what the task asked for.

ALL 21 HAVE A tex.zip. The second reading is available everywhere and
injected almost nowhere; what separates penev_A/B from the rest is that
`injectlatex` was run on them, not any property of the documents. The
present subset records which command happened to be run.

Building it for all 21 would produce fifteen more files with the baseline
three columns — fifteen more duplicates of formula-report.html. That is
the same shape as the dangling "see tables.html" pointer: an artefact
whose presence implies a comparison that is not in it.

So compare.html belongs to a subset, but a DIFFERENT subset than the one
that exists: documents carrying a second reading. That set has two
members today. The fix is to inject the second reading (`injectlatex`,
which is free — every one of the 21 has its e-print), not to emit the
empty comparison. Until then the four baseline-only files stay and the
documentation says in terms that they are not to be read as comparisons.

That is a decision, not a deferral: nothing is built for the 15, and the
reason is now written next to the artefact instead of being inferable
only from which files exist.

HOW IT DIFFERS FROM report.pdf's Corrected PAIRS
-------------------------------------------------
Both put two readings against one scan.

                    compare.html              report.pdf Corrected
  second reading    a CANDIDATE, unjudged     the ACCEPTED correction
  rows              every equation with a     only corrected ones
                    crop (johnston: 3,332)    (johnston: 8)
  the reader        decides which is right    sees what was decided,
                                              and on what basis
  carries a score   yes, per candidate        no — basis + ink
                                              before/after

compare.html is read BEFORE a decision; the Corrected section is the
record AFTER one. A row leaves the first and enters the second by being
accepted, which is why 509's section is short and this file is long.

NOT DONE
--------
`injectlatex` was not run on the other 19. It is free and offline and
would turn compare.html into what it is for on all of them — but it
writes to the model, and running it across the published set unasked is
a change to 19 documents, not a report.
