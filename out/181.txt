The confidence bands always apply, so the legend is always printed. Where the
residual half has nothing to describe, only that half is omitted, and its

   measured    Conf. MathPix confidence: ■ ≥0.9  ■ 0.5–0.9  ■ <0.5  --- no value
               Residual render vs scan (inkdrill): ● C component  ● W weak
               ● S stable  ● N noise  ● K clean  ● not measured

   unmeasured  Conf. MathPix confidence: ■ ≥0.9  ■ 0.5–0.9  ■ <0.5  --- no value
               Residual not shown for this document — no residual measurements
               are paired with these rows.

WHY SAYING IT MATTERS MORE THAN OMITTING IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The previous behaviour dropped the residual line silently when there was
nothing to show. A key that lists only what is present LOOKS COMPLETE. A
reader with one report in front of them has no way to know that a second
column exists on other documents in the same collection and is missing from
this one — the legend reads as "this report has one column", not as "this
report is missing a column".

Those are different statements and only one of them is true. This is the same
shape as the seven documents whose legend promised a reading they did not
carry, inverted: there the key over-claimed, here it under-claimed, and in
both cases the reader could not tell from the page.

BOTH FOOTER HOOKS ARE PRESERVED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The legend is emitted as \endfoot AND \endlastfoot, so it repeats on every
page including the last — 138 established that one alone leaves exactly the
complementary set of pages bare. Verified on the rebuilt 0902.0431 report:
2 of each, and the confidence bands appear 5 times across the document.

--no-legend IS UNCHANGED AND STILL NECESSARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Worth stating because "print the legend on every report" and "--no-legend"
look contradictory. They are not: --no-legend exists for the MEASUREMENT pass,
not for reading. 158 established that the legend's \multicolumn footer is read
by the ink lattice as a data row with empty Rendered/Scan cells, scoring a
false "clean" and displacing the last real row on its page. The ink pass
therefore measures a legend-free build and the reader always gets the legend
one. No report anyone reads is built without it.
