THE SOURCE CROSS-CHECK, WHICH IS WHY IT IS THERE
--------------------------------------------------
`tex_declares` reads the author's own `\tableofcontents` and
`\bibliography` where a source exists. It corroborates rather than
replaces: a heading scan finding nothing where the source declares a
bibliography is a DETECTION FAILURE, not a document without references.

  sources declaring a bibliography            3
  of those, headings found none               1   — 1605.05775

1605.05775 is the case the cross-check exists to surface: its source
declares a bibliography and its headings show none, so its 0 above is a
miss rather than an absence. It is reported, not patched around.

THE ANCHORING MATTERS AND IS TESTED
-------------------------------------
The patterns match a whole heading with an optional leading number, never a
substring:

  "Index Theorems"                         -> body, not index
  "die betreffende Literatur als Metapher" -> body, not bibliography
  "5 Bibliography"                         -> bibliography

Three tests hold it, plus one that a heading ENDS the previous region — a
bibliography runs to the next heading, not to the end of the document — and
one that `page_info` is never a heading, which is the German case above.

Tests: 2,935 passing.
