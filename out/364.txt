READER CHANGES

    `declared_splits` reads dataset_info.json and prints it first.

    and falling back to `open_file` on ArrowInvalid, since HF writes either
    depending on version. Memory-mapped, so a 500 MB shard is not read into
    RAM and a limit of 100 touches only the first shard.
