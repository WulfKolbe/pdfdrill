THE PART WORTH NOTING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This equation is NOT unnumbered. `refnum` = 204 and `refnum_anchor` is set, so
the pairing in equation.py `_match_equation_numbers` succeeded — there is also
a separate `equation_number` line on that page which it matched. So the number
is in the model twice: once correctly as the refnum, and once as three
characters of the equation's own body.

That is why this case matters more than a cosmetic blemish. The refnum is
right, so nothing downstream flags the equation as defective, while the LaTeX
that gets rendered, scored and published carries a stray `.(204)` that is not
mathematics. It renders. It compiles. It is simply wrong, and every check that
looks at the refnum says the equation is fine.

POPULATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

above.

The looser pattern — any `(\d{1,4}(\.\d+)?)` anywhere inside a maths value —
matches 4,111 lines, but that population is dominated by legitimate mathematics:

contamination, so the loose count is not a defect count and should not be
quoted as one. Distinguishing the two reliably needs the position test — the
number trailing the body after a closing delimiter, as here — not the presence
