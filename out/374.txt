MARKED IN THE MANIFEST

  datikz-fixture/manifest.json now carries a `contamination` block naming
  the counts and pointing at out/371.txt and out/374.json, and any row that
  is one of the 92 carries `in_v2_test: true`.

  NONE of the fixture's own 100 rows is one of the 92. That is worth stating
  rather than leaving as an empty field: the flag is present and false
  everywhere, so a reader does not have to wonder whether it was applied.

ON THE REPORT PAGE

  The caveat gained a Split paragraph, held by
  test_the_caveat_records_the_split_and_the_contamination:

    "These rows are V4 train. 92 of DaTikZ-v2's 442 test rows appear in
     V4's training data BY PICTURE — the same tikzpicture body — though not
     by document, because V4 rewrites every wrapper to standalone and a
     whole-file hash therefore finds none of them. Neither release page
     states this. 350 V2 test rows are clean; a model measurement must use
     those, and this report is a renderer measurement where it does not
     matter."

  A reader cannot tell a training row from a held-out one by looking at the
  figure. That is the whole reason the sentence has to be on the page rather
  than in a commit message.
