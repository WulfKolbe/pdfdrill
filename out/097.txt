WHY SO FEW MOVED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Measured rather than assumed, before running: of the 725, only 11 contain
   a non-ASCII character at all (10 are e-acute, which always set correctly),
   and only 2 equations in the corpus were mis-split by the tail rule. The
   maximum this pass could resolve was small and known in advance.

   The rest were flagged for reasons no glyph or split fix addresses -
   delimiter auto-sizing, \frac against \dfrac, line and column spacing, and
   the resolution difference that opens letter counters in the render.

A REGRESSION I INTRODUCED AND THEN REMOVED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The first run of this compare returned 723, with 6 rows made WORSE by 1-2
   units. All were e-acute rows in 1205.3463v2. My 090 fallback wrapped every
   character the mono font covers in \ifmmode\text{}, which moved e-acute out
   of the maths font although it had never been in danger. A rescue that
   touches characters that were never at risk is a regression, and it is now
   restricted to code points above U+00FF.

   5 rows still read 1 unit higher than in out/066. Their preambles are now
   empty - no declaration is emitted for them - so nothing about how they are
   typeset has changed. The movement is re-render variation between two
   invocations of the same pipeline, which is exactly the quantity the noise
   floor of 6 was measured to bound. It is reported rather than smoothed over
   because a difference with no cause is worth naming as such.
