Two independent cross-checks, neither needing a browser:

   * poppler's pdftotext returns `C|+76` twice on page 1 — once from the 148
     selectable type, once from the appearance stream. Poppler is reading the
     glyphs out of the /AP.

     count unchanged (2 before, 2 after). Nothing was waiting to be generated.
