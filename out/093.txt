MATHPIX DID NOT TRUNCATE THIS. WE DID.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   This reverses what out/070 concluded. The lines.json text above is
   COMPLETE: it has the leading "Pack", all three arrows, and the trailing
   "-complete." The loss happened inside our own ingest, and the model keeps
   enough intermediate state to see exactly where:

     latex_raw       \[ \text { Pack }_{<\omega} ... \text {-complete. } \]
                     complete, as received

     latex_pretail   \text { Pack }_{<\omega} ... \text {-complete. }
                     display delimiters removed, content still complete

     latex           _{<\omega} ... \text { pack }_{2}
                     leading "\text { Pack }" GONE, trailing
                     "\text {-complete. }" GONE

   The damage is between latex_pretail and latex - the tail-split step
   (mathqc.text_tail, task P7). On a line that is entirely prose with
   subscripts, it strips the leading and trailing \text{} runs as if they were
   trailing commentary, and here they are the subject of the sentence.

   So the correct statement, replacing out/070's: MathPix read this line
   correctly at confidence 0.916. Our normalisation removed two thirds of its
   words, and the report then displayed the mutilated version.

WHAT TO SEND
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Nothing needs sending to MathPix for this row. The reading is right.

   The bug is ours and it is specific: text_tail must not strip a \text{} run
   that is bounded by other \text{} runs, or in general must not strip when
   what remains is not a mathematical expression. The affected class is math
   lines that are really prose - implication chains, "where X denotes Y" -
   which is exactly the class 020-022 were about.

   Not fixed here: out/093 was asked to resolve the identifier and produce the
   line, and the fix belongs with a measurement of how many other rows
   text_tail truncates the same way.
