DISAGREEMENT WITH THE BRIEF
-------------------------------------------
The brief states 0902.0431 has "5 of 1,219" refused equations, "four" nested-
array-strip and "one" brace-macro. I loaded model.docmodel.json and ran
`renderable()` over all 1,219 Equation objects directly (not re-deriving the
report-build pipeline, just the gate function) and got 7 refusals, not 5:
  - 5 non-CJK refusals, and ALL FIVE, not four, fail via the identical
    mechanism: `has_bare_align_marker` returns True on the row surviving the
    item-3 non-greedy strip loop (obj_bb583b24e12c, obj_2612f0c6637d,
    obj_a9d4871a3b4b, obj_ad5deeab2cb1, obj_3fbf7b74e056). I found ZERO rows
    refused by the brace-macro mechanism, because that mechanism is not
    broken here (item 4, confirmed present and general — see above).
  - obj_3fbf7b74e056 DOES contain a `\stackrel` (inside `\xrightarrow{\stackrel
    {f^{-1}}{\longrightarrow}}`), which is presumably why the brief counted
    it as the "one brace-macro" case — but I tested directly: removing the
    `\stackrel`/`\xrightarrow` wrapper and leaving the nested arrays intact,
    the row is STILL refused (`renderable()` still returns ""). The `\stackrel`
    is not the cause; the nested `\begin{array}` inside `\begin{array}` in the
    same value is. So this row belongs entirely to the nested-array class,
    not a distinct brace-macro class, on this document.
  - 2 further refusals (obj_128e57e0e8d7, obj_57f7fb159a25) are CORRECTLY
    refused by `cjk_defect` — both carry ideographic description characters
    (U+2FF1, U+2FFB) — and are not a gate bug at all; they are the CJK gate
    working as designed. The brief's "5" appears to exclude these
    (consistent framing, not a contradiction), but its own total of 7 vs. 5
    was not stated, and the 4-vs-1 split inside the 5 does not match what I
    measured: it is 5-vs-0 here, with the sole `\stackrel`-bearing row
    misattributable to the wrong class if read at a glance.
  This does not overturn the brief's core claim (the nested-array-strip
  defect is real, confirmed, and dominant on this document) — it sharpens
  it: on 0902.0431 specifically, the brace-macro defect class the auditor
  measured on OmniDocBench (28 clean formulas demoted) does not manifest at
  all, because item 4 is already fixed here, more generally than the named
  list, under an unrelated bug number from before the CRxz session.
