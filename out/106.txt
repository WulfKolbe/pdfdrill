WHAT THIS PAIR DEMONSTRATES ABOUT THE VALIDATOR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Both proposals have uniform row widths. Both are well-formed tables. One is
   right and one is wrong, and 105a cannot tell them apart, by construction:
   width uniformity is a claim about SHAPE and says nothing about VALUES.

   That is not a gap in the validator - it is the division of labour. The
   validator rejects proposals that cannot be right; the ink measures whether
   a well-formed one IS right. Running them in that order is what makes the
   second test meaningful, because a malformed proposal renders to a plausible
   artifact and then the ink comparison is measuring a typesetting accident.
