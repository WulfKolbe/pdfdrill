TWO COUNTERS, TWO MEANINGS - STATED BECAUSE THEY DISAGREE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The command's own summary line reports 5 demoted for the whole corpus;
   counting '(not rendered)' in the regenerated report.tex files gives 51.
   Both are right and they measure different things:

        5  demoted by the COMPILE FIXPOINT — the row reached TeX, TeX
           errored, the row was replaced and the document recompiled
       46  rejected by renderable() at GENERATION time — the value never
           reached TeX at all, so no compile counter can see it
       51  total rows showing '(not rendered)' to a reader

   A reader of the summary line alone would conclude the corpus has 5
   unrenderable equations. It has 51. The generation-time rejections are
   the larger half and the silent one.
