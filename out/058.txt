TWO COUNTERS, TWO MEANINGS - STATED BECAUSE THEY DISAGREE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The command's own summary line reports 5 demoted for the whole corpus;
   counting '(not rendered)' in the regenerated report.tex files gives 51.
   Both are right and they measure different things:

        5  demoted by the COMPILE FIXPOINT — the row reached TeX, TeX
           errored, the row was replaced and the document recompiled
       46  rejected by renderable() at GENERATION time — the value never
           reached TeX at all, so no compile counter can see it
       51  total rows showing '(not rendered)' to a reader

   A reader of the summary line alone would conclude the corpus has 5
   unrenderable equations. It has 51. The generation-time rejections are
   the larger half and the silent one.

ADDENDUM - THREE NUMBERS FOR THE SAME QUANTITY, AND WHICH POPULATION EACH IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Reconciled against the consumer's independent scan; every figure below is
   reproduced on both sides. They differ only by population, and stating the
   population is the whole point:

      93  all report.tex in the library, all row kinds, 14 documents
      72  arXiv only (drops the 3 Heim books: BH1org_OCR 11, bh2 9, BH3FR 1)
      51  arXiv, dropping 1511.08771 (21 rows) - the 058 corpus, 10 documents
      49  the same, EQ rows only - the consumer's operative exclusion set

   The 2-row gap between 51 and 49 is one FO row each in 1205.5935v1 and
   1411.5002. The consumer's compare consumes EQ rows only, so 49 is the
   number that governs their measurement; 51 is the number a reader of a
   report.pdf would count.

   Coincidence worth naming so nobody builds on it: the three Heim books
   total 21 rows and 1511.08771 alone totals 21 rows. Two subtractions of
   the same size for unrelated reasons. 93-21=72 and 72-21=51 both hold and
   neither implies the other.

   Counting these rows needs the brace-aware identifier pattern
   \\ident\{([^}]*(?:\}[^}]*)*?)\} - breakable_ident inserts \allowbreak{}
   after each dot, so a plain [^}]* truncates every identifier at the first
   brace and classifies all 93 rows as "other". That is out/056's identifier
   regex defect a second time, from a different cause.

CONCENTRATION - A PROPERTY OF ONE DOCUMENT, NOT A CORPUS RATE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   0902.0431 supplies 31 of the 51 remaining rows: 61% of all row kinds,
   63% of the 49 EQ rows. Every one is the nested \[ \] validator class.

   So "the corpus has a 51-row residue" and "one document has a 31-row
   defect and the other nine have 20 between them" describe the same data,
   and only the second is actionable. Any downstream claim about the
   remaining demotion rate should carry the document name, or exclude
   0902.0431 and say so.
