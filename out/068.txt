068 - a shaded-background test, not an exclusion by name
==============================================================================

ANSWER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   8 of the 725 come from a document with shaded equation boxes.
   3 of the 725 are themselves shaded crops.

   Both numbers are the same document, 1211.3375, and it is the ONLY one:
   all 4,342 corpus crops were scanned, and exactly 3 are shaded.

     1211.3375    3 of its 23 crops shaded (13%)
     the other 48 documents   0

WHICH WAS BUILT, AND WHY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The task offered either. A detector was built, because excluding one
   document by name only postpones the problem - the next shaded document
   arrives as the same surprise, and nothing in the pipeline would announce
   it. A test fires on any document, including ones not yet drilled.

   src/pdfdrill/shading.py, five tests.

THE TEST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Share of pixels that are MID-GREY - luminance in [150, 245], neither
   paper (>245) nor ink (<150). auto_mask thresholds at 200, so a box
   interior in that band is exactly what fragments and hands the scan
   components the render has no counterpart for.

   The threshold was measured, not chosen:

       shaded crops        0.884 .. 0.942
       every other crop    0.000 .. 0.225   (highest: 1304.0071v3)
       cut at              0.500

   The two populations are separated by a factor of four with nothing at all
   in between. The cut sits in a void, not at the edge of either - so it is
   not sensitive to where exactly it is placed, which is the only kind of
   threshold worth trusting.

   It fires per CROP, not per document, and that distinction carries weight
   here: five of 1211.3375's eight hits are NOT shaded and score 6, 7, 7, 8
   and 8 ink, while its three shaded ones score 128, 181 and 213. Excluding
   the document wholesale would have discarded five sound measurements to
   remove three bad ones.

CORROBORATION THE DETECTOR WAS NOT TUNED FOR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The detector reads only the background histogram. It never sees the ink
   distance. Yet the three crops it flags are precisely the three largest
   ink values in that document, and its five clean crops are precisely the
   five smallest:

       EQ0015   ink 213   midgrey 0.942   SHADED
       EQ0016   ink 181   midgrey 0.911   SHADED
       EQ0011   ink 128   midgrey 0.884   SHADED
       EQ0019   ink   8   midgrey 0.081   clean
       EQ0022   ink   8   midgrey 0.042   clean
       EQ0002   ink   7   midgrey 0.055   clean
       EQ0014   ink   7   midgrey 0.060   clean
       EQ0021   ink   6   midgrey 0.057   clean

   A clean split at 128 vs 8, from a measurement that had no access to the
   quantity it split.

EFFECT ON THE SYMBOL-HALF EXPERIMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   067 flagged that three of the eight rows the symbol half would promote
   are 1211.3375's. All three are now identified as shaded and can be
   labelled artifact rather than removed by document name - 1211.3375's
   other five rows stay in the experiment, where they belong.

   The remaining 722 hits carry no shading. Whatever the symbol half
   surfaces among them is not this artifact, which is what makes 069's
   ranking readable.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT COVER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   One artifact, one corpus. A halftone or a scanned photographic
   background would also land in the mid-grey band and would be flagged the
   same way, correctly. A coloured box converted to a near-white grey, or a
   very light tint above 245, would NOT be caught - the detector would call
   it clean, and it would be. Nothing here claims to find every way a crop
   can mislead the mask; it finds this one, with a stated margin.
