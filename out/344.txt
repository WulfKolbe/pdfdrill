344 - 337's method does not have 106 rows to run on. It has four.
====================================================================

WHY, and it is not the model or the prompt

  337's method needs BOTH images: the base file and the crop of how that
  figure appears on the page. The addition is the difference between them,
  so one image cannot show it.

  The 106 around rows carry 106 base files. They do not carry 106 crops.

THE POPULATION, narrowed twice

  106 around rows sit in FOUR folders:
    2004.05631                               44
    2004.05631v1                             44
    2012.15581                               16
    2508.19773                               2

  2004.05631 and 2004.05631v1 are the SAME document in two folders, and
  only v1 holds the tex.zip. So 44 of the 106 are duplicates of another
  44, and the distinct population is 62, not 106.

  Of those 62, pairing a base to its crop by page (339's method):

    document          around  UNIQUE  contested  ambiguous  no crop
    2004.05631v1          44       4          0         39        1
    2012.15581            16       0         12          4        0
    2508.19773             2       0          2          0        0
    ----------------------------------------------------------------
    total                 62       4         14         43        1

  FOUR rows have a unique crop. 43 sit on pages carrying several crops;
  14 are contested, where several bases claim one crop and neither can be
  assigned without choosing -- 301's defect and 339's.

AND THE FOUR ARE THE FOUR 337 ALREADY MEASURED
    figures/ch4/mpsapprox2.jpg
    figures/ch3/rho_xi.jpg
    figures/ch1/bothways.jpg
    figures/ch2/pt2.jpg

  mpsapprox2, rho_xi, bothways, pt2 -- exactly 337's four `around` rows.
  There is no new measurement to make here.

SO: WERE THE SEVEN FAVOURABLE OR REPRESENTATIVE?

  Neither. They were EXHAUSTIVE. Seven pairs is not a sample drawn from a
  larger set that might have been kinder -- it is every row in the corpus
  where a base, a crop and an author reference can all three be named
  without guessing. The distribution cannot be compared against a wider
  one because a wider one does not exist yet.

  That is a better answer than a number would have been, and it names the
  bottleneck precisely: it is not the model, the prompt, the position
  statement or the ink metric. It is the crop pairing. 337 measured the
  around population at a median ink distance of 10 against 180, on all
  four rows that exist.

WHAT WOULD UNLOCK THE OTHER 58

  43 ambiguous rows need the page's several crops told apart -- by
  rectangle, which needs each inclusion's own placement, or by hand.
  14 contested rows need a person to say which base is which crop.
  That is exactly 340's sidecar, and this quantifies the case for it:
  without it the measurable population is 4, with it it is 62.

A BUG FOUND ON THE WAY, worth recording because the first answer was 0

  in BOTH texzip/ and texsrc/, and I globbed both, so every crop was
  counted twice and every page looked ambiguous. Deduplicating by filename
  gives 4. A count of zero from a doubled population is not a finding.
