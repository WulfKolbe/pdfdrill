SO YES, B NEEDS A PROSE SECTION
--------------------------------
It is not a nicety. Nine maths items in ten are inline, and today they are
reachable in exactly one artefact.

And the confidence point is right and forced: **FO tiddlers carry no
confidence and no page.** Measured on 2010.14265 — 369 FO tiddlers, 0 with
a confidence, 0 with a page. Their fields are title, latex, text, caption,
displayMode, trailing_punct, tags, type. Nothing to rank or filter on.

The host line HAS one. Every MathPix line carries `confidence` and
`confidence_rate`, so an inline formula can inherit its parent line's — the
only confidence it will ever have, exactly as you put it. That also gives
the prose section a page, which the FO row lacks, because the line has a
region.

A consequence worth stating for 523: a quarter of inline maths is inside
table cells. B's Tables section and B's prose section are not disjoint, and
501's rule — a table cell's content is a cell, not a Formula — decides
which one shows those 367,516 spans.
