WHAT THE TEN SAY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   NOT ONE of the ten is a content error. Every symbol, index, sign and
   subscript matches the page. Nine are typography - delimiter auto-sizing,
   \frac against \dfrac, line and column spacing - and the tenth is a grey
   background defeating the mask.

   That is a finding about the RANKING, not a failure of the list. Sorting
   725 rows by confidence and breaking ties by ink puts the largest pixel
   disagreements on top, and the largest pixel disagreements in a
   confidently-read corpus are typographic. A tall \left( changes many more
   pixels than a wrong digit does.

   For contrast: 0902.0431_EQ0516, which out/063 proved has 19 of 48 array
   cells wrong or missing, scores ink 18. It sits far down this same list,
   below every one of these ten.

THE SEPARATION THAT DOES WORK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The five-tuple splits cleanly in two:

     symbol features  components, holes   - how many marks, with what shape
     layout features  stacked, centred, offset - where they sit

   A typographic difference moves the layout features and leaves the symbol
   features alone. A misread symbol moves the symbol features. EQ0516's
   delta is components 12, holes 6, stacked 0, centred 0, offset 0 - pure
   symbol. These ten are the mirror image.

   Over all 725 hits:

       458  (63%)  layout-dominated   |stk|+|ctr|+|off| > |comp|+|holes|
       267  (37%)  symbol-dominated

   and 9 of the top 10 are layout-dominated. Ranking the same 725 rows by
   the SYMBOL half instead of by total L1 would put content candidates on
   top. That is a one-line change to the sort key, and it is the obvious
   next experiment rather than something to assert here - the eight rows it
   promotes have not been inspected, and three of them are 1211.3375, the
   grey-background document, so the artifact would ride up with them.

CAVEAT WORTH CARRYING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Shaded backgrounds break the mask. 1211.3375 puts its displayed equations
   in grey boxes, and all three of its high-ink rows are that. Before the
   symbol half is used as a content filter it needs a background test,
   otherwise one document's styling will dominate the top of the list for a
   reason that has nothing to do with mathematics.
