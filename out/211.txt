A NOTE ON HOW THE CROPS WERE MADE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three of the four crops are not in any report-crops directory, so I cropped
them from the local PDFs. My first attempt put all four in ONE output
directory, and `scan_crop` rasterizes into `_pages` beside its output — so
page 260 of Seven Sketches was served as page 313 of the QFT volume, and I
was looking at a category-theory page while believing it was supergravity.

Caught because the content was obviously wrong for the document. Redone with a
per-document directory. It is the same contamination as the two concurrent
runs in out/184: a shared work directory, and an image that looks like a
perfectly good measurement of something else.
