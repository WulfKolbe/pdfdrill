WHERE THE GUARD DOES FIRE: penev_A AND penev_B — STOPPED
----------------------------------------------------------
Not the six, but the two that already hold a second reading.

  penev_A   proofs.verify = False   tex 111
  penev_B   proofs.verify = False   tex  59

Their models are newer than their lines.json by mtime, so this is the
content-hash proof failing, not the old mtime heuristic. ANY command
routing through `_stale_or_absent` — injectlatex or compare — would call
`cmd_model` and destroy the 111 and 59 `tex` realizations, which are the
only explicitly-supplied author readings in the corpus.

So I did not touch them, and that includes NOT rebuilding their
compare.html. They are the documents 521 exists to serve and they are the
two it must not run on. Their sidecars record a `latex_source_path` under
a DIFFERENT project's scratchpad (`-home-wkolbe-pdfdrill-github-io`), from
2026-08-25, which is the likeliest reason the recorded inputs no longer
hash-match.

Repairing that proof is its own task: it needs the model rebuilt and the
111/59 candidates re-injected from `penev_diss.tex`, which is not on disk
here.

SIX, NOT NINETEEN — AND THE REASON IS THE POINT
------------------------------------------------
`<stem>.tex.zip` is MathPix's OWN reconstruction. `cmd_injectlatex` says
so in its own docstring — "redundant with the mathpix column" — and its
source order prefers a real e-print over it for exactly that reason.

  author source available     7 of 21   (the arXiv e-prints)
    already injected          1         (1510.06699, 268 candidates)
    injected by 521           6
  penev_A/B                   2         author .tex, supplied by hand,
                                        blocked by the proof
  MathPix tex.zip only       12         NOT injected

Injecting the tex.zip on those 12 would fill compare.html's second column
with the same engine's output — a comparison that cannot disagree, which
is the failure shape this project keeps catching. They are left alone.
