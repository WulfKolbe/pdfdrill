156 — the confidence square's colour mapping
============================================

THE MAPPING (report_tex.conf_cell)
-----------------------------------
   confidence            band        colour            RGB
   >= 0.9                confgreen   green             0,128,0
   0.5 <= v < 0.9        confamber   amber/brown     190,120,0
   v < 0.5               confred     dark red        190,0,0
   None / "" / unparsable  ---       no colour at all

   Thresholds are inclusive at the lower edge: exactly 0.9 is green, exactly
   0.5 is amber. An absent value renders as a dash and never as a colour —
   no reading is not a good reading, and a blank green square would assert one.

   0.018 therefore resolves to confred. There is no cyan in the mapping, and no
   fourth band.

0.018 IS NOT RENDERING CYAN — VERIFIED
---------------------------------------
   in the source        \confcell{confred}{0.018}
   colour definition    \definecolor{confred}{RGB}{190,0,0}

   Rasterised with ghostscript at 150 dpi and every saturated pixel sampled
   across pages 1-6:

      rgb(160,  0,  0)   x 96      confred, anti-aliased
      rgb(192,  0,  0)   x 29      confred
      rgb(208, 16, 16)   x  9      inkComponent (the residual bullet)

      cyan-ish pixels (b>150, g>150, r<150):  0

   Not one cyan pixel exists in the rendered page content.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY CYAN
---------------------
The AcroForm field. Viewers TINT interactive form fields with their own
highlight colour — pale blue/cyan in Chrome, Firefox and Acrobat alike — drawn
on top of the page, which is why it cannot appear in a ghostscript raster of
the page content.

The field sits in the same "Conf. + class" column as the confidence square:

   ■ 0.018   •  C|+25   [ field ]
   ^ red             ^ viewer paints this box pale cyan

55 of 57 rows carry one. A column containing a small red square and a larger
pale-cyan box reads as "the square is cyan" at a glance, especially at
\footnotesize where the square is about 1mm across and the field is 2.4em wide.

So the defect is real and visible, and it is not in the mapping: nothing is
mis-banded, and no threshold is wrong. It is the field's viewer highlight
sitting beside the square it appears to recolour.

That is the same field 155 asks to hide and 157 asks whether anything still
needs. Removing it removes the cyan.
