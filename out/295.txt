WHAT THE 2.3% MEANS, and it is the number that matters

  41,668 image-region rows across the corpus, and 950 carry any LaTeX at
  all. The other 40,718 are rows where MathPix emitted a crop and no code —
  an \includegraphics is its statement that it could not read the region.

  So the region-compile rate of 83.2% is a rate over 950 rows, not over
  41,668. It says the renderer handles what it is given; it says nothing
  about the 97.7% it is never given.

  This is the same shape as 337's seven pairs being exhaustive rather than
  favourable: a high rate on a small denominator, where the denominator is
  the finding.
