WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED

That these are the same KIND of thing. Similar geometry is a reason to look,
not a finding. Two regions can be the same size and shape and be a matrix and
a commutative diagram, which are different objects that happen to occupy the
same rectangle.

450 measures whether they are close in INK, which is the part that can be
settled without reading either.

ONE PROPERTY OF THE SET WORTH NOTING BEFORE ANYONE LOOKS

Six of the pairs are same-page or adjacent-page within two documents
(Seven Sketches, Homotopical QFT). A same-page pair is the strongest kind:
the same scan, the same rendering, the same page geometry, and two different
classifications. Nothing about the page can explain the difference.
