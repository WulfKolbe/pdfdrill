WHAT 483 FIXES: THE 16
----------------------
mielke EQ0190 (p080) and EQ0453 (p151), both at confidence 1.000:

  \[ T_{G}: \quad T_{g} \psi(m)=D(…) \psi(g^{-1} m) . \] }
  \[ L_{a b}^{(\star)}:=\frac{1}{2} \varepsilon_{a b c d} L^{c d}=L_{a b} . \] }

The trailing-`\]` strip is anchored at the END of the string. A stray `}`
after it means the anchor never matches, the `\]` is then mid-string, and the
gate refuses a row whose mathematics is perfect at confidence 1.000.

481 established the brace is OURS — MathPix's lines.json for those two lines
reads `'\[\n…\n\]'` with no brace at all. `_drop_stray_closers` removes
UNMATCHED closers from the end, by counting rather than by position, before
the delimiter gate runs. Escaped `\}` does not count; a value unbalanced the
other way is left alone, because that is a different defect.

AND A DOWN-PAYMENT ON 485: THE FURNITURE
-----------------------------------------
Diagnosing the 167 mid-string rows showed 483 and 485 are the same repair
seen twice. Their shape is

  \begin{figure} \captionsetup{labelformat=empty} \caption{Table 2. …} \[ … \]

446 already drops the leading `\begin{figure}`. What it left behind was the
CAPTION, and that keeps the `\[` mid-string just as surely as the float did.
`_drop_leading_furniture` strips `\captionsetup`, `\caption`, `\label`,
`\subcaption`, `\captionof` and the bare `\centering`-class commands from the
front — with braces matched by counting, since a caption can nest them.

THE GUARD IS THAT A `\[` STILL FOLLOWS. A `\caption` is furniture only when
there is a display for it to be furniture around; a value that IS a caption
is left exactly as it is, and a caption with unbalanced braces is left alone
rather than guessed at. Both are tested.

WHY THIS IS A READ-TIME STRIP AND NOT A FIELD FIX
--------------------------------------------------
`docs/layers/PROPS.md` is explicit: `latex_refined` exists because "`latex` is
never overwritten" (232). And a model rebuild is what 430 measured — object
ids are `uuid4`, so a rebuild leaves 1 id in common out of 2,196 and every
in-place enrichment goes with it.

Stripping at read time also keeps MathPix's bytes verbatim on disk, which 465
showed has evidentiary value: the defect reports to the vendor rest on the
stored value being what they actually sent.
