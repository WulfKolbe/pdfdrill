WHAT THE STAGE DOES
Render each surviving proposal standalone, crop the scan under the object's
region, measure both with inkdrill, and accept only when the crop ink distance
FALLS. A tie is not an acceptance.

WHY THIS GATE IS THE POINT OF THE COMMAND
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the 3-row pilot run all three proposals passed all four structural checks
and all three were rejected here: the model had shortened 439 -> 206 and
1566 -> 1220 characters, quietly dropping rows from tables of root
coefficients, and the ink distance rose (+47, +50, 0).

A validate-only pipeline accepts all three. They are well-formed LaTeX, they
compile, their tables are uniform, and they are SHORTER and cleaner-looking
than the originals — which is exactly what a plausible wrong answer looks like.
The only thing that catches them is rendering the proposal and looking at how
much ink it puts on the page next to the scan.

That is the difference between "a model said this is better" and "this is
measurably closer to the document".

UNMEASURABLE ROWS
   0 here, but they are counted separately and never silently accepted. A row
   that could not be rendered, or whose crop could not be taken, records a
   `measure_error` and keeps status "proposed" — it does not become an
   acceptance by default and does not become a rejection either. It is
   unfinished, and the file says so.

A BLANK REFERENCE IS NOT A MEASUREMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Added after the pages session found a guard of their own that reported success
having inspected zero files. The same shape was in this stage, and it was worse
than useless — it inverted the metric.

A scan crop with no ink components is what EVERY crop bug looks like: a region
scaled wrong, a crop off the page, a blank strip, an image inkdrill read and
found nothing in. Measured against it:

   scan blank, original renders 40 glyphs  ->  ink_before = 45
   scan blank, proposal renders 12 glyphs  ->  ink_after  = 13
   accepted, delta -32

The gate rewards the proposal for deleting content, and rewards the emptiest
proposal most. Blank against blank is worse: distance 0, which the ink gate
reads as "the render already matches the scan" and skips as needing no work.

So zero ink in the REFERENCE is now refused and the row is reported as
unmeasurable rather than measured, in both the gate and the measure stage.

This changes none of the counts above: all 19 proposals had a measurable scan
reference, the smallest carrying 98 ink components. The defect was latent here
and would have surfaced the first time a region was wrong — which is precisely
the bug I had already fixed once in this build, when unscaled MathPix
coordinates were cropping the wrong part of the page.
