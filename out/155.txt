155 — the AcroForm field: REMOVED, not hidden
=============================================

WHY REMOVE RATHER THAN HIDE
---------------------------
155 asked for the field to be made visually hidden so it "remains the update
channel without duplicating the display", with the standing condition that if
157 found nothing re-fills it, 155 becomes remove.

157 found nothing re-fills it. Every reference to fill_ink_fields.py in the
repository is a comment or a help string; the script is not in version control;
no command declares the ink measurement as a prerequisite; and reporttex always
rebuilds report.pdf from source, resetting every field to its placeholder.

Observed rather than inferred: the fields were filled twice today and BOTH
times a later rebuild wiped them and I re-filled by hand. The "update without
recompiling" path the field existed for was never once taken.

So it is removed.

WHAT THIS ALSO FIXES
--------------------
156 reported the confidence square rendering cyan at 0.018. The mapping was
never wrong — 0.018 resolves to confred, and a ghostscript raster of pages 1-6
contains zero cyan pixels. The cyan was the viewer's own highlight tint on the
form field, drawn on top of the page beside the square. With no widgets left
in the file, there is nothing for a viewer to tint.

WHAT WENT WITH IT
-----------------
   \inkfield          the field macro
   \begin{Form}       the environment that existed only to hold fields
   \usepackage{hyperref}   loaded only for TextField
   \special{pdf:put @catalog <</NeedAppearances true>>}
                      the request to regenerate appearances

   The report no longer loads hyperref at all.

TWO THINGS DELIBERATELY KEPT
-----------------------------
1. pdf_reading.assert_form_fields and its five tests. The gate is correct and
   it earned its place — it caught the 133 regression that silently dropped
   every field in the demo. It now guards a capability nothing in the pipeline
   uses, which is where it started; that is a reason to keep the tests, not to
   delete the check.

2. The --ink flag and everything downstream of it. The residual data still
   reaches the report — as the bullet colour and as selectable text — which is
   what made the field redundant in the first place.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE AUDITOR'S TOOL
-----------------------------------
fill_ink_fields.py's MEASUREMENT half is still needed: it produces the --json
dump that --ink consumes. Its FILL half now has nothing to write into and will
raise SystemExit("no AcroForm fields") on a report built by current pdfdrill.

It needs a measure-only mode. That is a change to the auditor's file and is not
made here.
