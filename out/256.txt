Every text line's `subtype` says how that line attaches to the line before it:

    continues_line_newline      155,679    996        join across a source newline
    continues_line_no_hyphen    142,042    929        join and DROP the hyphen

    1,228,183 lines, on 41% of the corpus's 2,425,957 text lines

`no_hyphen` versus `no_space` is precisely the distinction the heuristic could
not make, and the docstring's own example is on the wrong side of it:
"CIFAR-" + "10" is `no_space` — keep the hyphen, add no space.

DIRECTION — CHECKED, BECAUSE GETTING IT BACKWARDS INVERTS THE RULE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The verdict sits on the FOLLOWING line, not on the broken one. Measured over
hyphen-ending lines:

    the hyphen line's OWN subtype   space 572 · no_hyphen 101 · no_space 14
    the FOLLOWING line's subtype    no_space 473 · no_hyphen 399 · space 32

The broken line's own subtype describes how *it* joined its predecessor and
reads `space` most of the time. Reading it as the verdict would have produced
"join with a space" on the majority of real hyphen breaks — the exact opposite
of the fix.

WHAT THE OLD RULE COST, AND IT IS NOT THE FAILURE THE DOCSTRING NAMED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The docstring worried about "one-to-one" becoming "oneto-one". I searched the
corpus for that shape: three line breaks after a word ending "one-", and not
one of them is "one-to-one". Meanwhile:

    139,660 lines carry `continues_line_no_hyphen` where the preceding line
    does NOT end in a hyphen — MathPix had already removed it.

`HYPHEN_TAIL` therefore never matched, the `m is None` branch ran, and a space
was inserted into the middle of the word:

    "the posterior probability that the hy" + " " + "pothesis is true"
    "The measure"  + " " + "ment result is k"
    "Never"        + " " + "theless, the content of"
    "of the mea"   + " " + "surement is k with probability"

That is 139,660 split words against 3 possible compound mis-joins. The named
failure was the rare one; the unnamed one was two thousand times larger and had
been sitting in the default path the whole time.

WHAT CHANGED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both implementations, because the one with teeth was not the one the task
named. `DehyphenationProcessor` (docmodel, the derived cleaned stream) is the
demo; `docops.mutators.Dehyphenate` is what rewrites `Paragraph.props.text` —
the text every projector reads. Both had the same `m is None` branch.

  src/docmodel/modules/dehyphenation.py
    `_STATED` maps the four values to an action. The stated join decides;
    absent it, the old heuristic runs unchanged. Each `dehyphenate` alignment
    now carries `join_basis`: "mathpix" | "heuristic" | "none".
    Guard: `no_hyphen` on a line that does NOT end in "-" means "no extra
    character", so the last letter is never amputated.

  src/docops/mutators/dehyphenate.py
    Same precedence, applied where the paragraph text is actually built.
    Counters `joins_from_mathpix` / `joins_from_heuristic` separate the two.

  tests/test_dehyphenate_stated.py — 9 tests, including the 139,660-line
  dominant case, the CIFAR-10 compound, both heuristic fallbacks (the existing
  "one-to-one" and "in-formation" cases still pass untouched), and the
  completeness of `_STATED` against the four corpus values.

Suite 2,516, all green.

THE HEURISTIC IS NOT RETIRED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
59% of text lines carry no subtype, and only 5,036 of the 37,185 hyphen-broken
lines have a verdict — the field is populated unevenly by document, not by line.
So the fallback carries most of the traffic and had to keep working, not merely
keep compiling. Both of its existing tests pass unchanged.

ONE SELF-INFLICTED DETOUR, RECORDED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
My first scan for the field looked for KEYS named `continues_line_*` and found
zero, in a corpus where 1.2 million lines carry those strings as VALUES of
`subtype`. A `grep -rl continues_line` over the library also returned nothing,
which agreed with the wrong scan and nearly closed the task as "the premise has
no support in the library". Two instruments agreeing is not corroboration when
they share the same defect.

The second detour: two test failures in a row that were stale `.pyc`, written
by my own corpus-validation runs — which I invoked as bare `python3` without
`PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`. That is HANDOVER-RULES rule 4, triggered by the
scripts I wrote to verify the fix rather than by the test command.

The third was mine and not the code's: I asserted the cleaned chunks join to
"thehypothesis" when the correct answer is "the hypothesis" — the surviving
space is between the article and the word. The code was right and my expected
value was wrong, which for a moment looked like the fix failing.
