THE AMENDMENT
-------------
`pdfdrill reporttex --formulas <rule>`, default changed from the old
behaviour to `unresolved`:

  unresolved  (default)  only the rows whose Rendered cell would say
                         "(not rendered)". When none do, the section AND its
                         manifest record are omitted.
  none                   the section is dropped outright.
  all                    every first-occurrence formula — what it did before.

A row is unresolved when it HAS LaTeX and `renderable` refuses it. That is
exactly the predicate the Rendered cell uses, so the section holds what the
reader would have had to hunt for. A row with no LaTeX at all is not
unresolved — it is absent, and shows as a dash; those are excluded.

The four kohlhase rows are one formula split across four objects by an
unbalanced `\hbox{` — a segmentation fault upstream of the renderer, not a
LaTeX one. The seven-sketches row carries CJK ideographs where the page
prints a composition symbol, which is the same defect class as the eight sent
to MathPix. Those are 458's material, and now they are the whole section
rather than seven rows inside 37,624.

WHAT THIS COSTS AND WHAT IT SAVES
---------------------------------
37,624 rows become 7. 17 of 21 documents lose the section entirely; the four
that keep it keep 1, 1, 4 and 1 row.

Nothing is lost that is not recoverable: `--formulas all` still builds the
full section, and the formulas were never the artefact's subject — they carry
no confidence, no residual, and no scan crop, so a formula row shows an
identifier, a page, a source and a render, which is a catalogue entry.

Three existing tests asserted the old whole-section behaviour
(test_report_filters, test_report_tex, test_trailingpunct). Each now passes
formulas="all" with a line saying why, because each is testing something
else. Full suite: 2,788 passed.

A NOTE ON WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO
-------------------------------
It does not touch formula-report.html, which is the artefact where a complete
list of formulas belongs and where 463 will generate one. Moving the
catalogue out of report.pdf is only half the amendment; the other half is
that the catalogue then exists somewhere on purpose.
