THE NEGATIVE RESULT, WHICH MATTERS MORE THAN THE FIX
-----------------------------------------------------
I set out to add a magnitude ceiling to the accept stage. Measured against
this population, no threshold on magnitude separates the two groups:

    signal              correct repairs    wrong repairs     separates
    ink delta           -95 .. -23         -261 .. -24       NO
    character ratio     0.58 .. 1.13       0.07 .. 0.94      NO
    string similarity   0.039 .. 0.498     0.011 .. 0.323    NO

johnston EQ1276 is a CORRECT repair at delta -95 and similarity 0.039,
because rewriting `\longdiv` as an array changes nearly every character while
preserving the mathematics exactly. Any ceiling strict enough to reject the
twelve rejects that too — and still passes voloshin EQ0197 at -24. So no
ceiling was written. One that looked principled and separated nothing would
be worse than none, and this repository has the scar already (the deploy
guard's removed equations check, and `publishcheck`'s stale `published/`
preference).

WHAT WAS ADDED INSTEAD: `refine.bleeding_proposals()`
------------------------------------------------------
The one signal that survived measurement. Two rows of one document whose
PROPOSED values are near-copies are bleeding: EQ1187~EQ0515 at 0.95,
EQ0543~EQ0748 at 0.69, with no false positives on this population. Perfect
precision, recall 4 of 12. Worth having because it is SPECIFIC — a pair it
names is bleeding, not merely large.

THE POLICY QUESTION THIS RAISES, FOR THE USER
----------------------------------------------
12 of 29 ink-verified refinements were wrong: a 41% error rate on values that
`_chosen_reading` PREFERS over MathPix's own reading and publishes. "Verified
by ink" does not mean verified — ink measures how much ink a rendering makes,
not whether it is the same ink.

The strongest available fix is to stop publishing ink-only refinements:
`_chosen_reading` would prefer a refinement only when it is verified by CENSUS
or EPRINT — evidence about identity — while ink-verified ones stay recorded
and the raw reading publishes. That costs the 5 good repairs above until they
are re-verified by census, which is cheap for 5 rows and would be a one-time
pass. It is not taken here because it un-publishes readings this task has just
judged correct, and that is the user's call.
