  9 of 9, 100% coverage.

493 framed the missing piece as "one number per book — the PDF page on which
printed page 1 falls". That framing is wrong, and usefully so: the answer is
not a number but a TABLE, and the publisher already wrote it. `page_labels[i]`
gives the printed label of PDF page i; inverting it gives printed -> PDF
directly, with no arithmetic and nothing to derive.

IS THE OFFSET CONSTANT? FOR SIX OF NINE. AND IT DOES NOT MATTER.
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  constant      cardona 11, fong-invitation 0, seven-sketches 12,
                gilmore 14, kohlhase 0, voloshin 16
  NOT constant  johnston   offsets 15 (x233), 14 (x167), 11 (x34), 13 (x21)
                lyche      14 (x53), 17 (x45), 11 (x35), 9 (x31)
                mielke      6 (x53), 13 (x51), 14 (x43), 16 (x35)

The cause is visible and is not roman front matter — that part is clean. The
ARABIC sequence itself skips:

  johnston  printed 233 -> 235 at index 248, 401 -> 403 at 415, and 5 more.
            7 breaks in 477 arabic pages.
  lyche     25 -> 27 at index 45, 55 -> 57 at 74, and 13 more. 15 breaks.
  gilmore   0 breaks — perfectly monotone.

So the offset is piecewise constant, stepping by one at each skipped printed
number, and a single offset is simply the wrong model for these three books.
The label table has no such problem: it states each page's label outright, so
a skip is data rather than an error term.

Front matter in roman was the suspected complication and is not one. Every
book's roman prefix is contiguous and the arabic run starts cleanly after it;
roman labels are simply excluded from the inverse map.

A WARNING ABOUT WHAT THE PATH MEANS
-----------------------------------
"Some TOC entry precedes this page" is not the same as "this is the enclosing
section". The number above is the coverage of a lookup, not its accuracy —
a math object one page before a chapter break gets the previous chapter,
correctly, and one on a page whose printed number was SKIPPED gets whatever
the previous mapped entry was, which may be wrong by a section. johnston has
7 such skips and lyche 15.

Accuracy needs a second measurement — the section a path claims against the
Section object the model already holds where it has one — and this report
does not make that claim.

WHAT DID NOT WORK, AND IS RECORDED SO IT IS NOT RETRIED
--------------------------------------------------------
Matching the OUTLINE against the TOC by title. Outlines exist on all nine
(17 to 279 entries, nearly all with a destination page) and named
destinations run to 3,283 on mielke — but title matching returned 0 for six
of nine books:

  outline titles carry no section number  'Preface', 'Focus',
                                          'Notes in the Margin'
  and sometimes a leading space           ' Contents' (gilmore)
  while the TOC parse splits the number off into its own group

It worked on the two books whose outline titles happen to include the number
— seven-sketches (136 matched, offset 12, 94.9% agreement) and voloshin (12
matched, offset 16, 100% agreement) — and both agree exactly with the page
labels, which is a useful corroboration of the label route and nothing more.

The outline is the worse instrument here and is not needed.

Nothing was changed.
