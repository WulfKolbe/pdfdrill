NINETEEN, NOT TWENTY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   You asked for 20 equation crops below confidence 0.1. There are 19 in
   the corpus, and that is the whole population, not a sampling limit -
   out/064 measured exactly 19 of 4,338 equations below that threshold.

   A twentieth requires relaxing the cut. out/064's distribution:
     < 0.05    14
     < 0.10    19      <- this set
     < 0.20    35
     < 0.50   178

   I did not relax it. Padding a threshold-defined set with a row that
   fails the threshold would make the trial's own population a fiction,
   and every per-variant number computed on it would inherit that.
