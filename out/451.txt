WHAT CHANGED IN THE LINE THAT DID NOT CHANGE
--------------------------------------------
Row 2 is the same pixels in both. Three differences in how it was read:

  1. the operator      A: \operatorname{hocolim}      B: \bigvee
     In B the word "hocolim" migrated OUT of the operator slot and INTO the
     superscript, and a large vee took its place. The page prints "hocolim".

  2. the superscript   A: \mathrm{h}                  B: \mathrm{hocolim}
     The page prints ℂ. Both are wrong, in different ways, from one image.

  3. the exponent      A: S^{T \square} \square_{f}-Q_{f}
                       B: S^{T \square_{f}-Q_{f}}
     The page prints S^{T□_f − Q_f}. B IS CORRECT. A duplicated the box and
     let the subscript escape the superscript.

So removing content from row 1 FIXED one defect in row 2 and CREATED another.
The effect runs in both directions, which is what makes it a mechanism rather
than a threshold: the model is not reading the second line on its own terms.

AND WITHIN ARM A ALONE
----------------------
The same construct appears twice in one image and is read two ways: the
superscript on row 1 is \mathrm{C}, on row 2 it is \mathrm{h}. The page prints

inconsistency-within-one-image.

real effect and it is not a fix: 0.02 is not a usable reading either.
