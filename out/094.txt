WHY A LEADING RUN IS DIFFERENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   text_tail exists to strip prose that TRAILS mathematics - 'where n is
   even', 'for all such x'. That is commentary: the expression is complete
   without it.

   A run that LEADS is the subject. '\text{Pack}_{<\omega}' is not
   commentary attached to a subscript; the subscript modifies it. Removing
   it leaves '_{<\omega}', which is not a smaller expression - it is not
   an expression.
