455 — what a dataset could be built from today
==============================================

MEASURED FROM DISK: 250 documents carry a residual measurement, 20,124 rows.

BY LABEL QUALITY

  verified CORRECTED          33 rows
      the accepted repairs. Basis: ink 32, author source 1. Every one carries
      latex_refined, verified_by, ink_before, ink_after, ink_delta, basis,
      author and a timestamp (437). This is the only class where a human
      decision is recorded per row.

  verified CORRECT         5,297 rows in 102 documents
      ink agreed (clean or noise) AND the author's own LaTeX is on disk.
      **This is a CEILING, not a count.** The author source being present
      means the reading CAN be checked against it; it does not mean each row
      HAS been. Per-row confirmation is not recorded anywhere — which is
      itself the finding for dataset purposes, because the difference between
      "checkable" and "checked" is the difference between a label and a hope.

  UNVERIFIED               9,101 rows in 148 documents
      ink agreed and nothing else did. The residual says the render matches
      the scan; nothing says the reading is what the page means. 407 measured
      what that is worth: an ink distance is a structural census, and two
      different pictures with the same census score zero.

  EXCLUDED                 6,851 rows in 102 documents
      every measured row of a document whose author LaTeX is on disk. For a
      dataset built from MathPix output these are the rows where a better
      label already exists, so training on the OCR reading would be training
      on the worse of two available answers.

  the four do not partition: "verified correct" is the agreeing subset of
  "excluded", by construction. The disjoint split is
      33 corrected  +  9,101 unverified  +  6,851 from gold-bearing documents
      +  4,139 measured rows that agreed on neither count (weak, component,
         stable, unrendered on non-gold documents)

WHAT THIS IS NOT

  It is not 20,124 usable pairs. The honest usable core today is the 33.
  Everything else is a candidate whose label rests on an instrument that
  measures ink, and 407 established the ink is a structural census rather
  than a reading.

THE LICENCE POSITION, which constrains all of it

  provenance of the 250 measured documents      docs      rows
    other / unknown provenance                   120     6,395
    arXiv e-print                                114     7,555
    publisher DOI / Elsevier                       8       489
    Z-Library book                                 8     5,685

  These are CROPS OF PUBLISHED PAGES. The crop is a reproduction of the
  page's typeset expression, not a description of it.

  * The 8 Z-Library books — 5,685 rows, 28% of everything measured — are
    redistributed copies of commercial textbooks. Nothing derived from them
    can be published, and they are the single largest contiguous block: the
    five biggest measured documents by row count are books.
  * The 8 publisher/Elsevier documents are the same position.
  * The 114 arXiv e-prints are the only clearly publishable subset, and only
    where the paper's own licence permits it — arXiv's default licence grants
    distribution of the PAPER, not automatic rights to redistribute derived
    crops, and CC-BY / CC-BY-SA / arXiv-perpetual differ on exactly this.
  * The 120 "other" have unknown provenance and would each have to be
    established before any of them could be included. Unknown is not
    permissive.

  359 measured the shape of this already from the other side: DaTikZ-V4's
  public release is 21% of its filtered set, and a licence split is the
  likely reason. A dataset assembled here would face the same arithmetic and
  worse, because a TikZ body is authored source under the paper's licence
  while a crop is a reproduction of a printed page.

WHAT COULD ACTUALLY BE PUBLISHED TODAY

  Of the 33 corrected rows, those on arXiv e-prints with a permissive licence.
  That is a number in the low tens at best, and every one would need its
  paper's licence checked individually.

  The 5,685 rows from books cannot be published at any label quality, and
  they are the rows where the measurement is densest.

  Nothing here says the corpus is not worth measuring. It says the
  measurement's value is internal — finding defects to report to MathPix,
  which is what it has been doing — and that a released dataset is a
  different project with a licence audit at the front of it, not at the end.
