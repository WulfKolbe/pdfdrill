257 — the exact MathPix request shape, and the three flags
==============================================================================

NONE OF THE THREE FLAGS IS SET. ANYWHERE. ON EITHER ROUTE.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   grep -rn "enable_document_layout|disable_lstlisting|disable_itemize" src/ tests/
   -> no matches

So all three are whatever the MathPix server defaults are. Every corpus
behaviour attributed to them is the server's choice, not ours, and nothing in
this repo has ever varied them.

ROUTE 1 — THE PDF ROUTE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   endpoint   POST https://api.mathpix.com/v3/pdf-file
   body       multipart/form-data, two parts:
                options_json  (the JSON below, verbatim)
                file          (the PDF, streamed in 4 MB chunks)
   headers    app_id, app_key, Content-Type: multipart/form-data; boundary=…,
              Content-Length
   polling    GET /v3/pdf/{pdf_id}
   formats    lines.json, md, tex.zip   (DEFAULT_FORMATS, download order)

   options_json AS TRANSMITTED — json.dumps(CONVERSION_OPTIONS), complete:

     {"conversion_options":
       {"tex.zip": {"include_equation_tags": true,
                    "idiomatic_eqn_arrays": true},
        "md":      {"math_inline_delimiters": ["$", "$"],
                    "math_display_delimiters": ["$$", "$$"],
                    "escape_ampersand": "true",
                    "escape_dollar": "true",
                    "escape_percent": "false",
                    "escape_hash": "true"}}}

That is the whole of it. Six string-valued escape flags and four booleans, all
inside `conversion_options`, which is the per-FORMAT rendering block. There is
no top-level options key at all — no enable_document_layout, no
include_line_data, no rm_spaces, nothing that would change what the OCR
segments, only how the chosen formats are written out.

Note the four escape values are the STRINGS "true"/"false", not booleans. That
is how they were copied from mtestzx.py and it is what goes on the wire.

ROUTE 2 — THE SNIPPET ROUTE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   endpoint   POST https://api.mathpix.com/v3/text
   body       application/json, built by build_payload():

     {"src": "<data: URI or URL>",
      "formats": ["text", "data"],
      "math_inline_delimiters": ["$", "$"],
      "rm_spaces": true,
      "include_line_data": true,
      "data_options": {"include_latex": true}}

   `is_async` and an arbitrary `extra` dict are supported and unused by the
   callers. Again: none of the three flags.

The two routes share no options object. The PDF route sends conversion_options
only; the snippet route sends top-level options only. Neither sends the other's.

WHAT THE CORPUS SAYS ABOUT enable_document_layout
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The premise is right that layout was active: 44,689 `code` lines and 7,078
`pseudocode` lines came back, plus 24,806 diagram, 22,638 figure_label, 2,136
chart and the whole table_of_contents family. A route with document layout off
would not emit those types.

Since we send nothing, that is the SERVER DEFAULT, uniformly, across all 1,350
jobs. It is not our setting and it never varied.

WHAT THE CORPUS SAYS ABOUT disable_lstlisting — AND THE HYPOTHESIS IS WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scanned all 1,216 tex.zip archives:

   \begin{lstlisting}   477 blocks in 161 documents
   \begin{verbatim}   6,370 blocks in 220 documents
   both, same document              54 documents
   lstlisting only                 107
   verbatim only                   166

**54 documents contain both.** 0212032 has 9 lstlisting and 25 verbatim in one
tex.zip; 2109.08899 has 2 and 2. A per-job flag cannot produce both inside a
single job's output. MathPix is choosing per BLOCK — lstlisting where it
identifies a listing, verbatim otherwise — and the choice is a property of the
content, not of a setting that varied across jobs.

So the corpus does not show the flag varying. It shows the flag never being
sent and the server making a per-block decision. The markdown route, separately,
is uniform: 0 lstlisting and 13,413 fenced blocks across 296 of 1,430 .md
files — fences are simply what the md format uses.

disable_itemize: 21,536 itemize/enumerate blocks in 1,017 of 1,216 tex.zip
archives. Not disabled, and never sent.

WHAT THIS MEANS IF ANY OF THEM IS EVER WANTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All three are top-level options on /v3/pdf-file, and CONVERSION_OPTIONS has no
top-level section — it is `{"conversion_options": {...}}` and nothing else. So
setting one is not a value change, it is a structural addition to the body, and
the current shape gives no place to put it that would be picked up by accident.

I have not added them. Sending an option we have never sent would change the
segmentation of every future job, and out/164 already measured that
enable_document_layout is not free: it was an A/B on one page of 0902.0431.
Changing it globally on the strength of a code read, with 1,350 documents
already drilled under the default, is not a change to make without the A/B
being redone at corpus scale.

Nothing changed in this task.
