611 — the manifest wired into inkmeasure, and what the pairing shows

WHAT WAS BUILT

  regionink._ROW widened from 15 columns to 20. inkdrill's compare gained
  `row h`, `row y0`, `row y1` in 606; the trailing three are captured as an
  OPTIONAL group, so an older inkdrill returning 15 still parses and its rows
  simply carry no y-extent.

  inkmeasure.rows_manifest(doc_dir) reads pdfdrill-rows.json and REFUSES when
  its measured_against.sha256 is not the report.pdf on disk — a manifest for a
  build that no longer exists would pair rows against rectangles measured
  somewhere else.

  inkmeasure.pair_rows(lattice, manifest, page, dpi) converts the manifest's
  bp (y up from the page bottom) and the lattice's px (y down from the top)
  into one frame and claims each lattice row for the identifier whose band
  contains its CENTRE. Containment, not counting: 604 measured that page 4 of
  0707.4470 had three identifiers and three lattice rows that were still not
  the same three.

IS THE HEADER DROPPED BECAUSE NOTHING CLAIMS IT?

  ON PAGE 3, YES — and that is the mechanism working exactly as the argument
  for the rect said it would. Page 3 has 6 lattice rows and 5 manifest bands;
  line 0 at y 568..617 falls inside no band and is dropped. No rule
  recognises it as a header. Nothing claims it, so it goes.

  ON PAGES 1 AND 2, NO — and the reason is 608#3, which is still open. Those
  pages pair 2 rows into 1 band and 28 into 27: the first data row of a table
  takes the TABLE'S TOP RULE, which is above the repeated header, so its band
  swallows the header row instead of leaving it unclaimed.

  So the two count corrections 604 argued against do disappear — the header
  needs no rule and the legend needs none — but only where the bands are
  right. The remaining failure is not in the pairing; it is one wrong rule in
  the emitter.

  Page 4's unpaired row (y 255..945) is 0049_DIA_0006, the page-straddling
  row. pair_rows excludes any row with rules_on_one_page false, because a row
  whose two rules are on different pages bounds no rectangle on either — so
  the lattice row is correctly left unclaimed rather than paired against a
  528 bp "rectangle".

608's THREE DEFECTS IN MY EMITTER — TWO FIXED, ONE ATTEMPTED AND REVERTED

  1. x WAS +6.177 bp ON EVERY RULE. FIXED. A savepos at a cell's start sits
     past \tabcolsep; the mark now carries \kern-\tabcolsep inside its
     zero-width smashed box, so the measurement moves and the layout does
     not. Table 1/2 now reads 54.35 against the PDF's 54.155, and 670.70
     against 670.499 — the residual is the +0.20 rule-centre convention
     named in 607's addendum, not 6.177.

  2. THE RIGHTMOST COLUMN RULE WAS MISSING FROM EVERY TABLE. FIXED. N
     cell-start marks give N left rules and never the right edge of the last
     column; a \hfill-carried mark now supplies it. Table 1/2 has 6 column
     rules where it had 5 (rightmost 1136.20 against the PDF's 1136.396) and
     table 3 has 8 where it had 7 (1169.31 against 1169.510).

  3. THE FIRST ROW OF EACH TABLE TAKES THE HEADER'S TOP RULE. ATTEMPTED AND
     REVERTED, and this is what pages 1 and 2 above are showing.

     The fix is right in principle — lay the rule at each row's OWN start
     rather than reusing the last one laid — and LONGTABLE DISCARDS A
     \noalign PLACED IMMEDIATELY AFTER \endhead. Measured: 0049_EQ0001 lost
     its mark entirely ("no savepos in the .aux") and 0049_FO0001 took a
     stale one, reading rule_above 680.3 BELOW its own rule_below 727.9 — an
     inverted band. 33 of 34 rows bounded instead of 34.

     Reverted to the previous behaviour, which is wrong for the first row of
     each table and right for every other. The header's own bottom rule
     cannot simply be marked either: the header lives inside \endhead and
     repeats on every page, so one label would be defined many times.

     The document is left at 34 of 34 bounded, 0 compile errors, with 1 and 2
     fixed and 3 open.
