The tiddler figures came from the projected arrays rather than the models. 52
documents carry a tiddler array OLDER than its model, and those 52 under-report

its array. Arrays that are NOT stale under-report zero.

That is the staleness class fixed in commit 123 (`_tiddlers_stale`): the guard
now rebuilds an array older than its model, but only when a command actually
touches that document. The arrays on disk stay stale until then, so any scan
reading them directly still sees the old numbers. The model is the source of
truth for this question and the tiddler count should not have been quoted as
if it were equivalent.
