THE LARGEST GROUP IS OUR OWN PREAMBLE, AND IT IS FIXED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   48 rows use \mathscr or \llbracket. The report preamble carried
   amsmath, amssymb, longtable, array and graphicx - but NOT mathrsfs or
   stmaryrd, which the STANDALONE renderer has carried since task 022. So
   these equations render perfectly on their own and throw 'Undefined
   control sequence' inside the report, which is exactly why they took so
   long to explain: every isolated reproduction succeeded.

   Verified end to end on 0707.4470, whose 31 demotions were ALL of this
   kind: its first compile pass logs 39 'Undefined control sequence'
   errors, every one a \mathscr. Adding the two packages:

       before   13 pages, 31 rows demoted
       after    13 pages,  0 rows demoted

   Fixed in src/pdfdrill/report_tex.py; the corpus reports still carry the
   old demotions until they are regenerated, which is the consumer's
   window to schedule, not mine to take.

   Two hypotheses died before this one, both refuted by measurement rather
   than argument: collateral demotion of a failing row's NEIGHBOURS (59 of
   60 unexplained rows had no failing row within three), and a cascade from
   an alignment-class error (0707.4470 has 31 unexplained demotions and
   zero alignment errors to cascade from).
