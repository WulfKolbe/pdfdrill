OURS, IN DETAIL

**\longdiv, 7 rows.** `longdivision.sty` IS INSTALLED on this machine —
/usr/share/texmf-dist/tex/latex/longdivision/longdivision.sty — and the report
preamble does not load it. MathPix emits `\longdiv` for long division and the
package that defines it is sitting there unused. 14 occurrences across 3
documents corpus-wide (this one 7, Number/Shape/Symmetry 5, Wolfram Language
2). A one-line preamble change.

**The escaped dollar, 2 rows.** Both values carry `\$` — currency, correctly
escaped: `\$ 151`, and a matrix with `\$` in two cells. `renderable()` does

    if r"\[" in lx or r"\]" in lx or "$" in lx:
        return ""
    if re.sub(r"\\%", "", lx).count("%"):
        return ""

The `%` check strips its escape. The `$` check on the line above does not.
Two adjacent lines, one rule each, and only one of them handles the escape.

**Bare & at brace depth 0, 4 rows.** The check is right in principle — a `&`
at depth 0 is a longtable tab mark and hangs the compile. Its env-stripping
loop is what fails:

    re.sub(r"\\begin\{(\w+\*?)\}.*?\\end\{\1\}", " ", …)

NON-GREEDY, so on a NESTED same-name environment — `array` inside `array`,
which is every one of these four — it pairs the outer `\begin` with the INNER
`\end` and leaves the outer `\end` and its `&`s exposed. All four are matrices
inside matrices.

MathPix's, IN DETAIL

**\begin{figure} \[ …, 3 rows.** MathPix glued a `figure` environment opener
onto display mathematics, so the value carries a `\[` that is not at the
start. The gate refuses it and xelatex confirms the refusal — `\eqno` in math
mode. This is the class the 967 `\end{itemize}` fragments belonged to, from
the other end: there a closer was glued on and `renderable()` learned to drop
it; here an opener is glued on and nothing drops it.

**Prose as a formula, 1 row.** `FOX_fabb4bba18` holds
`1 per dozen peanut butter cookies sold, \\` — English prose with a trailing
`\\`, segmented as an inline formula. Not mathematics at all.
