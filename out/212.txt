WHY EVERY ONE OF THE ELEVEN NEEDS THE CHAIN, NOT JUST THE NEW ONES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The point raised with 213 is right and it is the reason this list exists. The
\mathbb fix changes 758 rendered equations. Any report built before 2026-08-26
10:10 typesets those as ⊮, ⊬ and ⊭ — and any residual measured against such a
report scored a difference between a blackboard digit on the scan and a
negated turnstile in the render. That difference is real in the artefact and
absent from the document.

So the five books already drilled are NOT exempt. Four of them were drilled
between 08-25 23:09 and 08-26 09:07 — before the fix. Only 2103.01507 has been
rebuilt since, in 194, and it is the single current document.

Three of the eleven have \mathbb hits and their pages genuinely change:
Geometrodynamics (9) and the QFT volume (24). The other eight need the chain
for the normaliser fix, for a reading build with a legend, or because they
have never been drilled at all.
