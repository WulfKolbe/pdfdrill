WHICH CHARACTERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The first-hit character per document:

     12  U+0001      5  U+000F      4  U+0011      4  U+0012      4  U+0013
      3  U+0003      3  U+0010      2  U+001B      2  U+0002      2  U+001A
      1  U+0014      1  U+0004      1  U+0016

All C0, all in the 0x01–0x1B band, none of them formatting characters anyone
would type. That distribution is the signature of a PDF font with a broken or
absent /ToUnicode map: the extractor is handing back raw glyph codes because
there is nothing to map them to.

A SECOND DEFECT IN THE SAME FIELD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Worth recording because it was visible the moment the value was read next to
its MathPix counterpart. The same `_geom.pdf_text` value is also scrambled:

   ours    'le derivative singular 1 terms (r) 2 form. + \x11 dt = \x10 2 dr i +
            of We points. + 2 on 2α bac'
   theirs  'The Dirac equation in Blackhole backgrounds has a number of singular'

That is not the same sentence with bad characters in it. The words are out of
order. So `_geom.pdf_text` on this line is doubly unusable, and the control
characters are the more visible half of a worse problem — a text layer being
read in glyph order rather than reading order.

WHAT THIS MEANS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing needs to be raised with MathPix. The 44 documents are a defect in our
geometry-fusion and pdfminer paths, and the fix belongs on our side: strip C0/C1
at the point of extraction, and treat a `_geom.pdf_text` that disagrees this
badly with the MathPix text as evidence the text layer is unusable for that
line rather than as a competing reading.

I have not made that change — this task asked where the characters come from,
and the answer is that they come from us.
