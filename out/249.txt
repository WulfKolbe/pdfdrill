249 — the three "absent" literals: none of them should be removed
==============================================================================

THE ANSWER IS THE OPPOSITE OF THE PREMISE, AND OUT/245 IS WHY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
out/245 reported "equation", "figure" and "caption" as type literals that occur
zero times in 4.0M line objects. That count was right and its scope was wrong:
it read `*.lines.json` only, and **MathPix is not the only producer of lines.**

   "equation"   emitted by the VISIONOCR / LLM-delegation route.
                tests/test_visionocr.py:119 builds exactly that shape.
                Removing it from EQ_TYPES broke two tests immediately:
                  test_ingest_folds_records_into_equation_nodes_with_refnum
                  test_visionocr_sandbox_roundtrip

   "figure"     exercised by tests/test_picture.py:117, which constructs a
   "caption"    `figure` line to drive _from_figure_line. The path has a test
                asserting the shape even though no MathPix document produces
                it.

So all three stay. I removed "equation" from EQ_TYPES and from formula.py,
watched the suite go red, and put both back.

"absent from the corpus" is not "absent from the inputs". The corpus is one
producer's output, and I had written the corpus-scoped claim as if it were a
claim about the schema. This is the second correction to out/245 in two tasks;
248 was the first.

It is also the exact trap I described one task earlier, in out/248: "removing a
guard because today's corpus lacks the shape is how the next document breaks."
I wrote that about renderable()'s \end{X} stripper and then did it to EQ_TYPES
in the next task. The test suite caught it; a corpus scan never would have.

WHAT _from_figure_line WAS MEANT TO HANDLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A line of type `figure` whose caption arrives as a CHILD of type `caption`:

   picture.py:70    if ltype == "figure": _from_figure_line(...)
   picture.py:84    if child.get("type") == "caption": caption = child text
   fallback         extract_figure_caption(text) — a \caption{} in the line

That is a coherent schema. It is not MathPix's.

WHICH TYPE CARRIES IT NOW
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   the figure    `diagram`  24,806 lines, 916 documents
                 `chart`     2,136 lines, 380 documents
   the caption   `figure_label` 22,638 lines, 887 documents

And the association is NOT the one the code expects. Of 22,638 figure_label
lines:

      18,222  have NO PARENT AT ALL
       4,142  are children of `column`
         113  of `list_item`
          48  of `diagram`
          38  of `rotated_container`

Diagrams owning a figure_label child: 46 of 24,760. Charts: 1 of 2,136.
**49 figure+caption pairs in the whole corpus, across 12 documents.**

So the parent/child shape exists, and is rare. The dominant relation is
POSITIONAL — 12,767 figures have a figure_label within one line of them on the
same page, 13,201 within three, and 11,542 have no label on their page at all.

Fixing _from_figure_line to read `diagram` with a `figure_label` child would
recover 49 pairs. Fixing it to read proximity would reach ~13,000 and would be
a heuristic, not a schema. That choice belongs to 253 and is measured there;
this task establishes only that the literals are not dead and what the real
shapes are.
