FINDING — the two Fong-Spivak books (the handover's "~96% missing")
  evidence-formula.pdf came out at 22 and 7 pages for 4,059 and 4,028 rows,
  with 100/105 xelatex errors left. Every failing row is
  \boldsymbol{\operatorname{...}}, which under `bm` raises "Improper
  alphabetic constant" and then a fatal \@savebox error that truncates the
  document. compile_fixpoint demoted one such row per pass and its cap of 6
  left the seventh. Fixed at the source (a79e0e7): renderable() rewrites the
  form to \operatorname{\mathbf{...}} (0 errors, verified standalone); the cap
  is 40; the gate refuses a log with remaining errors. After: 156 and 157
  pages, 0 errors, both READY.
  A second lesson from the same log: a bare "^! " count is not an error
  count — an Underfull \hbox trace wrapped a row's "yes!" onto a line start.
  The gate pairs "! " with an l.N line within 8 lines (rule 11).
