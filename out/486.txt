44 — A DISPLAY WITH CONTENT OUTSIDE IT. ALSO OURS, AND THE PROOF IS THEIR OWN LINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All 44 are on `source=mathpix` models and all 44 have a crop URL. What sits
outside the display:

  float furniture (caption / captionsetup / end{figure})   42
  text or prose                                             2

So they are 481's figure-wrapper class seen from the other side.

The ownership question is settled the same way 481 settled the `\]`: by
reading what MathPix actually returned. For 2005.05886_EQ0001, page 2,
confidence 1.000, their line `e453e20fa71e` (type=math, region 756x43) is:

  '\[\nR \longrightarrow P\left|P^{-} \quad B \longrightarrow A\right| \geq_{1} R \quad C \longrightarrow A \mid \geq_{n} R\n\]'

That is the display and nothing else. No `\begin{figure}`, no
`\captionsetup`, no `\caption`. And their markdown keeps them apart too —

  $$\nR \longrightarrow P… \geq_{n} R\n$$\nFigure 1: Syntax of $D L$-Lite…

the display as `$$…$$`, the caption as the prose line after it.

Our tiddler's `latex` for that object is

  \begin{figure} \[ R \longrightarrow … \] \captionsetup{labelformat=empty} \caption{Figure 1: Syntax of \(D L\)-Lite …

The float furniture is not in their line, not in their markdown, and is in
our field. OUR ASSEMBLY put it there. The 756x43 crop is one line tall; the
value it is attached to contains a whole figure.

WHY THIS IS NOT THE MAIL
------------------------
The case for sending it was that a crop showing two separate equations
returned as one value is a segmentation error the admin can check against his
own logs, and the mirror of 465's `\hbox{` case.

It is not that. There are no such rows. And for the 44 that do involve
MathPix, their own line and their own markdown both keep the display and the
caption apart — the gluing happened downstream of them, in our assembly.
Sending it would ask the admin to explain a defect his logs would show he did
not commit, which is worse than sending nothing: the correspondence has five
withdrawn claims already and each cost credibility.

465's `\hbox{` case remains genuinely theirs. This one is genuinely ours, and
the same instrument that found the first is what shows the difference.
