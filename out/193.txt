THREE ATTEMPTS AT PER-PAGE PAIRING, AND WHY I ABANDONED THEM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A global count is coarse: it cannot say WHICH pages are sound, and a document
with four bad pages loses its other 477. So I tried pairing per page, taking
the identifier count for each page from the rendered PDF's own text.

It failed three times, each time fixing one document and breaking others:

   1. `\ident\{([^&\n]+?)\}` stopped at the first `}`, which belongs to the
      \allowbreak{} INSIDE the identifier. Every count came out zero.
   2. Anchoring on the identifier suffix fixed 2103.01507 (198 of 232 pages)
      and broke the other four, whose long bibkeys are split across lines in
      the rendered text so the prefix never appears contiguously.
   3. Counting the suffix `(?:EQ|FO|TAB|DIA)\d{4}` validated exactly against
      the prefix count on 2103.01507 — and still produced 110 of 232, worse
      than attempt 2.

Three fixes, three regressions, one of them contradicting a validation I had
just run. That is the signature of guessing, so I stopped rather than trying a
fourth.
