THE FLAG
--------
  pdfdrill inkreport <pdf> [--profile internal|published]

  profile              formula rule   the formulas section holds
  internal (default)   unresolved     the rows that did not render
  published            none           nothing; the section is omitted

`internal` is the default, so a hand-run gets the working view without being
asked. `published` is what 463 and the publish path use.

IT SETS THE FORMULA RULE AND NOTHING ELSE
-----------------------------------------
Not the paper, not the legend, not the crop column, not the confidence bounds.
That is the claim in the name, so it is checked rather than asserted:
`test_a_profile_sets_the_formula_rule_AND_NOTHING_ELSE` parses `cmd_inkreport`
and fails if `rule` is ever read anywhere except as the `formulas=` argument
of a call — twice, plus the one line that prints it. A profile that quietly
set a second thing would turn "which profile built this" from one field into a
question about behaviour.

BOTH BUILDS TAKE THE SAME RULE, AND THAT IS NOT TIDINESS
--------------------------------------------------------
inkreport builds twice: step 2 is the measure build, step 3 measures its
pages, step 4 joins the result back onto the reading build at step 5. Setting
the rule on the reading build alone would produce a measurement of pages that
no longer exist — the same class of defect as 400, where a forgotten
`--no-legend` stamped the wrong phase and the measurement was refused five
steps later. The test asserts both builds get it.

OMITTING THE SECTION DOES NOT OMIT THE COUNT
--------------------------------------------
This is the one thing I added beyond the ask, because `none` as written would
have hidden a fact along with the rows.

Under `published` the header line now reads

  4,061 inline formulas (section omitted; 1 did not render), 262 display
  equations, 68 tables, 12 unrecovered image regions.

and, when nothing is owed,

  213 inline formulas (section omitted; all rendered), 62 display equations, …

Dropping the rows is a judgement about what deserves a page. Dropping the fact
would be a claim of cleanliness the document has not earned — and 7 of the 22
documents do have unresolved formula rows (460: 1, 1, 4, 1 across four
documents, 37,624 rows total).

WHY published OMITS RATHER THAN RESTRICTS
-----------------------------------------
report.pdf puts two independent instruments in adjacent cells — MathPix's
confidence and inkdrill's residual — on every equation, table and
image-region row. A formula row carries neither: no confidence, no residual,
no scan crop. It is the one part of the artefact that is not QC, and across
the 22 published documents it was 37,624 rows of it.

The 7 rows that do not render stay visible in the internal build, and their
count stays in the published header. If you would rather the published report
carry those 7 rows, `published` becomes `"unresolved"` — one word in
`INKREPORT_PROFILES`, and the test that pins the mapping is the only other
thing that changes.

THE ARTEFACT SAYS WHICH PROFILE BUILT IT
----------------------------------------
`report.build.json` gained `formula_rule`. 456 had to infer a report's shape
by parsing its own `.tex` — counting `p{` in a longtable column spec — to
answer "does this one have six-column tables". A field is cheaper and does not
lie.
