IS \mathfrak AVAILABLE? YES — AND NOT FOR THE REASON GIVEN
----------------------------------------------------------
Compiled against our own preamble:

  $\mathfrak{g}+\mathfrak{m}$        OK

It does not need eufrak. `\mathfrak` is declared by **amsfonts**, and
`amssymb` loads amsfonts — so it arrives through a package we already have,
under a name that is not in the list. That is why 2103.01507's `\mathfrak{m}`
drew no warning, and why gilmore's `\Re e \mathfrak{g}` in 477 got as far as
a `\noalign` error rather than an undefined control sequence.

Two more that are present for the same indirect reason, checked the same way:

  $\mathscr{L}$                      OK   (mathrsfs, named)
  $\llbracket x \rrbracket$          OK   (stmaryrd, named)
