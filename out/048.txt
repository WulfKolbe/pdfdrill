THE THREE SIGNALS, AND WHAT EACH ONE IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   SLT flagged   the SLT edit distance between MathPix's LaTeX and the
                 AUTHOR'S is > 0 (out/041). Text against text; never ink.
   ink flagged   the ink distance between the report's Rendered cell and
                 its Scan cell exceeds 6, the measured p95 of the same 208
                 expressions through two rasterizers (inkdrill out/042).
                 Render against scan; never text.
   conf flagged  MathPix's own confidence for that math line is < 0.1
                 (the threshold used in out/043).

   They are three different questions and the table is worth building
   precisely because they need not agree.

      They agree on 27 of 53 rows and disagree on 26.

      That is the expected shape, not a fault: 'SLT only' is a text-side
      difference that renders identically (a \mathcal read as \mathscr, an
      \begin{aligned} the author did not write), and 'ink only' is a
      rendering difference in an equation whose LaTeX matches the author's
      exactly - a font or spacing effect on the page, invisible to a source
      comparison. Neither signal is a substitute for the other, and 26 rows
      would be missed by whichever one you picked alone.

      confidence contributes none of them.
