573 — the duplication, and a correction to 570 that matters more
================================================================

WHERE THE PAIR COMES FROM
--------------------------
Not one producer emitting twice. TWO producers seeing one heading:

  added_by=None              the line-based producer, carrying cmd,
                             line_index, section_number
  added_by=heading_promote   `heading_cleanup` promoting a PARAGRAPH that
                             begins with \section{...}

`heading_promote` already guards against duplicates by normalised caption,
and that guard CANNOT SEE THESE, because the two producers disagree about
where the caption ends: johnston flow 2 is "Introduction" and flow 3 is
"Introduction to Linear and Matrix". Different strings, so `norm not in
existing` was true and a second Section was added. The pairing was total
because the disagreement is systematic.

FIXED by de-duplicating on POSITION, which is what both producers agree on:
a heading whose flow index is already occupied by a Section, or one position
after, is that Section. Nothing in the tree reads `added_by ==
heading_promote`, so no consumer depended on the pair. 2,936 tests pass.
