A COORDINATE BUG, found because the count disagreed with 356's

  The first run reported 4,610 against 356's measured 4,529.
  `texgraphics.calls` strips comments and reports positions in the
  STRIPPED text; `extract_graphics` reports them in the RAW body. The
  containment test compared the two directly -- different coordinate
  systems -- so an inclusion preceded by a comment was misclassified, and
  every Picture would have been placed at the wrong point in the document
  flow. Both sides are now computed on the same stripped text and the
  positions mapped back. An 81-object disagreement was the only symptom.
