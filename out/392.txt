392 — the ceiling: the recovery prompt where the answer is known
================================================================

WHY THIS RUNS BEFORE 391 REPORTS ANYTHING

A rescued MathPix figure has no reference. Nobody can say whether the
recovery is right, which is where 281 has sat all week. A DaTikZ row has the
author's own TikZ, so the same prompt can be scored against a known answer,
and the distance distribution here is what makes a distance in 391 mean
something.

Same prompt for both, deliberately (tools/recover_prompt.py). If they
differed, this would measure a different question.

THE RUN

  20 rows, minimax/minimax-m3 via Novita, max_tokens 16000
  19 compiled, 0 failed to compile, 1 empty reply
  engines: xelatex 19, pdflatex 0
  seconds: median 5, max 141

THE FIXED PREAMBLE DID ITS JOB. Zero compile failures in 19 replies. 337's
one error was a missing \usepackage, and asking a model for its own preamble
makes every reply a fresh chance to reintroduce it. Loading the libraries
unconditionally costs nothing per reply and removes that failure class for
all of them at once.

16000 TOKENS IS STILL NOT ALWAYS ENOUGH. DTZ00000 returned empty with
finish_reason="length" — the same shape 4000 produced, one budget up. It is
the most complex row in the sample (352 components in 383's pair table). So
the empty reply is a budget, not a refusal, and the budget scales with the
figure.

THE DISTANCES

                 n   median  mean   max   classes
  vs AUTHOR     19      4     8.5    90   clean 2, noise 14, weak 1, component 2
  vs PNG        19      2     7.4    90   clean 4, noise 13, weak 1, component 1

vs AUTHOR is our compile of the reply against our compile of the author's
code — same engine, same renderer, same dpi, so the difference is the
model's drawing. vs PNG is against the dataset's own 448x448 render and is
looser: 383 measured our renderer against theirs on IDENTICAL code and it is
not zero, so that column carries the renderer floor as well.

THE POPULATION IS NOT ALL FIGURES, and that had to be split
------------------------------------------------------------

Many of the first DaTikZ rows are one-label fragments, not figures. DTZ00003
is a single node reading "Prediction"; DTZ00002 is "Semantic LOSS" beside
"Ground Truth". Reproducing those is trivial and a distance of 0 on them
says nothing about recovering a figure.

  author components <= 5   n= 6   d median 2.0   (2 clean, 4 noise)
  author components >  5   n=13   d median 5.0   11 of 13 within d<=7

The ceiling that matters is the second line: on 13 substantial figures, 11
land inside the noise band.

WHAT A DISTANCE MEANS — CHECKED BY EYE, BOTH ENDS
--------------------------------------------------

The five-tuple is a structural CENSUS (components, holes, stacked, centred,
offset). Two different pictures with the same counts score 0, so the number
had to be checked against the pictures before it could be used as a ceiling.

  d = 4, DTZ00018 (author 96 components, model 96). Looked at both renders:
  the same commutative diagram — four objects, three labelled arrows,
  identical layout. A low distance IS a correct recovery here.

  d = 90, DTZ00001 (author 87 -> model 169). Looked at both renders: ALSO
  correct. Same seven nodes, same square, same triangle, same pair of dotted
  arcs. The picture is right.

  The distance is DOT DENSITY. A dotted stroke is not one component, it is
  one component per dot, and the model chose a different dot spacing. 82 of
  the 90 is comp_delta and essentially all of it is the dotting.

So the five-tuple OVER-PENALISES dashed and dotted art, by a factor that has
nothing to do with whether the figure was recovered. That is the same shape
as the earlier finding that ink >= 6 with confidence >= 0.9 is a
rendering-difference detector rather than a defect detector: the instrument
is measuring a real difference that is not the difference being asked about.

DTZ00001 is the only row in this sample where it fires — the other
component-flagged row, DTZ00002, has comp_delta 3 on a two-label fragment.

WHAT 391 MAY AND MAY NOT CONCLUDE FROM THIS

  MAY: on a substantial figure, this prompt reaches d <= 7 against a known
  answer 11 times in 13. A rescue landing in that band is doing what the
  prompt does at its best.

  MAY NOT: read a large distance as a failed recovery without looking at the
  stroke style. A dotted figure can be recovered correctly and score 90.
  Any 391 row flagged `component` needs an eye on it before it is called
  wrong.

  MAY NOT: read d = 0 as "identical picture". It means identical structural
  census. On the trivial rows that is nearly free.

  AND: budget for empty replies proportional to figure complexity. The one
  row that returned nothing is the most complex one in the sample.
