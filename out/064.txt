WHAT confidence_rate IS NOT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   confidence_rate never drops below 0.9306 anywhere in the corpus; its
   median is 1.0000 and NOTHING would flag at 0.1. It is a per-token mean,
   so a line can be badly misread and still score high on it - EQ0516 reads
   0.9921 while being 60% wrong. Only `confidence` is usable as a flag, and
   a threshold applied to the wrong one of the two would silently flag
   nothing forever while looking like a working check.

HOW IT SHOWS, AND WHY THERE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   \lowconf{0.041} renders as a bold [conf 0.041] in the IDENTIFIER column.
   That column already holds machine keys, so the Source, Rendered and Scan
   cells stay byte-identical - the consumer's per-column ink probe keeps
   working and an unchanged column stays a free control (rule 16). A test
   asserts the row remainder after the first & is identical with and
   without the flag.

   The flag is ADVISORY: the row still renders. A doubted reading is still
   the best reading available, and suppressing it would make a masked
   failure look like a clean report (rule 11).

   conf_flag() returns '' for an absent or unparseable value. Absence of a
   reading is not evidence of a good one, and it must never render as
   confident.
