   including the 'a' that should be a 0.

   distance and a confident, false conclusion that MathPix matched the

   not a tar at all but a single gzipped SpEcxp.tex.

   Anything in this repo that reaches for "the tex source" of a MathPix
   document needs to know which of those two files it has.
