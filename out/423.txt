THE REST SAY "(not rendered)", AND THAT IS THE HONEST STATE

A `tabular` inside `\FitMath{$\displaystyle …$}` is not mathematics, and
`has_bare_align_marker` refuses a cell whose `&` or `\\` sits at brace depth
zero — which is most tabulars, since that is what a tabular IS. The guard
fires and the row shows source-only, exactly as a malformed equation does.
That is a state the reader already knows how to read, and it is not an
apology: the source is right there in the column beside it.

Raising that rate is 424's job — compile the tabular STANDALONE, as a
document rather than as inline math, and put the result in the column this
task created. The column now exists and 99.1% of published table rows have
something to put in it.
