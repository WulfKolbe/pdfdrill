619 — the pairing drift, fixed one-to-one

THE DIAGNOSIS. The drift went BOTH WAYS, which is what named the cause:

    0902.0431   988 measured against 1014   26 SHORT
    johnston   1398 measured against 1405    7 SHORT
    lyche       907 measured against  905    2 OVER

Containment alone is many-to-one in both directions. A band with two lattice
rows inside it took both; a band with none was silently absent and the count
simply came up short. The same code produced an under-count and an over-count
on different documents.

THE FIX. Each band takes the single lattice row whose centre is nearest its
own, and each lattice row is claimed at most once. A repeated header still
goes unclaimed because nothing contains it — that property is untouched.

AND THE TWO REAL GAPS ARE NOW NAMED RATHER THAN COLLAPSED INTO A REFUSAL:

    straddling    a row broken across a page bounds no rectangle on either
    undetected    the rectangle is right and the lattice found nothing in it
                  — 0707.4470's pages 1-2 were the first of these and they
                  are still unexplained

The old check turned both into one message, "N data rows against M
identifiers", which named a drift of 2 to 26 without saying which rows or
why. What is still checked exactly is the property inkconvert depends on:
every measured row carries its own identifier and no identifier claims two
rows. A duplicate is refused by name.
