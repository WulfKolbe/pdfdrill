551 — report.tables.json is empty in 21 of 21
=============================================

WHY IT IS NOT BUILT FROM tables.json: THEY ARE DIFFERENT ARTEFACTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  report.tables.json   the BOUNDARIES OF THE LONGTABLES IN THE REPORT (321)
                       — caption, column count, whether the legend repeats,
                       whether the header repeats, and the identifier list.
                       `inkmeasure` reads it to segment the RASTERISED
                       REPORT into rows.
  tables.json          the DOCUMENT's own tables, from `pdfdrill tables`,
                       the keyless pdfplumber extraction. Up to 103 rows.

Neither is derived from the other and neither should be. They describe
different objects and the names are the whole confusion: one is about the
report, the other about the document.

WHY IT IS EMPTY
---------------
Every `tables_manifest.append(...)` in `build_report` sat inside the
`if not findings:` block — lines 3132, 3150, 3198 and 3379. A findings
build never reaches any of them, so the list stays `[]` and the manifest is
written EMPTY over one that previously described the report's sections.

516 rebuilt all 21 published documents in the findings shape. That is why
it is 21 of 21 and not a handful.

WHAT IT BROKE
-------------
`inkmeasure` raises

    MeasureRefused("report.tables.json names no %r table")

without a matching record. So a re-measurement of the published shape could
not run — which is 540, and it was blocked by this rather than by anything
about the measurement.

THE FIX
-------
A findings build now records its OWN longtables: one `_table_record` per
non-empty section, with the identifiers it actually emits. Corrected
contributes two identifiers per pair, `<id> (was)` and `<id> (now)`,
because that is what the section prints.

  johnston, --pages 10, findings shape:
    Corrected                 rows 16   cols 6
    Unresolved                rows  7   cols 6
    Flagged, not acted on     rows 37   cols 6
    total identifier rows     60

  BradleyGastaldiTerilla2023: 0 tables, correctly — all four of its
  sections are empty, so there is no longtable to describe.

A test builds a two-row findings report and asserts the manifest names its
section and that `rows == len(identifiers)` on every record.

Tests: 2,931 passing.

NOT DONE
--------
The 21 published manifests are still empty on disk. They are rewritten by
the rebuild in 540, not by this task.
