337 - can a model reproduce the annotation over a figure?
====================================================================

SEVEN PAIRS. Each is a base figure file, the MathPix crop of how it appears
on the page, and the author's own tikzpicture compiled standalone as the
reference. The right answer is known by construction (332's trick), so
nothing is joined and nothing is judged by eye.

  MiniMax-m3 via Novita, both images per call, asked for the annotation
  TikZ only -- the \includegraphics line is given in the prompt.

THE POSITION STATEMENT, and it had to be re-measured first

  342 classified these 7 as 5 overlapping / 2 around. That was wrong for
  three rows. My extent came from `scale=`/`width=` arithmetic, and for
  width=\linewidth I had hardcoded 'a Tufte text block, ~12 cm'. Measured
  against pdfplumber's actual image rectangles: mpsapprox2 340 pt computed
  against 143 measured, psi_N2 261 against 142, pt2 408 against 222. All
  three flip from overlapping to around on the measured extent.

  The split is 4 AROUND and 3 OVERLAPPING, which is what you said it was.
  Every position below comes from a rectangle measured in the compiled
  PDF, not from my arithmetic.

THE SPLIT IS THE MEASUREMENT

  around       n=4   distances [2, 6, 10, 22]   median 10
  overlapping  n=3   distances [72, 180, 216]   median 180

  An eighteenfold difference in median ink distance between a row whose
  position was STATED and one where it could not be. Every `around` row
  lands in 2..22; every `overlapping` row in 72..216. The two populations
  do not touch.

  So a stated direction helps, and it helps decisively. And an overlapping
  row is not recoverable from the images alone -- the model can see that
  something was added but not where it goes, and 3 of 7 here are that
  case. 342 measured 59% of the corpus that way before its own extent bug
  was found; the corrected share on these seven is 43%.

THE CLOSEST REPRODUCTION

  bothways, distance 6, 57 components against 57:
    model  : \node at (-2.00,0) {density operator}; \node at (2.20,0) {probability distribution};
    author : \node at (-2,0) {density operator}; \node at (2.2,0) {probability distribution};

  It recovered the author's own coordinates to two decimals. That is what
  a stated position buys: the prompt gave the image extent and the offsets,
  and the model placed the labels where the author placed them.

ONE RETRY, AND WHY

  pt2 returned an EMPTY reply with finish_reason='length' at a 4,000-token
  budget. minimax-m3 is a reasoning model that spends completion tokens
  before emitting a character -- refine._novita_chat's docstring says so.
  Retried at 16,000 it answered in 82 characters and compiled. An empty
  reply from a budget is not a refusal and must not be scored as one.

out/337.replies.json carries every prompt, reply, render and ink row.
