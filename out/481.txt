2 — CODE IN A MATHS ENVIRONMENT: 5 rows, 2 documents. MathPix's.
--------------------------------------------------------------
This one cost three wrong proxies and the wrong numbers are worth recording,
because each looked plausible:

  any `\backslash` in the value          2,516 rows   WRONG
      — 2,285 of them are `A \backslash B`, set difference. Legitimate.
  `\backslash` before any letter           231 rows   STILL WRONG
      — `M \backslash L` is set difference with a one-letter operand.
  `\backslash` before `\text` or `\mathrm`  ~60 rows  STILL WRONG
      — `\Gamma \backslash \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbf{Z})` is a quotient, and
        `\mathrm` is an operand font, not a prose font.
  `\backslash` before `\text{`, or escaped braces inside `\text{}`,
  or `\verb`/verbatim                        5 rows   the answer

  OMDoc (kohlhase) EQ0004  p297  conf 0.035
     \backslash \text { CSum\{k\}1\infty\{\Cexp\{x\}k\} instead of } …
  2503.21383  FO0046-0049   \verb|<think>| \verb|<\think>| \verb|<answer>| …

The row is not mathematics: it is TeX or markup being DISCUSSED, typeset as
though it were an equation. Same class as 465's kohlhase p215, where three
verbatim listings of `$\{\,p\mid\hbox{…}\,\}$` were read as maths — and note
that those three do NOT match this signature, so 5 is a floor, not a total.
This class has no clean corpus-wide regex; it needs the crop.

3 — UNESCAPED UNDERSCORE: 85 rows, 40 documents. MathPix's.
----------------------------------------------------------
YES — the raw value carries `_` inside `\text{}`. kohlhase EQ0001, p060,
confidence 0.863:

  \forall M . \operatorname{magma}(M) \Leftrightarrow \exists E, F . M=(E, F)
    \wedge \text { law_of_composition }(E, F)

In text mode `_` is illegal and the row dies with "Missing $ inserted". The
identifier `law_of_composition` is printed on the page with literal
underscores; MathPix transcribed it faithfully into `\text{}` and did not
escape it. Faithful and unusable.

  2512.21782v1 10, 1710.04811 9, Geometrie und GPS 6, Autonomous Driving 4,
  math_0202059 4, 2606.13669 3, … 34 more documents

The repair is one substitution — `_` -> `\_` inside a text run — and needs no
model.

5 — \] INSIDE A MATHS VALUE: 1,168 rows, 178 documents. BOTH, and the split
   is checkable.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
mielke EQ0190 p080 and EQ0453 p151, both at confidence 1.000. Their values:

  \[ T_{G}: \quad T_{g} \psi(m)=D(…) \psi(g^{-1} m) . \] }
  \[ L_{a b}^{(\star)}:=\frac{1}{2} \varepsilon_{a b c d} L^{c d}=L_{a b} . \] }

MathPix's own lines.json for those two lines reads, verbatim:

  '\\[\nT_{G}: \\quad T_{g} \\psi(m)=D\\left(…\\right) \\psi\\left(g^{-1} m\\right) .\n\\]'
  '\\[\nL_{a b}^{(\\star)}:=\\frac{1}{2} \\varepsilon_{a b c d} L^{c d}=L_{a b} .\n\\]'

So:

  the `\[` and `\]`   THEIRS. It is how MathPix delimits a display-math line
                      in its `text` field. Not a defect on their side — it is
                      their format.
  carrying it into    OURS. `latex` is supposed to hold mathematics, and the
  the `latex` field   report wraps it in `$\displaystyle …$`; `\[` inside
                      `$…$` is illegal. Stripping the delimiter is our job
                      and we do not do it.
  the trailing ` }`   OURS. It is not in MathPix's text at all.

out/056 already counted this as 41 rows on a 49-document sample and called it
"nested display delimiter". Corpus-wide it is 1,168 rows in 178 documents —
the largest of the five by a factor of 14, and the only one where the repair
is unambiguously ours and needs no model, no crop and no call.
