130 — pre-call gate: skip a row whose baseline is already below the noise floor
===============================================================================

THE GATE
--------
   Before spending an LLM call on a row, compute the baseline ink distance
   between the MathPix render and the scan crop. If it is below the noise
   floor, skip: the render already matches the scan to within measurement
   error, so there is no defect to recover and a variant can only hold the
   row level or make it worse.

   This is not a heuristic. out/121 measured it directly: on rows already
   below the floor, variant C was better on 0, worse on 3, same on 2 — every
   single call spent on that group was wasted or harmful.

THE FLOOR
---------
   6 = rasterizer p95 (the same image rendered twice)
   7 = render-vs-scan, measured on 052-slt-zero.tsv

   7 is the right one here because that is the comparison being made. Both
   are reported below so the choice is visible rather than assumed.

WHAT THE GATE BUYS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   At floor 7, 198 of 763 scorable rows (26%) are skipped before any
   call is made. On a two-variant trial that is 396 calls not spent, and by
   out/121's measurement those calls could not have helped: the rows they
   would have gone to were better on 0, worse on 3, same on 2.

   The gate does NOT claim those rows are correct. It claims the render and
   the scan agree to within the noise of the measurement, so THIS instrument
   cannot tell them apart and re-transcription cannot be scored on them. A row
   below the floor may still be wrong in a way ink distance cannot see —
   out/119 found the disagreements are largely typographic, and out/128 found
   a class (hallucinated CJK) that never renders at all and so has no ink.
