131 — retire variant E
======================

DECISION
--------
Variant E is retired. C remains the working prompt: BASE + the MathPix prior.

E = C plus one sentence, added in 118, describing the single defect out/116
actually located:

    "A short horizontal bar in this image may be a minus sign, a hyphen or a
     dash rather than a fraction bar. Write \frac only where there is content
     both above and below the bar; where there is not, transcribe the bar as
     the operator or punctuation it is."

THE THREE TRIALS
----------------
   trial   band          n   C median   E median   verdict
   118     conf < 0.1   19      -8         -8      indistinguishable
   115     conf > 0.9   20      +0         —       E not run
   121     0.1–0.5      20      +0         +0      indistinguishable
   121     0.1–0.5      20      +0         +0      re-run, clean columns

(medians over compiled replies; negative = closer to the scan)

E was never measurably better than C on any band. In 118 the two medians were
identical at -8. In 121 both are +0. The sentence has now been tested on 39
rows across two bands and has not once moved the median.

WHY THE FIRST 121 DID NOT SETTLE IT
-----------------------------------
The first 121 run reported "indistinguishable" from an E column holding 14 of
20 replies — six calls had died on NetworkBlocked, and the missing third was
never accounted for. A comparison whose two columns have different n is not a
comparison, so that run could not retire anything. It was re-run with retry and
backoff: 40 of 40 calls scored, zero failures, both columns n=20 on the same 20
rows. Only then was the result usable as evidence.

THE REGRESSION
--------------
   sigma12-060_EQ0006   confidence 0.457   baseline ink 20
        C   19   (-1, unchanged in practice)
        E  143   (+123)

E pushed that row roughly seven times further from the scan than leaving it
alone. It is the largest single divergence between the two variants in any
trial, and it runs the wrong way.

One row is not a population, and this is not a claim that E is reliably harmful
— across 121's 20 rows E was worse on 4 and better on 3, which is noise. The
regression matters because of what it is paired with: a variant that has never
demonstrated a gain has no offsetting benefit to weigh against a tail risk of
this size. An extra sentence that cannot be shown to help, and can occasionally
do this, is not worth carrying.

WHY THE SENTENCE WAS REASONABLE AND STILL FAILED
------------------------------------------------
118 was deliberately careful about this sentence. out/116 found rules whose
bands are EMPTY on both sides — a component the aspect gate accepted as a rule
with no numerator and no denominator, i.e. a dash or minus sign mistaken for a
fraction bar. It found NO missing bars, so E was written to describe only the
defect that was measured, not the one that would have been easy to assert
("watch for fraction bars you may have missed" would have been an invented
hint).

So the sentence was grounded in a real, located defect and still bought
nothing. That is the useful result: a prompt addition being TRUE about the
corpus does not make it USEFUL to the model. The defect E describes is real and
rare, and telling the model about it did not change what it produced.

WHAT REPLACES IT
----------------
Nothing. C is the working variant. The evidence across 113 / 115 / 118 / 121 is
that the MathPix prior is the whole of the effect, and that the effect tracks
how wrong the existing render already is rather than the confidence value: the
five largest recoveries in 121 all had large baselines, and rows already inside
the noise floor could only be held level or made worse. That is what 130
addresses — gating on baseline ink before spending a call — not another prompt
variant.

E remains in the trial harness as a defined variant so these results stay
reproducible; it is retired from use, not deleted from the record.
