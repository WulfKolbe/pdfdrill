I EXPECTED THIS TO BE A SIZE SAVING ONLY, AND I WAS WRONG
----------------------------------------------------------
`pagesel` discards pages at SHIPOUT — the document is still TYPESET in
full — so I wrote in the constant's docstring that the saving would be in
what reaches the PDF and "a claim that it makes the build ten times faster
would be wrong". It is 10.3x. The embedding and PDF-writing dominate, not
the typesetting: a page that is never shipped never has its crops written
out. The comment now carries the measurement instead of my reasoning.

A SLOT THAT BROKE SEVEN CALLERS
--------------------------------
Adding `%(pagesel)s` to PREAMBLE broke every existing `PREAMBLE % {...}`
with KeyError — two tests and three tools among them. A preamble slot is an
OPTION and a caller that does not know about it should get the default, not
a crash. `report_tex.preamble(**slots)` now fills missing slots with "" via
a dict subclass, and the call sites route through it. The next slot added
will not do this again.
