HOW THEY WOULD BE RESOLVED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Matching is by exact member name, then by suffix, then by basename. The
   suffix and basename steps are needed because \lstinputlisting paths are
   relative to the MAIN FILE's directory, not the tarball root, and a paper
   whose main.tex sits in a subdirectory writes paths that never match a
   member name literally.

   Basename matching is the loosest step and can collide when two
   directories hold the same filename. It is used last and, on this
   population, is never reached: every hit above matched on name or suffix.
