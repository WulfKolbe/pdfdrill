ONE THING THE STRUCTURE DOES NOT DO
------------------------------------
The tar is keyed by BIBKEY, as specified — but for 9 of the 21 the bibkey
is NOT the folder name on disk:

  cardona-qft-methods      -> Geometric, Algebraic and Topological Methods…
  johnston-linear-matrix…  -> Introduction to Linear and Matrix Algebra (…)
  voloshin-hypergraph      -> Introduction to Graph and Hypergraph Theory…
  …and six more

462 renamed the identifier stems and not the folders, so "a path in the tar
is a path on disk" holds for 12 of 21 and requires the bibkey→folder map
for the other 9. The map is derivable from each `<bibkey>.drill.json` in
the tar, and stating it here is cheaper than an auditor discovering it.
