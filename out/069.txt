THE SIGN PATTERN, WHICH SEPARATES THEM CLEANLY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Among the ten, the two content errors are the only unshaded rows where
   BOTH deltas are negative and the component delta is large:

     typography    components  -1 .. -3     holes  +20 .. +35
     content       components  -17          holes   -7, -8
     artifact      components  -111 .. -177 holes   -3 .. -10

   The sign is the discriminator, and it has a plain meaning: holes POSITIVE
   means the render resolved more detail than the scan (a rendering
   difference), while components NEGATIVE and large means the scan carries
   marks the render has none for - MathPix dropped something, or the region
   caught something that is not the equation.

   Shading produces the same sign pattern at much larger magnitude, which is
   why 068's detector has to run first rather than the sign being used alone.

CORRECTION (070) - THE ARROWS IN EQ0019 WERE NEVER DROPPED
==============================================================================
   The verdict on 0911.3722_EQ0019 above says MathPix drops all three "=>".
   It does not. The stored LaTeX contains all three, as raw U+21D2 inside
   \text{}, and xelatex cannot set that character:

     Missing character: There is no => (U+21D2) in font [lmroman10-regular]

   The arrows are lost at RENDER time, in our own standalone pipeline, and
   the warning is not an error so the compile fixpoint never demotes the
   row. I read the rendered image and charged the OCR for a loss the
   renderer caused.

   EQ0019 IS still a content error: the leading "Pack" of the first term and
   the trailing "-complete." are genuinely absent from the LaTeX. The
   classification in the table stands; the mechanism is truncation at a
   boundary, not lost operators. See out/070.txt.
