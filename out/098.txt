THE RULE FROM 069 NEEDS A QUALIFIER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   out/069 read a large negative component delta as the signature of a
   content error - the scan carrying marks the render cannot account for.
   Row 8 is a counterexample: components can also go negative because the
   render MERGED glyphs that the scan keeps separate.

   The two are distinguishable by the hole column. Missing content removes
   marks and their counters together, so holes go negative with components
   (rows 4-7, 9-11 all have holes < 0). Merging joins outlines without
   destroying counters, so holes stay put (row 8 is exactly 0).

   comp << 0 AND holes < 0   -> something is absent
   comp << 0 AND holes ~ 0   -> something is touching

   On these 11 that separates 10 from 1 with no overlap. It is the rule
   out/100 uses to classify the whole set, and row 8 is why it needs two
   signs rather than one.
