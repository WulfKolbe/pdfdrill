WHY A REBUILD WAS NOT ENOUGH
-----------------------------
679 withdrew mielke-geometrodynamics_EQ0378's refinement — a sentence of
English prose published in place of its equation for seventeen days. That
edits the model, so the ink's recorded `model_sha256` stopped describing the
model on disk and `publishready`'s timestamp gate refused. Correctly: a
measurement that names the model it was taken against is worth exactly as much
as that name being true.

THREE ARTEFACTS THAT MUST MOVE TOGETHER
