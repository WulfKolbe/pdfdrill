055 - every transformation of ~ in LaTeX values, on the ingest and projection paths
==============================================================================

WHY ~ MATTERS HERE
   In LaTeX `~` is a non-breaking space. MathPix emits it INSIDE macro
   arguments — `\mathrm{~d}` where an author writes `\mathrm{d}` — so it is a
   token that appears on one side of a comparison and not the other while
   rendering identically. Whether a path preserves, strips or escapes it
   therefore decides whether a difference is real or manufactured.

THE HEADLINE
   NO path transforms ~ inside a math value. It is preserved verbatim from
   MathPix's lines.json, through the model, into every projection. Every
   transformation found is on PROSE, captions or bibliography text, or is a
   comparison-time normalisation that never touches what is stored.

   The one defect is not a transformation but a DISAGREEMENT: the two
   comparison normalisers apply opposite rules to the same character.

COMPARISON PATH - AND THE DEFECT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   src/pdfdrill/scoring.py:21  _SPACING = ("\,", "\;", "\!", "\:", "\ ", "~")
      RULE  normalize_latex() DELETES ~ before comparing.

   src/pdfdrill/crossref.py::formula_signature -> mathgold.parse_latex_slt
      RULE  ~ becomes its own SLT NODE and is compared.

   Measured on `\mathrm{~d} x` against `\mathrm{d} x`:

      scoring.normalize_latex   ->  'dx' vs 'dx'   equal, similarity 1.0
      crossref SLT distance     ->  2              nodes ['~','d','x'] vs ['d','x']

   So the same pair of values is IDENTICAL to one comparator and two edits
   apart to the other. Neither rule is wrong on its own — a similarity score
   for matching wants spacing gone, and a layout tree may legitimately carry
   spacing — but they are used together: injectlatex PAIRS equations with
   normalize_latex (>= 0.55) and out/040 then SCORES those pairs with the SLT
   distance. A pair can therefore be matched on the understanding that ~ is
   irrelevant and then charged for it.

   Scale, from out/041: 20 of 935 differing pairs involve a ~ that renders
   identically, and neutralising spacing-only tokens collapses 12 of them to
   distance 0. Small, but it is a systematic bias against MathPix, since ~
   inside \mathrm{} is a MathPix habit the authors do not share.

   rule wins changes published numbers (out/040, out/041, out/052). The
   choice should be made deliberately, not as a side effect of an audit.
