THE FALLBACK IS THE FINDING, NOT THE INJECTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Injecting the author's preamble ALONE made things WORSE: 66.7% -> 52.2%. It
rescued the documents whose regions need it —

    2004.05631    3/25 -> 21/25
    2210.06079   16/25 -> 25/25

— and took three others to ZERO:

    2208.01506   24/25 -> 0/25   ! LaTeX Error: Missing \begin{document}.
    2602.23211   16/25 -> 0/25   ! Illegal parameter number in definition of \hgnodea.
    2508.08293v1 17/19 -> 0/19

Both errors are in the extracted PREAMBLE, not in the region: all three
documents compile fine generically. `standalone_preamble()` pulls macro bodies
and declarations out of a preamble, and on these three what it pulls does not
stand on its own — a stray token before `\begin{document}`, a `#1` lifted out
of the definition that gave it meaning.

So neither preamble is right for every document, and which one is right cannot
be decided in advance. It is decided by trying: the author's first, the generic
one on failure. That costs a second compile only on rows that fail the first,
and it yields the per-document maximum — 165, which is exactly
max(generic, author) summed across the sample.

Had I shipped the injection on its own measurement I would have reported "the
author's preamble is the fix" and regressed three documents in ten.

WHAT STILL FAILS — 42 ROWS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2512.15098v4 is 21 of the 42 and fails under every preamble:

    \begin{tikzpicture} \node (img) {\adjustbox{max width=\linewidth,
                                     max height=\ImageMaxHeight}{...}}

`\adjustbox` was simply missing from the guarded package list and is now there.
`\ImageMaxHeight` is an author `\newlength`, and `standalone_preamble` collects
`\newcommand`/`\def`/`\DeclareMathOperator`/`\newcolumntype` but not
`\newlength`/`\setlength`. That is a named, specific extraction gap rather than
a mystery, and it is the next thing to widen if this number matters more.

The remaining 21 are spread thin: author macros the extractor missed, and
figure files the e-print does not contain.

A SECOND DEFECT, FOUND BY DISAGREEING WITH inkdrill
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Converting 2103.01507's measurement, my classes read C 92 against inkdrill's
component 90 — the other four identical. The two are EQ0383 and EQ1005, both
rows the report DEMOTED to `\emph{(not rendered)}`.

A demoted row has no rendering. Measuring it compares italic English against a
formula, which scores a large component delta and was being called `component`
— a defect in the conversion, not in the extraction. inkdrill excludes such
rows; I was counting them.

It matters beyond the tally: `publishready` gates on the p90 component ratio,
and `component_ratio_p90` already takes a `rendered` mask for exactly this
reason — the machinery existed and `inkconvert` was not using it. A false
`component` inflates the number the gate reads.

Fixed: `inkconvert` now marks such rows `rendered: false` with flag
`unrendered`, code `U`. 2103.01507 re-converts to C 90, K 300, N 904, S 15,
W 234 — matching inkdrill's five classes exactly, with U 2 alongside.
1510.06699 has no demoted rows and is unaffected, so its published numbers
stand.

286's OTHER HALF
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
inkdrill has agreed to expose the page detection as
`python3 -m inkdrill pages --pdf report.pdf --columns 6`, JSON on stdout, same
probe and leading-run and coverage and sliver filter — one implementation.

They also caught something I had understated: `reportcompare` does not detect
the header, it skips `ln == 0` on EVERY page unconditionally. That is right for
the equation table, whose `\endhead` repeats the header; on my image table,
where the header prints once, it would drop a DATA row from every page after
the first. So the subcommand takes `--header first|every` as an argument rather
than assuming. They asked me to test both sides of that switch against real
tables once it lands, which I have — the equation table repeats its header and
the image table does not.
