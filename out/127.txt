TWO CORRECTIONS THE RAW COUNT NEEDED
------------------------------------
1. `{}^` — the tight form — occurs ZERO times. Not in the four books, not in
   the comparison, not anywhere in the corpus. MathPix always emits the spaced
   `{ }^`. The task asks for both; one of them does not exist in this data.

2. `{ }^{...}` is NOT always a prescript. It is an empty group carrying a
   superscript, and that construct has two uses:

       { }^{2} \bar{\kappa}          PRESCRIPT — a left superscript
       \mathbf{210}{ }^{\prime}      TRAILING  — a prime on the preceding group

   Only the first is what the task means. Classifying by what precedes the
   occurrence: of 1,397 in the four books, 1,033 are prescripts and 364 are
   trailing. Reporting 1,397 as "prescripts" would have overstated it by 35%.

   This matters most outside the books — see the clustering answer.

BH1 vs BH1org_OCR — THE TWO READINGS AGREE ALMOST EXACTLY
----------------------------------------------------------
   BH1          319 pages  10,216 lines  241 math lines  587 occ  420 prescript
   BH1org_OCR   319 pages  10,217 lines  240 math lines  589 occ  420 prescript

One line apart, one math line apart, two occurrences apart, and IDENTICAL on
the classified prescript count. On this construct the two renderings of the
same book are interchangeable.

That is worth setting against out/104, which found the two genuine
discrete-integral instances in BH1org_OCR and not in BH1. Both can be true:
the readings diverge on a rare glyph-level judgement (a bold script S with
limits) while agreeing completely on a common structural one. "The two
renderings differ" is not a property of the pair — it depends on what is being
counted, and this measurement is the case where they do not differ at all.

has mathematics.
