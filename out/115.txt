WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   It does not show the prompts are useless. It shows they do not help on
   rows where MathPix is confident AND the ink already disagrees - which
   out/067 and out/100 established is mostly typography: 43% of the
   flagged population is 'wrong' by mark shape, 22% merged glyphs, 14%
   pure displacement. A different transcription cannot fix a delimiter
   that is set at a different size.

   The population where they DO help is the one MathPix already flags for
   itself. That is a narrower and more useful claim than 113 alone
   supported: use the model where the OCR reports doubt, not as a general
   second opinion.
