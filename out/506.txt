GRANULARITY IS PER OBJECT, NOT PER BOOK — AND THAT IS A CORRECTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------
My first cut gated coarseness on TOC DENSITY, entries per page, with the
threshold at 0.25. It put voloshin on the wrong side at 0.244 — a book that
scored 74 of 74 in 505.

Density is a proxy for the distance between an object and the heading that
labels it, and that distance is measurable, so it is what is used. Measured
over the six gated books:

  book                     entries   median gap   p90   max
  ----------------------------------------------------------
  lyche                        290          0.0     2    16
  gilmore                      147          1.0     5    16
  mielke                       147          1.0     5    19
  voloshin                      74          2.0     5    17
  seven-sketches               146          1.0    45    65
  johnston                      55          9.0   113   140

A book is not uniformly coarse — it is coarse in the stretches where its TOC
is thin. seven-sketches has a median gap of 1 page and a p90 of 45, so
labelling the whole book coarse would penalise the 90% of it that is fine.
The path therefore carries its own grade:

  gap <= 5 pages    section
  gap <= 30         chapter
  beyond            distant

and every path returns `pages_since_heading` and `starts_on_pdf_page` beside
the title, so a consumer can decline it on its own terms rather than trusting
a flag someone else set. 3,363 objects come back "distant", 2,664 of them
johnston's — a path that is true and too far away to be a section.
