WHY NOT SEND THEM THROUGH THE INK GATE
---------------------------------------
Because the ink gate cannot tell right from wrong. It accepted a sentence of
English prose over an equation on a -96 delta (679), and 17 of 30 recorded
refinements carry that same shape. Passing a repair whose right answer is
already KNOWN through a gate that cannot judge it would stamp
`verified_by: "ink"` on something the ink never established.

So these carry `verified_by: "census"`, and the realization records
`census_names` and the justification string — auditable, not asserted.

THE ROUTE IS NARROW BY CONSTRUCTION
------------------------------------
`glyphcensus.justifies(original, proposed, glyphs)` accepts only when:

  1. every token the repair INTRODUCES is justified — a literal character the
     census places in that region, or a LaTeX command `NAME_TOKENS` ties to a
     glyph name the census reports; and
  2. at most 3 tokens are introduced at all.

(2) is what makes it unable to accept a rewrite. MiniMax's own proposals for
these rows fixed the symbol and appended a neighbouring equation; that tail is
dozens of tokens.

IT REFUSED THE AUTHOR'S OWN FIRST REPAIR
-----------------------------------------
I proposed `\mathbf{匕}` -> `\mathrm{Ł}`. The census refused it: `\mathrm` is
a font change the page cannot justify and that MathPix never got wrong. The
minimal repair replaces the CHARACTER only, `\mathbf{Ł}` — which is the
"change only what is damaged" rule I had written into the propose prompt that
same morning, applied to me, by a check written an hour earlier. Pinned as
`test_a_token_the_page_cannot_be_shown_to_carry_is_refused`.
