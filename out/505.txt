2026-09-02T20:05Z · 1,027 mapped TOC entries across the nine books

FIRST, THE COMPARISON 505 ASKED FOR CANNOT BE MADE
---------------------------------------------------
The task was to check the TOC path against the Section objects the model
holds. Those objects are not a ground truth:

  book                      Section objects   what parent_section resolves to
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  gilmore-lie-groups                      4   '2' (4,222 objects), and the
                                              book's SUBTITLE
  voloshin-hypergraph                     6   'Introduction to Graph' — the
                                              book's own title
  fong-spivak-invitation                 20   '7' (1,796 objects)
  johnston-linear-matrix-algebra         17   'Remark 2.1.1' (1,569) — a
                                              remark, not a section
  lyche-numerical-linear-algebra         64   'Chapter 1', 'Chapter 13'
  kohlhase-omdoc                         38   '14.3 Text Fragments and Their…'

Four Section objects for a 333-page book, six for a 303-page one, and captions
that are bare numbers, a Remark and a subtitle. Comparing a TOC path against
that would measure the noise in the Section extraction, not the accuracy of
the path. So the measurement below uses the PAGE ITSELF as ground truth —
the same move 502 made when a derived statistic disagreed with the pixels.

THE MEASUREMENT: DOES THE MAPPED PAGE ACTUALLY CARRY THAT HEADING?
-------------------------------------------------------------------

that page's text, and look for the entry's title on it and on +-1, +2.

AND 81.8% IS THE WRONG NUMBER TO ACT ON
----------------------------------------
It is the average of six books at 92-100% and two at 3-6%. There is no book
anywhere near 82%. The rule must be gated per book, not applied at a corpus
rate, and the gate is knowable in advance:

  THE TWO FAILING BOOKS ARE EXACTLY THE TWO WITH NO /PageLabels.

  book                          /PageLabels   first labels
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  cardona                       YES           C, i, ii, iii, iv, v
  fong-spivak-invitation        NO            1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6
  fong-spivak-seven-sketches    YES           i, ii, iii, iv, v, vi
  gilmore                       YES           C, c, i, ii, iii, iv
  johnston                      YES           C1, i, iii, iv, v, vii
  kohlhase-omdoc                NO            1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6
  lyche                         YES           C1, i, ii, iii, iv, v
  mielke                        YES           C1, i, ii, iii, iv, v
  voloshin                      YES           cover, coverback, i, ii

pypdf's `page_labels` returns a synthetic `1..N` when the PDF carries no
/PageLabels at all, and 503 read that as "offset 0, constant" — a clean
answer to the wrong question. The two documents 503 reported as having the
tidiest offsets are the two with no labels at all.

Checking `"/PageLabels" in reader.trailer["/Root"]` separates them exactly:
7 books with real labels average 97.7% (833 of 853); the 2 without average
4.2% (7 of 168).

A CORRECTION TO 503
-------------------
503 reported "every TOC entry that parses maps — 927 of 927". The mapping
claim is right; the arithmetic is not. 47+146+147+55+121+290+147+74 = 1,027,
not 927. The correct figure is 1,027 of 1,027.

THE DISAGREEMENTS BY CAUSE
--------------------------
  no /PageLabels                  124 of the 139 misses, in two books.
                                  The printed-to-PDF map is fictional, so
                                  every entry lands wherever the synthetic
                                  numbering puts it. kohlhase's 30 near-misses
                                  at +1/+2 are that fiction being nearly right
                                  by luck, not a small offset error.

  a chapter opening               johnston printed page 1 -> PDF 16 carries
                                  "1. Vectors and Geometry", the chapter
                                  head, while the TOC entry is "1.1 Vectors
                                  and Vector Operations". The page is right
                                  and the heading text is the parent's.
                                  Counted as a hit by the automated test
                                  when the title matches, as a miss when it
                                  does not.

  a heading one page off          voloshin "3.4 Distances in Chordal Graphs"
                                  maps to a page whose running header reads
                                  "Chordal Graphs 59" — the right region, the
                                  heading itself on the neighbouring page.

  a skipped printed page          the smallest cause, and not what was
                                  expected.

THE SKIPPED PAGES, REPORTED SEPARATELY AS ASKED
------------------------------------------------
A math object is AT RISK when a label skip falls between the entry its path
picks and its own page — the printed numbering moved under it.

  book                      skips   math objects   at risk
  ----------------------------------------------------------
  johnston                      7          6,813    1,679   24.6%
  mielke                       12          2,942       32    1.1%
  lyche                        15          5,694        4    0.1%
  all others                    0                       0    0%
  ----------------------------------------------------------
  TOTAL                                   36,623    1,715    4.7%

**Risk tracks TOC DENSITY, not skip count.** lyche has more than twice
johnston's skips and 4 at-risk rows against 1,679, because lyche has 290
mapped entries for 376 pages and johnston has 55 for 492. A dense TOC
re-anchors the path within a page or two of any skip; a sparse one lets a
wrong section run for fifty pages.

And johnston scored 55 of 55 on the heading test. Its 1,679 at-risk rows are
at risk from a cause the entry-level test cannot see: the entries are right
and there are too few of them.

WHAT THIS SAYS FOR 494 AND 487
------------------------------
  use the TOC path      where the PDF carries real /PageLabels — 7 of 9
                        books, 97.7% of entries landing on the page they
                        name
  omit it               where it does not — fong-spivak-invitation,
                        kohlhase-omdoc, and cardona which parses no entries
  and treat as coarse   johnston, where the path is accurate per entry and
                        sparse enough that 24.6% of math objects sit far
                        from the entry that labels them

That is a rule with a test behind it rather than a rate. Your framing was the
right one: 82% corpus-wide would have taught the model a wrong section a
fifth of the time, and the fix is not a better matcher but a gate that knows
which documents to trust.
