WHAT RECORD DOES
An accepted proposal is attached as EVIDENCE, not applied as an overwrite.

   props.latex            unchanged — the original MathPix reading stays
   props.latex_refined    the accepted value, under its own name
   a Realization          stream "refined", role "latex_candidate",
                          provenance "change", carrying verified_by "ink"
                          and the before/after distances

WHY A TWIN FIELD AND NOT AN OVERWRITE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The requirement was that the original stays, and there is a mechanical reason
it must: the MathPix reading is the only thing tying this object to a paid
extraction with a pdf_id and a date. Overwrite it and the provenance chain ends
at a model's guess, verified or not.

But "the original stays" and "the change is visible" pull against each other in
a differ that compares props. If nothing in props changes, modeldiff reports
nothing and the refinement is invisible to the tool built to surface exactly
this. So the accepted value lands in `latex_refined` — a new field, so the diff
has something to show — and the realization repeats the value under the SAME
field name, which is what `_evidence_for` matches on to find the evidence path.

That is why the line above reads `null → …` rather than one value replacing
another. Nothing was replaced. Something was added, and it is signed.

RECONSTRUCTING THE "BEFORE" MODEL
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I did not snapshot the model before recording, so the before-model above is
reconstructed by stripping exactly what record adds — 4 twin props and 4
provenance="change" realizations. That is exact rather than approximate,
because record_one only ever adds those two things and never modifies anything
else; a fresh `record` on a stripped copy reproduces the current file.

Stating it because a diff is only as good as its left-hand side, and this
left-hand side was computed rather than kept.
