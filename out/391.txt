391 — the recovery prompt on MathPix rescue crops
=================================================

20 crops, one from each of 20 DIFFERENT documents (per document, not per
crop: 52 archives hold more than 100 rescues each and a uniform draw over
20,833 would take most of the sample from a handful of books). Crops under
15 KB skipped — a 2 KB strip is a rule or a page number.

Same prompt as 392, same fixed preamble, minimax-m3, max_tokens 16000.

THE RAW RESULT

  compiled              14
  empty reply            4      all finish_reason="length"
  did not compile        2

  compiled: d median 240, mean 1192, min 7, max 11630
  flags: component 13, noise 1
  WITHIN 392's NOISE BAND (d <= 7): 1 of 14

Against 392's ceiling — 11 of 13 substantial figures inside d <= 7 — that is
a collapse. And the reason is not the prompt or the model.

THE POPULATION IS NOT WHAT THE PROMPT ASSUMES

I looked at four of the crops. They are four different KINDS of thing, and
only one of them is a figure a TikZ prompt can answer:

  A LINE FIGURE. "Complex Analysis" — a lattice plot with labelled lambda
  and mu axes, 10 components. The prompt's own case. d = 7, the single row
  in the noise band, and the only one.

  A MATHEMATICAL DISPLAY. 2102.11507 — an exact sequence over a table of
  cohomology cases, 172 components. Not a picture at all. MathPix declined
  it because it is a complex aligned display, and the prompt answered a
  question nobody asked: it wants TikZ, and this needs align/array. d = 44,
  and the number is meaningless because the tool is wrong.

  A SHADED 3D RENDER. 2006.0218v1 — grey translucent tubes with arrows,
  661 crop components. Those components are antialiasing and JPEG noise on
  the shading, not structure. The model returned 4 components, i.e. gave up.
  d = 1972, essentially all of it the crop's own noise.

  A PAGE SCREENSHOT. 2409.01704v1 — a full-page image of an OCR demo showing
  three Chinese book pages and their transcription. 3,587 crop components =
  thousands of CJK glyphs. No TikZ reproduces this and none should try.
  d = 11630, the largest, and it measures nothing.

So the 20,833 of 390 are not 20,833 figures. They are 20,833 rectangles
MathPix declined, and it declined them for at least four different reasons.
Counting them as one population and sending them all one prompt is the
defect this run found, in itself.

THE COMPARISON IS ALSO BIASED, AND 392 IS WHY I CAN SAY SO

392 compared a render against a render — both clean vector output, same
engine and dpi. 391 compares a clean render against a JPEG crop of a printed
page. Antialiasing, halftones and JPEG ringing all become components:

  crop components across the sample: 10, 172, 172, 189, 195, 233, 263, 285,
                                     358, 643, 661, 676, 1187, 3587

The single-digit one is the line drawing. Everything above ~150 is carrying
noise the model has no way to reproduce and should not. So a large distance
here is not evidence of a bad recovery, exactly as 392 established for
dotted strokes — the same instrument error, one step larger.

WHAT CAN HONESTLY BE CLAIMED

  On the one crop in the sample that is the kind of thing the prompt is for,
  it scored d = 7 — inside the band 392 measured as this prompt's best.

  n = 1. That is the finding, not a rate. A rate over a population of four
  mixed kinds would be a number with no referent, and reporting "1 of 14
  matched" as a success rate would be exactly that.

FOUR EMPTY REPLIES, ALL "length", AT 16,000 TOKENS

392 had one, on its most complex row. Here there are four in twenty. Both
runs agree the empty reply is a BUDGET and not a refusal, and the budget
scales with the crop's complexity. These crops are more complex than DaTikZ
rows.

TWO COMPILE FAILURES

  pgfplots: "the input coordinate `0.6' has not been..." — a malformed
  coordinate in the reply.
  "Undefined control sequence" — a macro the fixed preamble does not load.

2 of 16 non-empty replies. 392 had 0 of 19 with the same preamble, so this
is the population again rather than a regression: these replies reach for
more.

WHAT TO DO BEFORE THIS BECOMES A REPORT

  1. CLASSIFY THE RESCUE POPULATION FIRST. Line figure / mathematical
     display / photograph or render / page image. Only the first is a TikZ
     recovery task; the second is a LaTeX-math task with a different prompt;
     the third and fourth are not recovery tasks at all and belong in a
     "cannot be recovered, keep the crop" class.
  2. Then measure the first class alone, where 392's ceiling applies.
  3. Do not publish a distance against a JPEG crop as a quality score. It
     carries the crop's noise, and on this sample the noise is the majority
     of it.

281 asked whether a recovery is right. 392 answered it where a reference
exists. This run says the prior question is which rescues are recoveries at
all, and that has not been asked yet.
