220 — WHY IT SHOULD NOT BE BUILT NOW
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The premise is that some rows the pipeline treats as mathematics are not
mathematics. Whether that is a REGION-TYPE error (MathPix boxed prose as an
equation) or a READING error (the region is right, the transcription is not)
decides what the detector would even look at — geometry and region metadata in
the first case, the LaTeX string in the second. Nothing measured so far
separates them. A detector built before that is settled would be tuned on
whichever class happened to dominate the sample, and out/186 is the standing
example of what that costs: a constant derived from one document that supplied
235 of 725 flagged rows, published, then retracted.

What would settle it: take the rows already in hand — the 1,840 structurally-
failing high-confidence rows from 184, or the 20 classified in 185 — and label
each as region-error or reading-error. That is a measurement, not a build, and
it is the prerequisite.
