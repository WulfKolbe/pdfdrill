READING IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   C and E are indistinguishable from each other here, as they were on the
   low band - the rule sentence neither helps nor costs.

   What the band comparison shows is where the effect lives. The prior
   helps where MathPix reports real doubt, and the help does not extend
   smoothly upward: by 0.1-0.5 it is already close to gone, and by 0.9 it
   is gone entirely. That is a narrower target than 'low confidence' - it
   is the bottom 0.7% of the corpus, not the bottom 4%.

   The 8 rows already inside the noise floor are part of why. On those
   there is no defect to fix, and out/119 showed why that is the normal
   case even above the floor: the disagreements are typographic, and a
   re-transcription cannot change how a delimiter is set.
