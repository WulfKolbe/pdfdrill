POPULATION - READ THIS BEFORE QUOTING THE 46%
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   20 arXiv papers, each with BOTH a cached author e-print and MathPix
   already run. Of those, 1,745 display equations carry both readings and
   parse to an SLT; also present and NOT in the denominator:
      803  equations with only one reading (no author match attached)
      162  pairs where one side does not parse to an SLT - counted, never
           guessed at

   THE PAIRING IS NOT INDEPENDENT OF THE ANSWER. `injectlatex` attaches an
   author equation to a MathPix one only when a normalised-string
   similarity reaches 0.55 (commands.py:8077). So every pair measured here
   already agreed enough to be matched, and the 803 unpaired equations are
   exactly where the two readings agreed LEAST. The 46.4% is therefore
   'given that MathPix and the author were matchable at all, they agree
   exactly 46% of the time' - an upper bound on agreement over all
   equations, not an estimate of it. Against the full 2,548 equations with
   any reading, the zero count is 810 (31.8%), which is the
   pessimistic bound. The true figure lies between the two.

   Trailing sentence punctuation is stripped from BOTH sides before
   comparison (025), so the 2,113-mark migration cannot show up here as a
   one-node difference on 40% of rows.

CORRECTION (2026-08-21) - THE 46.4% WAS INFLATED BY A BROKEN SIGNATURE
==============================================================================

   mathgold's parse_latex_slt collapses a \begin{aligned} (or array) block
   to ONE node labelled UNRESOLVED_aligned. Two unrelated multi-line
   equations therefore produce byte-identical signatures, and the edit
   distance returns 0 without ever comparing their contents.

   Worked example, 1408.0838_EQ0011: MathPix's latex (1,176 chars, five
   \sum) and the author's (1,328 chars, eight \sum) both reduce to that one
   node and scored a perfect match. MathPix had dropped three summations
   and a whole line - plainly visible in the crop against the render.

   RECOMPUTED with degenerate pairs excluded:
      comparable pairs        1328
      excluded as degenerate  417   (an aligned/array block on either side)
      distance ZERO           485   (36.5%)

      The headline moves 46.4% -> 36.5%. Ten points of the original
      figure were multi-line equations agreeing with themselves.

   THE POPULATION CAVEAT ABOVE STILL APPLIES ON TOP OF THIS: pairs exist
   only where injectlatex matched at similarity >= 0.55, so 36.5% remains
   an upper bound on agreement over all equations, not an estimate of it.

   Multi-line equations are now EXCLUDED rather than scored, because the
   SLT layer cannot compare them - not because they agree. Scoring them
   needs an SLT that descends into an aligned block, which mathgold does
   not do today. That is the honest state: 417 of 1,745 pairs in this
   corpus are currently unmeasurable by this metric.
