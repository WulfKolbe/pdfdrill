   The stacked form is worth naming because an earlier, looser pattern
   reported 26 of them and they were \underset/\overset around other symbols
   entirely - the pattern did not require S inside the nest. Corrected, the
   stacked count is zero: MathPix writes these as ordinary sub/superscripts,
   never as stacked limits.

   For scale on the same four books: \sum appears in 479 expressions. So the
   OCR is perfectly willing to emit a summation operator, and does, 479
   times. It reads this symbol as a lettered S with scripts instead - which
   is the correct literal reading of the ink and exactly why the meaning has
   to come from a notation table rather than from the OCR.
