IS ONE PROBE PER DOCUMENT ENOUGH?

It is a proxy and it was checked rather than assumed. Five crops spread
across the model — first, quarter, half, three-quarter, last — on four
documents chosen at random:

  lyche-numerical-linear-algebra   1071 crops   200 200 200 200 200
  1605.05775                         40 crops   200 200 200 200 200
  johnston-linear-matrix-algebra   1853 crops   200 200 200 200 200
  bradley_spring22                   26 crops   200 200 200 200 200

And the dead document fails the same way, wholesale:

  230209-algebraic_similarity       107 crops   500 500 500

So expiry is per DOCUMENT, not per crop: a pdf_id is either served or it is
not. One probe per document is therefore a sound proxy, and 22 of 22 is a
real all-clear rather than a lucky sample.

WHAT THIS CHANGES

The urgency in the task does not survive the measurement. No published
report's evidence has expired, so no republishing is needed today.

What DOES stand is that the failure is possible, it is silent, and it is
total when it happens — 107 of 107 in the one case seen. The mitigation
built for 230209 (`pyramid` + `imageserve` + PDFDRILL_CDN_BASE, out/396)
now exists and is proven on that document, so if a published report's
pdf_id does expire the scan column is rebuildable from the PDF at 600 dpi
without a key and without MathPix.

That is worth more than the all-clear: the all-clear is a fact about today.
