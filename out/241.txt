THE FALSE POSITIVE, AND IT IS MINE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A row demoted to \emph{(not rendered)} has no rendered mathematics. Its
Rendered cell holds literal words whose ink is a constant 13 components, and it
is compared against a full scan cell. The ratio is legitimately enormous — the
render is correctly ABSENT, not disagreeing.

2010.14265 is 62.5% demoted and reads 8.62 over all rows, 1.00 over rendered
ones. OMDoc 1.2 is 50% demoted: 9.08 and 1.08. Every other document is at or
below 1.9% demoted and reads identically either way.

I derived 3.0 from five clean documents (p90 1.03 to 1.07) against 0902.0431
(7.76), and none of those six had many demoted rows. The failure mode was
outside the population I fitted on, and I sent the threshold anyway with "I
would not defend it past one significant figure" — which covered the number and
not the shape. The shape is what was wrong.

THE FIX: p90 OVER RENDERED ROWS ONLY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
report_tex.component_ratio_p90(rows, rendered) excludes rows that rendered
nothing, and demoted_flags(tex_body) reads which those are, in table order.
publish_ready now runs the credibility check itself — it was the one gate layer
I had recommended to someone else and did not run.

Note who can apply it: excluding demoted rows needs report.tex, which is in the
library and not in what gets published. So on the published side the correction
is not directly available. Either the ink.json carries a per-row rendered flag,
or the published gate keeps 3.0 and accepts that documents with a high
demotion fraction need an exception. That is the pages side's call and I have
told them which two documents it costs.
