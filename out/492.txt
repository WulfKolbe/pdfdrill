**`level >= 2` is the constraint that empties it.** 330 rows survive the
other three. Adding the section-depth requirement takes it to zero.

AND THE REASON IS STRUCTURAL, NOT A SHORTAGE
---------------------------------------------
Two numbers explain it and they compound:

  the region join works for 11.3% of rows. `parent_section` lives on the
  MODEL object; a projection row reaches it only by matching its region
  5-tuple. Most rows are inline formulas, which have no region of their own —
  the same fact 488 recorded when it found FO rows carrying no page and no
  confidence, and the same one that will decide whether 498's junction check
  can see 0707.4470_FO0175 at all.

  of the rows that DO join, level >= 2 holds for 378 of 13,248 — 2.9%. A
  paper's section tree as this model represents it is flat: the parent is the
  document's own title.

Multiply the two and the level>=2 population is 0.32% of all rows, and its
intersection with the 0.5% that fail is empty. It is not that failing rows
avoid deep sections; it is that almost nothing is in a deep section.

WHAT THIS DECIDES
-----------------
487 as written cannot be tested on papers, and the reason is not the test
bed — it is that the context does not exist to add.

  the abstract          available, 81% of rows, 442 of 491 documents
  the enclosing section NOT available. 0.32% of rows corpus-wide, and zero
                        of the rows that need help
  the k nearest         available by flow_index (488: 100%), and by page for
                        18.6% of rows

So of the three context items 487 wanted, one is broadly available, one is
partly available, and one is effectively absent for papers. Combined with
488's finding that johnston's parent_section points at level 1 — whose
caption is the book's title — the section path is unavailable for BOTH
populations, for two different reasons.

493/494 therefore stop being an enhancement. If a section path is to exist at
all it has to come from the TOC, and for papers it may have to come from
somewhere else again or be dropped. That is 493's question and it is now the
load-bearing one.

487 should not run until it is answered. Running it now would compare a
prompt carrying an abstract and some neighbours against a prompt carrying an
abstract and some neighbours, with the section line omitted in both — which
is a test of the neighbour list, not of the change that was asked for.
