   the author writes \mathrm{d}, three times, and the two render identically.
   That invited the conclusion that the distance is mostly typographic, so I
   measured it: neutralising every spacing-only token (\mathrm{~x}, '\text {',
   \quad, \, \; \!) collapses only 12 of the 935 to distance 0 - 1.3%.
   The zero rate moves 46.4% -> 47.1%. So the eye-catching first case is
   ATYPICAL: 923 of 935 differences survive spacing normalisation and are
   substantive (median 5, p90 27).

   against them rather than case by case:
       55  ( 5.9%)  MathPix wraps the equation in \begin{aligned}, the
