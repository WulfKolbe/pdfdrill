052 - every SLT-zero row in the corpus, unconditioned on ink distance
================================================================================

FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   out/052-slt-zero.tsv
   columns: identifier, slt, ink, demoted
     identifier  <bibkey>_EQnnnn, the projector's own numbering
     slt         SLT edit distance, MathPix's LaTeX vs the AUTHOR'S - 0 on
                 every row here, by selection
     ink         L1 over inkdrill's five-tuple between the STANDALONE render
                 of that equation (400 dpi) and its MathPix scan crop
     demoted     yes if the report renders that row as '(not rendered)'.
                 LABELLED, not dropped, so the exclusion can be checked

POPULATION - WHY 42 DOCUMENTS AND NOT 49
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The corpus is 49 documents. An SLT distance needs the AUTHOR'S LaTeX,
   so `injectlatex` was run over all of them first, and:
      48  resolved to a genuine author e-print (arxiv .tgz)
       1  1012.3259 resolved to the MathPix .tex.zip instead - MathPix's own
          reconstruction, so its 'author' side would BE MathPix and every
          row would be a manufactured SLT-zero. EXCLUDED, as in out/040.
      42  of the 48 actually contribute rows; the other 6 matched no
          equations at the 0.55 similarity threshold, so they have no
          SLT-zero rows to emit rather than being dropped for cause.

WHAT THE ROWS SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Content is identical by construction on every row, so each ink distance
   is the metric's own noise for render-vs-scan, never a disagreement.

      min 0   p50 2   p75 6   p90 14   p95 22   p99 43   max 243   mean 5.7
      above  6 :  364  (24.5%)
      above 22 :   69  (4.6%)

   p95 = 22, UNCHANGED from the 808-row sample in out/051.txt even though
   the population nearly doubled (808 -> 1,488) and 22 new documents joined.
   An estimate that does not move when the sample doubles is worth more than
   the same estimate from one pass, so the floor of 22 now rests on
   stability rather than on a single measurement.

   The median stays at 2 and the tail lengthens (max 243 against 75
   before): the body is tight, and a handful of equations disagree with
   their own scan far beyond anything a rasterizer can explain. Those are
   worth looking at individually - they are the rows where content agrees
   with the author and the printed page still does not match.

   Largest contributors: 0902.0431 (243), 1205.5935v1 (207), 1411.5002 (129), 0806.1585 (124), 1510.06699 (102).
