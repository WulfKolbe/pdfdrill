531 — where the KaTeX warning lands
===================================

THE TEXT, ONE PLACE
-------------------
`src/docops/katex_notice.py` — `KATEX_WARNING_HTML` (a banner) and
`KATEX_WARNING_TEXT` (the same sentence in plain text).

  KaTeX renders without a preamble: it cannot use \usepackage or this
  document's own macros. \bm alone occurs 327 times across the corpus,
  \Perp is defined by no package and spans 10 documents, and 11,088 of
  11,624 undefined occurrences are the source document's own macros.
  A row that looks wrong here may be correct, and one that looks right
  here may not compile. Judge from the LaTeX report.

Every number in it is measured and sourced: 327 and 11,088 from out/482,
the 10 documents from out/484. A test asserts all three appear in the
string, so it cannot decay into a vague caution.

WHERE IT LANDS — FIVE GENERATORS, NOT THREE
--------------------------------------------
The search for `katex` in the tree returns five emitters, and the warning
is wired into all five:

  src/docops/projectors/formula_report.py    formula-report.html
  src/docops/projectors/comparison_html.py   compare.html
  src/docops/projectors/distill_reader.py    the distill reader
  src/pdfdrill/docinspect.py                 <bibkey>.inspect.html
  tools/corrections439.py                    corrections.html

Two of those are ones 523 said would SURVIVE consolidation —
corrections.html (corpus-wide, so B cannot answer it) and inspect.html
(regions that never became rows). They are the ones that most need the
warning, because they are the HTML that is not going away.

Counted in the published set: 391 katex references in
formula-report.html, 44 in corrections.html, 23 in compare.html, 7 in
inspect.html. index.html has none and gets no banner.

VERIFIED, NOT ASSUMED
---------------------
Rebuilt 2010.14265's compare.html through the CLI: the banner is present
in the emitted file. 2,922 tests pass.

A DEFECT I INTRODUCED AND FIXED
--------------------------------
`docinspect._TEMPLATE` is a RAW string, `r"""..."""`. My first splice closed
it and reopened it non-raw, so every backslash in the following ~1,000
lines of JavaScript became a Python escape — `/\s+/` and friends. 35 tests
failed with a JS syntax error at line 1190. Reopening with `r"""` fixed it.
The lesson is small and exact: when splicing into a triple-quoted string,
reopen it with the same prefix it had.

WHAT THE WARNING DOES NOT DO
-----------------------------
It does not make the KaTeX column correct. It says the column is not
evidence, and names where the evidence is. The only fix that makes an HTML
rendering trustworthy is a preamble it cannot have — which is why B is
LaTeX.
