WHY THE CORPUS HAS NO SUBSUBSECTIONS
--------------------------------------
Because route A is not reading sections. johnston's 17 are

  Introduction · Definition 1.1.3 · Example 1.4.10 · Remark 1.4.2 ·
  Theorem 1.A.1 · Remark 2.1.1 · Definition 2.D.1 · 2.D.4 …

Those are THEOREM ENVIRONMENTS. A 500-page linear algebra textbook with
chapters and numbered sections contributes nine headings, and not one of
them is a chapter or a section. gilmore gives 4 from 218 headers; cardona
24 from 259; lyche 64 from 292.

"226 at level 1, 88 at level 2, nothing deeper" is therefore not a fact
about textbooks. It is the shape of a detector that finds bold display
lines in a scan and calls the ones with children a section.

Route B, on the one document where it runs, returns real sectioning
commands with real names. It is the better route and it is nearly unused.
