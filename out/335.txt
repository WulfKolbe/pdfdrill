THE TRIPLE IS NOT YET A GOLD SET, AND THE MISSING LEG IS THE SAME ONE

  A triple needs the base file, the annotation, and the crop OF THAT
  FIGURE. The first two are in hand for 221 inclusions. The third is not:
  the crops exist as files, but which crop shows which inclusion is the
  join 301 measured at 0 matched of 8 known pairs. Counting a document's
  crops is not resolving one.

  So this census reports what it can establish -- 221 annotated figures
  whose base file is on disk, in documents that hold crops -- and stops
  short of calling them triples, because the pairing that would make them
  triples is the thing that does not work yet.

  336 diffs a base against a crop. For that it needs ONE pair it is sure
  of, not 221 it is not. 2004.05631v1's tex.zip sits inside texsrc/ (331),
  so for that document the crop's page and rectangle are stated in the
  filename, and a pair can be established by page and shape rather than by
  order. That is the narrow route worth taking before the wide one.

out/335.json carries every inclusion with its siblings and base path.
